# Asset-reuse visual-direction amendment

Status: **PROPOSED / NOT ACCEPTED**  
Mode: `PROCESS_ONLY / DOCS_ONLY`  
Date: 2026-09-28  
Amends: `Docs/art/VISUAL_BIBLE.md`, `Docs/art/SETTING.md`, `WP-ART-01`, `WP-ART-ENV-02`, `WP-ART-CHAR-01`, `WP-ART-URBAN-01` and `WP-ART-03` visual-selection semantics.

## Owner decision

Juego2 keeps a strong fictional northern-Spain port-town identity, but **drops per-asset geographic realism as a selection gate**.

The project must prefer **maximum practical reuse of already-owned/admitted source assets** when their function, proportions or component parts can be made coherent with Juego2 through bounded adaptation. Visual identity is judged at the level of the composed street, building, character group and neighbourhood, not by asking whether every upstream mesh could have been photographed unchanged in northern Spain.

The target remains stylized low-poly / late-PS2-to-early-PS3 in spirit, not photorealism. The goal is a coherent, recognizably northern-Spain port town built efficiently from the available asset ecosystem rather than a geographically literal reconstruction that discards most of that ecosystem.

## Core rule — reuse before rejection

For an admitted source asset, use this order:

1. **DIRECT / A** — usable essentially as-is.
2. **ADAPTABLE / B** — keep the asset as the basis and alter bounded materials, palette, selected parts, proportions, props, signage, roof/opening treatment or surrounding composition.
3. **DONOR / C** — the whole prefab is not keeper-ready, but useful components may be extracted/recombined: walls, beams, roofs, windows, doors, stairs, chimneys, balconies, furniture, trim, props, clothing parts, etc.
4. **REJECT / D** — the core silhouette, function, theme or adaptation cost remains incompatible after reasonable reuse analysis.
5. **BLOCKED_EXTERNAL** — a required role remains genuinely uncovered after DIRECT/ADAPTABLE/DONOR reuse and lawful derivative creation have been considered.

`DONOR` is a first-class production outcome. Source prefabs are not required to remain intact. Component reuse must retain upstream/derivative provenance.

A category-level rule such as “wooden buildings are out”, “medieval pack assets are out” or “fantasy-origin clothing is out” is invalid unless a later reviewed amendment justifies that exact category ban.

## Wood and medieval/fantasy-origin assets

**Wood is not vetoed.** Timber may appear in facades, balconies, galleries, beams, workshops, port structures, service buildings, interiors, stairs, porches, sheds, shopfronts and mixed stone/render/timber assemblies.

Likewise, an asset coming from a medieval/fantasy-labelled pack is not automatically rejected. The relevant question is what it reads as **after bounded adaptation and composition**.

The project still rejects a final keeper scene whose aggregate read is dominated by clearly incompatible fantasy/alpine/medieval signals such as castle/fortification language, exaggerated chalet massing, pervasive decorative half-timbering, thatch as a town-wide language, fantasy signage/weapons or other unmistakable genre markers. Those signals may be removed, diluted, recombined or used only as donor components.

The test is therefore:

> **Does the composed result read as Juego2's northern-Spain port town?**

not:

> **Was every source asset originally authored for northern Spain?**

## Where final identity comes from

Final identity should be carried by a combination of:

- dense human-scale urban composition and irregular frontage rhythm;
- coherent material/palette treatment;
- damp northern light and weather language;
- stone/render/timber balance chosen per street rather than by blanket bans;
- port/work/commercial ground treatment;
- signage, shopfronts, street furniture and utility details;
- vegetation and terrain appropriate to the setting;
- selected hero landmarks and bespoke/high-value derivatives;
- character styling and role context;
- day/evening/night presentation;
- repeated local motifs that make reused upstream assets feel like one authored world.

These identity layers are allowed to do more work so the base mesh library does not have to carry geographic specificity alone.

## Adaptation-cost rule

Reuse is preferred, not mandatory at any cost.

An asset should move to `REJECT / D` when making it belong would require effectively rebuilding most of the asset, when its core silhouette/function fights the target, or when a cleaner admitted alternative/derived piece is cheaper and more reusable.

Workers must record the causal reason for rejection. “Not realistic enough for northern Spain” is not sufficient by itself.

## ART-01 recovery pass

`WP-ART-01` must **not** close under the previous restrictive triage without a bounded recovery pass.

Before ART-01 final candidate/PASS, re-examine a representative sample of assets previously rejected or deprioritized primarily because of:

- amount of visible wood;
- medieval/fantasy source-pack label;
- lack of direct Cantabrian/northern-Spain specificity;
- material/palette mismatch that could plausibly be corrected;
- prefab-level mismatch despite potentially useful component parts.

The recovery evidence must include at least:

1. a list/sample of prior rejects with the original reason;
2. reclassification through `DIRECT / ADAPTABLE / DONOR / REJECT`;
3. at least one **environment** recovery demonstration showing source → bounded adaptation/component reuse → third-person composition;
4. if relevant admitted wardrobe material exists, at least one **character/wardrobe** recovery demonstration or a documented handoff to `ART-CHAR-01`;
5. explicit evidence that wood itself is not being used as an automatic rejection criterion;
6. unresolved roles that still justify `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` only after recovery/reuse has been attempted.

There is **no recovery-percentage quota**. The purpose is to prove that useful source material is no longer being discarded by an over-narrow visual interpretation.

Existing ART-01 structural work, dimensional grammar, paintovers, H2F sequencing and proxy/keeper rules remain valid. This amendment does not restart ART-01 or invalidate its completed `PREFOUNDATION_INPUT`; it changes how source breadth is triaged and what the final visual target is allowed to reuse.

## Workpack mapping

For `WP-ART-01`, existing dispositions map as follows:

- `KEEP_AS_IS` = `DIRECT / A`;
- `ADAPT` = `ADAPTABLE / B`;
- add `DONOR_COMPONENTS` = `DONOR / C`;
- `REJECT_FOR_KEEPER` = `REJECT / D`;
- `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` remains unchanged.

`CREATE_DERIVED` remains valid when a new derivative is cheaper/better than adapting a whole source asset. A donor component may become part of a `CREATE_DERIVED` Juego2 assembly while retaining lineage.

For `WP-ART-ENV-02` and `WP-ART-URBAN-01`, the scenario factory and port-town kit must preserve these dispositions so future streets can reuse components rather than requiring bespoke whole-prefab matches.

For `WP-ART-CHAR-01`, fantasy-labelled wardrobe pieces may be adapted or used as donors when silhouette/components are useful. Genre-specific armour, weapons or unmistakable fantasy ornament may still be rejected or removed; the pack label alone is not the rejection oracle.

For `WP-ART-03`, the final look lock judges **coherence of the integrated result**. It must not retroactively require every reused source mesh to satisfy literal regional realism in isolation.

## Purchase/acquisition consequence

Do not buy or adopt a new environment/wardrobe pack merely because the current source was filtered by the previous stricter realism interpretation.

A new paid or external source is justified only when a material production role remains blocked after:

- DIRECT reuse;
- bounded ADAPTABLE treatment;
- DONOR/component recombination;
- reasonable Juego2 derivative creation from admitted sources.

Existing dependency/IP policy and owner cost thresholds remain unchanged.

## Non-changes

This amendment does **not**:

- turn Juego2 into fantasy, medieval or alpine fiction;
- change the accepted large northern-Spain port-town setting, five-zone model or nightlife distribution;
- weaken `KEEPER_READY / PROXY_VISUAL / COVERAGE_BLOCKED` truthfulness;
- permit visible dressed-greybox output as keeper art;
- remove assembly, dimensional, provenance or H2F requirements;
- require every source asset to be salvaged;
- authorize unlicensed/unadmitted assets;
- alter CITY topology or gameplay semantics.

## Acceptance test

PASS if a reviewer can confirm that:

- northern-Spain identity remains strong at composed-scene level;
- strict per-asset geographic realism is no longer a gate;
- wood and pack labels are not automatic rejection reasons;
- donor/component reuse is explicitly lawful and traceable;
- ART-01 has a bounded recovery obligation before PASS without restarting prior valid work;
- downstream environment/character/final-look work consumes the same reuse-first rule;
- external acquisition happens only after reasonable reuse paths are exhausted.

FAIL if the amendment effectively accepts any fantasy asset unchanged regardless of scene read, removes visual coherence/provenance requirements, or silently converts reuse preference into a mandate to spend more effort adapting a bad asset than using a better lawful solution.
