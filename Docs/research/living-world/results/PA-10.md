## 0. Question and recommendation

PA-10 asks:

> How can legitimate actor/player causes become multi-step autonomous events that remain bounded, reconstructible and capable of ending, without a hidden storyteller choosing the story on behalf of the actors?

**Recommendation:** adopt a **bounded causal-chain coordinator** whose authority begins only after a legitimate cause exists. It may preserve lineage, expose bounded consequence opportunities, recruit candidates through local/contextual relevance, enforce propagation/termination constraints and coordinate collision policy. It may **not** choose an actor's meaningful action/target and then claim the actor originated the event.

```text
legitimate action / material change / activity outcome
        ↓
chain root with causal provenance
        ↓
bounded consequence opportunity
        ↓
relevant actor/world owner encounters it
        ↓
PA-02 actor chooses / owner applies legitimate delta
        ↓
next bounded consequence, de-escalation or stable state
        ↓
TERMINATE at resolution or changed equilibrium
```

A chain is therefore neither a screenplay nor an infinite event graph. It is a compact causal envelope around already-owned decisions and consequences.

## 2. Chain semantic responsibilities

These are conceptual responsibilities, **not frozen runtime fields/classes**.

A useful chain needs only enough semantic identity to answer:

- what legitimate state/action/outcome started it;
- what material/social/institutional condition is currently unresolved or transformed;
- what bounded actors/places/resources are currently relevant and why;
- which already-owned decision/delta produced each accepted next step;
- whether further propagation is still allowed;
- whether the chain ended, de-escalated or reached a stable changed state;
- what observable/provenance outputs remain available for PA-11.

A debug representation may therefore contain a root cause, parent/child lineage, involved owners/participants, current frontier, recruitment reason, salience/scale hint, propagation budget and terminal reason. The exact persistence schema is deferred.

## 3. Actor action versus chain coordination

### Actor owns

- whether to act when an opportunity/conflict is encountered;
- meaningful target/response choice;
- refusal, substitution or de-escalating response where applicable.

### Domain owner owns

- material/service/activity/rule truth and legitimate state transitions.

### PA-10 chain layer may own

- causal grouping/lineage;
- whether a consequence is eligible to continue this chain;
- bounded candidate/relevance envelope;
- duplicate/cooldown/salience pressure;
- termination/stable-state bookkeeping;
- explicit `BLOCK / DEFER / SUBSTITUTE / REPLAN` coordination at authored-story collisions, without stealing underlying authority.

### Forbidden storyteller form

```text
manager decides:
  Carmen argues with Antonio
  Manolo intervenes
  Antonio storms out
  Carmen apologises tomorrow

then assigns those roles/actions to actors
```

Even if presented as emergent, that is authored/random role assignment, not actor-owned causality.

## 4. Bounded recruitment

Recruitment is a **candidate discovery** problem, not a global cast search.

Valid narrowing reasons include:

- direct participants/targets in the prior causal step;
- current place/proximity or shared opportunity;
- a relationship/obligation/role already allowed by PA-03;
- legitimate knowledge of the situation through PA-04/05;
- service/activity involvement from PA-07/08;
- ownership/authority over the affected resource/rule;
- a bounded explicitly authored story role when GameFlow lawfully exposes it.

Adding 1,000 irrelevant actors must not semantically alter the candidate set. A future runtime may use indices appropriate to those owners; PA-10 freezes only the locality/relevance rule, not the data structure.

## 5. Termination, de-escalation and normality

Every chain requires at least one explicit path to a terminal or stable state.

Valid termination reasons include:

- the root problem is resolved/repaired/fulfilled;
- a participant refuses and no legitimate next opportunity remains;
- the next consequence has no eligible bounded participant/owner;
- the causal/scale budget is exhausted;
- the material/social state reaches a stable changed equilibrium;
- a chain is superseded/merged by a more direct current state where no duplicate propagation is useful;
- an authored reservation explicitly defers or substitutes participation and later re-evaluation ends with no remaining conflict.

Termination **does not mean reset**. A bar may stay closed, a relationship may remain changed, a resource may remain moved, or a policy aftermath may persist after the chain itself stops generating new equivalent consequences.

De-escalation must have a causal reason. A changed relationship, available substitute resource, apology/restitution, successful repair, actor refusal, or changed opportunity may shorten/soften the chain. “Randomly rolled calm” may add flavour but cannot be the sole explanation for a material branch used to satisfy the acceptance oracle.

## 6. Canonical NPC-originated 3+ step fixture

Detailed cross-unit evidence lives in `Docs/evidence/WP-PA-B2/BATCH_CAUSAL_FIXTURES.md`.

Compact form:

1. Bar F01 has depleted `bar-service-stock`, degrading an accepted PA-07 service opportunity.
2. Carmen legitimately encounters the shortage and **PA-02 chooses** to request a bounded replacement from Manolo, who has a relevant role/opportunity; this actor choice is the chain root.
3. Manolo receives the request through ordinary information flow and **chooses** to help. A bounded stock transfer/restoration action changes shared material state.
4. The transfer reduces one compatible opportunity at Manolo's side while restoring the bar service condition.
5. Antonio later encounters one of those changed opportunities and **chooses** wait/substitute/participate according to his own state.
6. The chain ends when the replacement transfer is settled and no unresolved service/restitution/recruitment condition remains.

This is `>=3` causally linked material/decision deltas. PA-10 coordinates lineage and bounded continuation; it does not command Carmen, Manolo or Antonio.

## 7. Player-originated chain using the same lifecycle

The accepted B1 player fixture already supplies a legitimate root:

```text
player validly returns missing Bar F01 service stock
 -> PA-07 shared material state changes
 -> Carmen later encounters restored service and chooses normal service action
 -> Antonio later encounters the activity/service opportunity and may choose cards
 -> Manolo owns accept/refuse if invited
```

PA-10 uses exactly the same chain semantics: root cause, bounded continuation, owned decisions, terminal/stable state, traceable provenance. No `PlayerChain` or quest-only event lifecycle is required.

## 8. Authored-story collision

The accepted B1 A11B-10 rule is inherited directly.

If an autonomous chain wants Carmen while GameFlow has an exclusive bounded reservation on Carmen/Bar F01 for an authored beat, the coordinator must select an explicit lawful policy:

- `BLOCK` the incompatible continuation;
- `DEFER` until reservation release;
- `SUBSTITUTE` a semantically eligible participant/resource if one exists;
- `REPLAN` from current state.

The original causal root and already-produced material state remain intact. GameFlow owns only its bounded reservation; it may not rewrite “the shortage never happened” or permanently strand actor authority.

## 9. De-escalation counterfactual

Two runs are identical except one legitimate causal input:

- Run A: Antonio has an unresolved obligation to Manolo and learns Manolo personally absorbed the replacement cost.
- Run B: that obligation is absent, with all other relevant state fixed.

When Antonio later encounters the degraded alternative opportunity, PA-02 may legitimately select `HELP_RESTORE / WAIT` in A and `USE_OTHER_OPTION` in B, shortening the first chain and terminating the second through a different stable state.

The required proof is not the exact action labels; it is that one owned causal input can produce a materially different de-escalation/termination path without a storyteller coin-flip being the explanation.

## 10. Outputs for PA-11 without omniscience

PA-10 must preserve enough **internal provenance** to distinguish root and accepted downstream deltas for debugging/reconstruction design, and domain actions may naturally create perceivable effects/traces.

It must **not** automatically create player-facing “clue objects” for every chain.

PA-11 receives:

- real changed world/material/rule state;
- which actors could actually perceive/participate;
- legitimate records/outcomes already emitted by domain owners where applicable;
- causal lineage for engine/debug validation only.

PA-11 then decides what evidence channels are legitimate. Debug lineage remains privileged and cannot itself become a quest marker.

## 11. Scale and concurrency

Micro/meso/macro labels are **optional descriptive hints**, not required engine types. What is required is a bounded propagation envelope so a minor cause cannot recruit the whole town by default.

A future PA-13/runtime consumer must budget:

- concurrently active chains;
- propagation/reconsideration frequency;
- retained chain/provenance history;
- salience thresholds/cooldowns;
- background simplification.

PA-10 fixes the semantic need for those limits, not numeric values.

## 12. Negative controls

### N10-1 — Random story assignment

FAIL if a generator decides a story/roles first and actor “choices” merely execute assigned parts.

### N10-2 — Infinite escalation

FAIL if each consequence automatically creates an equal-or-stronger successor with no causal exhaustion/stable-state path.

### N10-3 — Global recruitment

FAIL if irrelevant population size directly expands candidate search/semantics without owner/context relevance.

### N10-4 — Event monolith

FAIL if one opaque scripted event encapsulates all participants/actions/outcomes so normal owners cannot explain the steps.

### N10-5 — Event spam as depth

FAIL if many simultaneous chains repeatedly contend for the same actor/POI without salience/cooldown/deferral semantics.

### N10-6 — Invisible incident

FAIL if a materially important chain has no legitimate changed state/perception/trace surface that PA-11 could ever consume.

### N10-7 — False reset termination

FAIL if “termination” means silently restoring prior state or deleting already-caused consequences.

### N10-8 — Story causality theft

FAIL if GameFlow resolves a collision by rewriting causal history or permanently owning the actor after its bounded reservation ends.

## 15. Unit conclusion

The chain layer may remember, bound and coordinate causality. It may not manufacture agency.
