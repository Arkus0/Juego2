## 0. Question and recommendation

PA-13 asks:

> How can Juego2 keep a persistent Living World bounded, reproducible, explainable and semantically truthful as actors, events and off-screen simulation grow, while still allowing sustained coherent player/world causes to transform the town?

**Recommendation:** simulation control is a **cross-cutting execution envelope around accepted semantic owners**, not a hidden director and not a second copy of their state.

```text
accepted semantic owner produces / considers work
        ↓
scope + eligibility + explicit budget class
        ↓
execute now / defer / degrade fidelity / reject with reason
        ↓
owner-preserving state transition
        ↓
inspectable causal + budget trace
        ↓
reconciliation / retry / termination under explicit rules
```

The boundary is strict:

> PA-13 may control **how much, how often, in what order and at what fidelity** accepted capabilities execute. It may not redefine **what their state means**, manufacture actor decisions, erase accepted causes, or restore a designer-approved equilibrium by fiat.

Normal town life should be an attractor under ordinary noise because commitments, bounded propagation, finite persistence and causal recovery damp accidental churn. That attractor is **not protected destiny**. Repeated coherent interventions may exhaust or overcome stabilisers and leave a materially different equilibrium or crisis.

This is research/product guidance. It does not prove a runtime scheduler, choose ECS/Unity architecture, validate shipping NPC counts, freeze CPU milliseconds, select save-size limits or establish hardware targets.

## 2. Authority boundary — control without semantic theft

Simulation control may observe and regulate work requests emitted by accepted owners. It does not become their authority.

| Concern | Semantic owner | PA-13 may regulate | PA-13 must not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| expected routine / current-context re-evaluation | PA-01 | reevaluation cadence, defer policy, fidelity tier | rewrite expected intent to make overload disappear |
| actor action/target/response choice | PA-02 | candidate cap contract, reconsideration cadence, commitment/inertia envelope | choose the meaningful action/target and call it optimization |
| relationships / obligations | PA-03 | retention/query work budget | collapse typed state into a cheaper universal score |
| belief / knowledge | PA-04 | query/update frequency and persistence pressure | substitute canonical truth for missing belief |
| information transfer | PA-05 | relay/propagation opportunity budget and cooldown | broadcast, silently drop delivered truth, expose hidden lineage |
| selected memory | PA-06 | finite capacity/compaction work and pressure | erase active causal minimum or create unbounded overflow |
| work/material/service | PA-07 | local claim/capacity processing | invent service availability or invisible replacement |
| activity sessions | PA-08 | concurrent-session/participation processing | settle local game/session semantics incorrectly for cost |
| player world action | PA-09 | same execution envelope as equivalent NPC action | route player through privileged causal shortcuts |
| event chains | PA-10 | active-chain/propagation/reconsideration envelope | assign story roles/actions or reset world state at termination |
| traces/evidence | PA-11 | retention class/compaction pressure | expose debug lineage or delete a promised reconstruction window silently |
| governance | PA-12 | evaluation/propagation workload caused by rule changes | turn policy into direct citizen belief/action mutation |

**Control result vocabulary** must remain semantically distinguishable enough for tooling/tests to answer at least:

```text
EXECUTED
NOT_ELIGIBLE
DEFERRED
CAP_HIT
DEGRADED_FIDELITY
REJECTED_UNREPRESENTABLE
TERMINATED
```

Exact enum/API names are deferred. The product requirement is that a reviewer can distinguish those outcomes without inferring them from ambiguous human logs.

## 4. Budget dimensions — dimensions now, constants later

A single global FPS target cannot express why a Living World operation was delayed or simplified. PA-13 therefore recommends **separate semantic budget dimensions**.

These are required accounting dimensions; their numeric values remain empirical.

### B1 — initiative / reconsideration

Controls how often actors may originate/reconsider semantically equivalent work when inputs are materially unchanged.

Required behaviour:

- commitment persists until completion, invalidation or declared reconsideration cause;
- small/noisy score differences do not cause tick-to-tick flip-flop;
- retries use cooldown/backoff/event-driven wakeup or another bounded mechanism;
- starvation of deferred valid work is observable.

### B2 — candidate / interaction scope

Controls how much relevant state is enumerated before expensive eligibility/comparison.

Required behaviour:

- query declares its authorized source/scope;
- unrelated global population is not scanned merely to rediscover that source;
- local density cannot create an unbounded all-pairs surface;
- cap/partition/reservation behaviour is deterministic and inspectable;
- no eligible result may trigger an emergency global scan.

This dimension carries **SV-1**.

### B3 — propagation / concurrent causal work

Controls active chains, relay opportunities, reactions and equivalent downstream continuation.

Required behaviour:

- each continuation has a causal reason;
- repeated equivalent propagation can be cooled/deduplicated without changing actor belief by hidden lineage;
- chain/reaction pressure can defer/terminate according to explicit rules;
- a cap ending a chain does not erase state already caused;
- deliberate high-impact repeated causes may generate fresh legitimate work later; a cap is not a permanent morality filter.

### B4 — persistence / trace / memory

Controls retained selected memory, active causal lineage, evidence windows and compact summaries.

Required behaviour:

- routine repetition does not generate unbounded permanent state;
- distinct salient items still face finite pressure rather than an unbounded overflow exception;
- active effects retain a bounded minimum reason witness through an owner-safe representation;
- accepted reconstructibility windows cannot be silently destroyed by compaction;
- debug lineage and actor/player knowledge remain separate.

### B5 — fidelity / reconciliation

Controls FULL, ABSTRACT and any later intermediate representation.

Required behaviour:

- fidelity choice never changes ownership or semantic meaning by accident;
- omitted presentation/spatial micro-detail is allowed only when required causal state remains representable;
- promotion/re-entry is explicit and inspectable;
- unrepresentable material divergence causes truthful degradation/failure, not fabricated continuity;
- reconciliation work obeys **SV-2**.

### B6 — cost / overload envelope

Controls aggregate execution pressure after scope-specific budgets have been applied.

Required behaviour:

- overload has an explicit degradation order;
- semantic truth is preserved before quantity/presentation fidelity;
- control outcomes expose which budget caused defer/degrade/cap;
- no overload path falls back to hidden global scans;
- numeric CPU/memory/save/frame targets are not frozen until representative runtime profiling exists.

### B7 — reservation / fairness

Controls finite ownership of actors/resources/POIs between routine, systemic work and bounded GameFlow reservations.

Required behaviour:

- reservations identify owner/scope/lifecycle;
- conflicting work selects explicit block/defer/substitute/replan semantics;
- release is finite and inspectable;
- release does not replay every missed background micro-action;
- persistent starvation is diagnosable.

## 5. Anti-thrashing recommendation

PA-13 adopts the **requirement** for commitment/inertia/cooldown-like stability, not one algorithm.

A conforming future implementation must be able to distinguish:

```text
MATERIAL INPUT CHANGED
  -> reconsideration may be warranted

NO MATERIAL INPUT CHANGED
  -> semantically equivalent option ranking noise alone cannot force perpetual flip-flop
```

Candidate mechanisms include commitment windows, hysteresis, event-driven invalidation, cooldown/backoff, minimum meaningful-delta rules or bounded reevaluation cadence.

The choice remains runtime-owned because exact cadence and thresholds need playable profiling. The semantic oracle is stable: equivalent state cannot produce endless oscillation solely because the resolver is asked again.

## 6. Salience, event caps and degradation

### 6.1 Salience is routing pressure, not truth authority

Salience may help decide **what deserves scarce execution/persistence now**, but it cannot decide whether an accepted cause “really happened”.

Useful inputs may include:

- unresolved active commitment;
- direct participant stake;
- player-visible/reconstructible importance;
- material severity/irreversibility;
- novelty versus repeated equivalent work;
- currently active institutional/service dependency;
- explicit authored reservation/importance.

Player distance alone is insufficient because off-screen causal state may matter later.

### 6.2 Event/chain caps

A chain/propagation cap may:

- stop creating further equivalent continuation;
- defer a valid next opportunity;
- compact inactive lineage;
- require an owner to re-evaluate from current state later.

It may not:

- undo already-caused world state;
- convert false belief to truth;
- teleport an actor to the “expected” location;
- drop an active obligation without its owner;
- pretend a deferred event executed;
- stop all future deliberate transformation because one earlier budget was hit.

### 6.3 Degradation ladder

Exact tiers are future-owned, but degradation order must follow this semantic principle:

```text
preserve canonical/owned material state + identity + causal minimum
        before
preserve every optional background initiative
        before
preserve exact hidden spatial path / animation / presentation detail
```

A valid future overload policy might therefore reduce initiation frequency, defer low-salience optional reactions, compact history or use cheaper ABSTRACT representation **before** corrupting owned state.

Any cheaper representation that cannot preserve a required semantic fact must declare that inability and either keep higher fidelity for that case, defer transition, or fail/degrade explicitly.

## 7. Kill-switch / emergency-control rules

A runtime kill-switch is permitted only as a **truth-preserving containment tool**.

Allowed emergency actions include:

- stop admitting new optional autonomous initiatives;
- stop extending non-critical causal chains after their current owned step;
- force explicit defer of new background work;
- keep affected entities at a safer supported fidelity;
- preserve current owned state and bounded causal reason for later recovery/re-evaluation;
- surface a diagnostic reason/counter.

Forbidden emergency actions include:

- resetting relationships/beliefs/goals to baseline;
- deleting already-caused material consequences;
- teleporting actors to schedule positions and claiming continuity;
- clearing active obligations/reservations without their owner;
- marking skipped work as completed;
- converting debug truth into actor/player knowledge;
- applying a global “calm town” state that suppresses coherent transformation.

A kill-switch therefore reduces **new work**, not history.

## 8. FULL / ABSTRACT semantic continuity

FULL and ABSTRACT may differ in rendering, spatial resolution, update frequency and amount of optional micro-behaviour. They must share the semantic ownership contract.

On `FULL -> ABSTRACT -> FULL`, a qualifying future fixture must preserve/reconcile as applicable:

- actor identity;
- current expected routine context and actual material position/state at supported granularity;
- current commitment/goal and its owner-safe reason;
- relationship/obligation state;
- actor-accessible belief/provenance;
- selected memory causal minimum;
- relevant service/activity/material claims;
- accepted event-chain state/terminal status;
- institutional conditions;
- pending authored reservations where still active.

Not every hidden step needs to run. What must remain true is that the cheaper representation cannot later claim outcomes that have no legitimate summarized cause.

### SV-2 — executable future ownership

SV-2 becomes executable in layers:

1. **H3** — if H3 introduces the first actual actor/POI/routine FULL↔ABSTRACT transition, its acceptance must include an elapsed-time scaling negative control for the state it owns.
2. **H4** — mandatory Living World integration owner: repeat with beliefs/relationships/memory/events/material consequences and require reconciliation work to scale with bounded material summarized change / declared catch-up budget rather than omitted micro-ticks.
3. **later representative performance/gate owner (including H7 if that remains the canonical scale/profile phase)** — instrument real content/hardware and set shipping thresholds; do not replace H3/H4 semantic proof with a pure millisecond benchmark.

No exact scheduler or representation is selected here.

## 9. SV-1 — irrelevant-population independence and bounded local work

SV-1 is adopted unchanged in substance:

> For a decision/query class claimed to be local, known-actor, role-, place- or opportunity-scoped, unrelated population growth cannot make the path enumerate the global persistent population first, and local density cannot silently recreate an unbounded all-pairs candidate surface.

Future executable negative control:

```text
BASE
  relevant source/scope = R
  semantic inputs = fixed

STRESS
  add >=10,000 irrelevant distant persistent actors
  preserve R exactly

PASS
  semantic result unchanged
  relevant enumeration unchanged or under same deterministic declared cap
  expensive comparison unchanged or under same deterministic declared cap
  no irrelevant records inspected merely to rediscover R
  cap/degradation behaviour explicit and reproducible
```

Dense-local variant:

```text
hold semantic local opportunity constant
increase unrelated/co-located candidate density sharply
PASS only if expensive pair/candidate surface remains explicitly bounded
```

A spatial index with an unbounded bucket fails this oracle.

### SV-1 — executable future ownership

1. **H3** — first runnable owner for POI/service/activity/local schedule opportunity queries must prove scope-first lookup against irrelevant actor/POI decoys.
2. **H4** — mandatory Living World owner for autonomous target/action/event recruitment; must apply the same oracle to PA-02 and PA-10 style candidate surfaces, including dense-local stress.
3. **later representative performance/gate owner (including H7 if canonical at that time)** — replaces provisional synthetic operation counts with instrumented runtime counters and reviewed thresholds.

The owner may use any suitable data structure; the oracle binds the work shape, not the implementation.

## 10. Paired stability/transformation fixtures

Detailed B3 fixtures are in `Docs/evidence/WP-PA-B3/SIMULATION_CONTROL_FIXTURES.md`.

### F13-A — clumsy player / ordinary-noise recovery

From a normal town, one bounded low/medium mistake creates a real inconvenience. The player does not reinforce it.

PASS requires:

- consequence is not erased;
- propagation remains bounded;
- actors/services may recover through explicit normal owners;
- no unconditional reset timer is needed;
- town remains broadly functional over the bounded fixture horizon.

### F13-B — persistent destabiliser

From the same start, the player repeatedly applies accepted high-impact destabilising interventions after legible feedback.

PASS requires:

- each step uses normal owners;
- recovery capacity may be consumed/defeated;
- resulting service/routine/social/institutional state can settle into a materially changed equilibrium/crisis;
- simulation-control caps may limit concurrent work but cannot restore the default town by fiat.

### F13-C — constructive transformer

From the same start, repeated accepted constructive interventions improve/reorganise services/activities/cooperation.

PASS requires a materially changed positive equilibrium through the same owners, proving simulation control is not a destruction-only or morality filter.

The three fixtures are paired deliberately. A design that stabilizes F13-A but forces F13-B/C back to baseline is over-damped. A design that permits F13-B but lets F13-A self-amplify to collapse is unstable.

## 11. Authored reservation / systemic collision

PA-13 preserves the accepted B1/B2 rule without taking GameFlow authority.

When a valid background/systemic operation conflicts with a bounded authored reservation, the future coordinator chooses one explicit policy:

- `BLOCK`;
- `DEFER`;
- `SUBSTITUTE` with a semantically eligible alternative;
- `REPLAN` from current state.

Requirements:

- the original causal root/history stays intact;
- already-produced material/social state remains owned and cannot be rewritten for story convenience;
- reservation scope/lifetime is finite;
- release returns authority cleanly;
- release does not trigger replay of every missed micro-action;
- any catch-up/re-evaluation still obeys SV-2 and current-context semantics.

## 12. Determinism and inspectability

PA-13 carries forward the donor requirement that semantic reproduction is a QA contract.

Future runtime proof should normalize around **domain semantic outputs**, not incidental presentation ordering.

Given the same accepted snapshot, seed and ordered semantic inputs, a reviewer must be able to compare:

- actor decisions/action families/targets where deterministic by contract;
- domain deltas;
- chain roots/accepted continuations/terminal reasons;
- budget decisions and cap hits;
- fidelity transitions/reconciliation outcomes;
- persisted causal minimums.

If iteration order or timing can change a semantic winner under contention, that ordering must itself be explicitly deterministic/seeded or otherwise declared as part of the accepted input. Hidden container order is not authority.

## 14. Negative controls

PA-13 fails if a future consumer relies on any of these patterns to claim conformance:

1. **Budget mind control** — overload directly rewrites actor beliefs, relationships, goals or choices to reduce work.
2. **Rubber town** — sustained accepted transformation is damped until the initial town always returns.
3. **Butterfly apocalypse** — isolated low/medium noise automatically becomes self-sustaining collapse without reinforcing causes.
4. **Global-scan fallback** — bounded lookup failure falls back to `AllActors` / whole-world enumeration.
5. **Spatial-index theatre** — a spatial index is called bounded while one bucket can yield unbounded expensive all-pairs work.
6. **Tick-debt ABSTRACT** — cheaper mode accumulates every skipped micro-tick for replay on promotion.
7. **Silent budget drop** — pending/causal work disappears with no explicit terminal/defer/degrade outcome.
8. **Magic teleport/fabrication** — fidelity reduction invents location/completion/service state.
9. **Truth substitution** — missing actor belief is replaced with canonical/debug truth because cheaper.
10. **Trace amnesia** — compaction destroys an accepted active reason or reconstructibility window without explicit contract.
11. **Unbounded exception list** — “important” state bypasses finite persistence budgets into another append-only collection.
12. **Story reset** — GameFlow collision rewrites causal history or permanently steals actor authority.
13. **Non-reproducible contention** — hash/container/timing order silently changes semantic winner with equivalent inputs.
14. **Guessed validated budget** — any numeric shipping threshold is presented as proven by this research or the synthetic viability spike.
15. **H2 proxy overclaim** — H2 idle/walk/bark presentation proxies are cited as Living World simulation-control proof.

## 17. Deferred proof and reopen points

PA-13 is reopened if representative runtime evidence shows that one of these semantic requirements is internally incompatible, cannot be bounded without changing accepted meaning, or requires a materially different ownership contract.

It is **not** reopened merely because:

- a different data structure/scheduler is faster;
- tuning constants change;
- one hardware target changes;
- a content author wants more background activity;
- H2 visual NPC proxies are simpler than the eventual Living World.

Empirical proof remains outstanding for real CPU/save budgets, population/density limits, update cadence, fidelity thresholds, player readability of degradation, long-horizon fun and target-hardware scale.

## 18. Unit conclusion

PA-13's control philosophy is:

> **Bound work before it explodes; preserve owners before fidelity; expose every cut; let ordinary noise settle; let sustained causes matter.**

The result closes the remote PA research spine without pretending the runtime exists. SV-1 and SV-2 remain executable future gates, not prose aspirations, and no simulation-control mechanism gains authority to protect the starting town from coherent player causality.
