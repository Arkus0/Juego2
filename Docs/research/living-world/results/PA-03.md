PA-03 answers:

The result is research/product guidance for future consumers. It does **not** implement a graph, select a database/index, freeze serialization, tune social values, choose a utility formula, define UI, prove scale, solve persistence or make H4 complete.

The central acceptance invariant is behavioural and counterfactual:

```text
same actor
same external world
same actor-owned goal/problem
same eligible opportunity surface
same seed / deterministic tie conditions
same non-relationship inputs
        +
exactly one declared material directed relationship input differs
        ↓
an explainable action, target, eligibility, willingness or response may differ
```

Changing only `A -> B` must not silently imply or mutate the same change in `B -> A`.

## 2. Canonical Juego2 finding

A useful social graph is **typed causal relationship state available to actor-owned decisions**, not a friendship meter and not a dialogue decoration.

Three semantic layers are sufficient as the current minimum conceptual vocabulary:

```text
A. Directed stance
   A -> B
   - trust
   - affinity
   - fear

B. Structural ties / endpoint roles
   relation between A and B
   - household / family
   - employment
   - authority / status-bearing role
   - other explicitly justified paired or reciprocal role

C. Identified obligations
   debtor / creditor or obligated / beneficiary endpoints
   - favour / debt / duty / promise-like obligation family
   - source / reason reference
   - current lifecycle state
```

These layers must remain semantically distinguishable because they answer different questions:

- `affinity`: how positively/negatively A is disposed toward B in a relevant social choice;
- `trust`: how willing A is to rely on B where reliability/confidence matters;
- `fear`: how much A is deterred/coerced/avoidant toward B where threat matters;
- structural role: what relationship or institutional role exists independently of liking, trusting or fearing;
- obligation: what bounded, sourced commitment is currently owed and can be fulfilled, consumed, discharged, replaced or otherwise leave the active set.

No universal authoritative `relationshipScore` or `opinion` may replace these meanings. A future debug/display summary may be derived if useful, but it cannot become the sole causal input when the decision needs the underlying semantics.

The relationship system also does not become a chooser. PA-02 still owns actor agency. PA-03 supplies typed inputs that an actor-owned action family may declare relevant.

## 3. Directionality and reciprocity

### 3.1 Directed stance is the default

Affective stance is ordered:

```text
trust(A -> B) != trust(B -> A)
affinity(A -> B) != affinity(B -> A)
fear(A -> B) != fear(B -> A)
```

Writing or changing `A -> B` does not create, mirror or mutate `B -> A` unless a later explicit operation legitimately changes both for its own reasons.

This is necessary for receiver ownership. An initiator can trust a receiver enough to ask for help while the receiver distrusts the initiator enough to refuse.

### 3.2 Structural ties declare their endpoint semantics

Structural ties are not forced into the directed-affect model. They may be:

- reciprocal same-role where the meaning is genuinely symmetric, e.g. one household membership relationship between two people where the exact future representation supports it;
- reciprocal paired-role, e.g. employer ↔ employee or authority-holder ↔ subordinate when that distinction matters;
- unilateral where the relation itself is one-sided;
- represented by one canonical relation with explicit endpoint roles rather than two mutable mirrored copies when reciprocity is guaranteed.

The exact enum/type system is deferred. The invariant is that reciprocity or paired roles are explicit semantics, never an accidental side effect of using one symmetric dictionary.

### 3.3 Player is not a social exception

If the player is represented as a social participant, player↔NPC relationships use compatible semantics. PA-03 rejects a privileged player-only friendship subsystem as the baseline because it would split the shared causal city into two incompatible social worlds.

This does not require every NPC system to expose every relationship mechanic to the player or define final UX here.

### Anti-inflation rule

A relationship dimension does not earn adoption because another game models it or because it sounds psychologically rich. It earns current semantic status only when a same-world counterfactual can show useful behavior that is not merely a rename of another dimension, a structural tie or an obligation.

This is why additional dimensions remain `LATER` by default.

## 5. Relationship semantics are action-specific

No dimension has one universal sign for all actions.

Examples:

```text
ASK_SENSITIVE_HELP
- trust may increase willingness to choose B
- an obligation owed by B may create a different named reason
- fear may be irrelevant or negative depending on the authored action semantics

CONFRONT
- fear may reduce willingness to confront
- authority may change whether confrontation/report/escalation is eligible
- affinity may suppress or redirect confrontation for this action family

SEEK_MEDIATION
- fear of B may make mediation preferable to direct confrontation
- trust in mediator C may affect target choice

WORK_REQUEST
- employment/authority may make a request eligible or duty-bearing
- that does not imply positive affinity or trust
```

Future consumers may use thresholds, weighted ranking, rules, planners or another mechanism. PA-03 does not choose the algorithm. It requires only that material relationship consumers are explicit enough to explain why the relationship mattered and that different semantic dimensions are not silently funneled through one global score.

## 6. Current relationship state vs biography

PA-03 owns **current causal relationship state** and the minimum provenance needed to explain it. It does not own an infinite event diary.

A future representation must be able to answer, conceptually:

```text
What is A's current relevant stance toward B?
What structural tie/endpoint role currently exists?
What active obligation currently exists?
What bounded current reason/source made this state or obligation materially relevant?
```

It need not answer by storing every interaction forever inside the edge.

### Bounded reason requirement

When relationship state is materially used, the state/change should be traceable to a bounded source or current reason such as:

- a structured consequence/event reference;
- an active obligation source;
- a currently authoritative structural role;
- a compact current reason record emitted by the owning consequence system.

The exact record shape, compaction policy, retention policy and long-term episodic memory are deferred to future owners, especially PA-06.

### Obligation lifecycle requirement

An obligation must be more than a permanent tag. At minimum, future consumers need semantics equivalent to:

```text
identity
obligated endpoint
beneficiary endpoint
obligation family / meaning
source/reason
current active/inactive lifecycle condition
legitimate way to fulfil/consume/discharge/expire/replace it
```

PA-03 does not freeze the lifecycle enum or exact transitions. It requires only enough identity and lifecycle to avoid “owes favour forever” and to explain why the obligation is or is not currently a decision input.

## 7. Relationship changes and ownership seams

Relationship state can change because of many events, but PA-03 does not absorb the systems that create those events.

The safe conceptual flow is:

```text
owning system resolves a structured cause/outcome
        ↓
normal shared consequence path emits an authorized relationship effect/request
        ↓
relationship owner applies/records current relationship change
        ↓
future actor decision may consume the new relationship state
```

This prevents a relationship module from becoming an omniscient world historian or a second copy of memory, rumour, work, activity or governance.

### Ownership matrix

| Concern | PA-03 ownership | Other owner / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| current directed relationship stance A→B | **PA-03 owns semantic distinction** | final runtime representation/tuning belongs to future consumer |
| structural social tie / endpoint-role distinction | **PA-03 owns need to keep it separate from affect** | exact family/job/institution taxonomy is later/content/system owned |
| current identifiable obligation as relationship input | **PA-03 owns semantic requirement** | exact domain transaction/economic rules may belong to PA-07 or another cause owner |
| actor knows its own stance toward B | **PA-03 may expose owned current state** | access policy still obeys actor-information boundaries |
| actor knows a private relationship between C and D | **not ambient PA-03 truth** | PA-04 belief/knowledge acquisition and PA-05 information transfer determine legitimate knowledge |
| belief that someone likes/trusts/fears another | PA-03 may be the canonical world fact being referred to | **PA-04 owns belief/truth/uncertainty semantics** |
| rumour/gossip about a relationship | PA-03 does not propagate it | **PA-05 owns communication/propagation** |
| autobiographical recollection of why a relationship evolved | PA-03 keeps only bounded current provenance as needed | **PA-06 owns memory/history/forgetting/compaction** |
| employment economy, shifts, production, wages/material dependency | PA-03 may represent a relevant current employment tie/role | **PA-07 owns work/material simulation** |
| activity participation/outcome | PA-03 may receive a structured relationship consequence after resolution | **PA-08 owns activity rules/stakes/outcomes** |
| player action causes social consequence | PA-03 can receive the same kind of structured relationship effect as NPC-caused action | **PA-09 owns generic player-action causality and interaction semantics** |
| institutional/governance rule changes authority/resources/access | PA-03 may consume changed role/tie/context and later relationship effects | **PA-12 owns governance/rules; policy does not directly rewrite every actor's affect by fiat** |
| runtime storage/index/query API, save/load, UI, network scale | no final PA-03 ownership | future H4/runtime consumers |

### Knowledge boundary

Canonical relationship truth is not automatically actor knowledge.

If Antonio needs to reason about `trust(Carmen -> Paco)`, PA-03 does not grant Antonio magical access merely because that edge exists canonically. A legitimate knowledge path must make the information available under PA-04/05 or another explicit public-rule/perception seam.

An actor may of course use its own current stance `Antonio -> Paco` when that state is internally owned and authorized.

## 8. Same-world / different-edge counterfactual fixtures

These are research acceptance fixtures, not executable runtime tests yet. Exact numeric values are illustrative bands, not global tuning contracts.

### CF-01 — Trust changes one target, exactly one relationship input differs

Purpose: prove the workpack's required relationship-dependent choice without correlated changes or tie-break dependence.

```text
GIVEN
  seed = S
  actor = Antonio
  actor-owned goal/problem = GET_SHIFT_COVERED
  eligible targets = [Manolo, Paco]
  both are reachable and otherwise equivalent for the tested action
  trust(Antonio -> Paco) = MEDIUM
  affinity/fear/roles/obligations and every external input are fixed
  the action family ASK_SHIFT_COVER declares trust as a relevant named influence

RUN A
  trust(Antonio -> Manolo) = HIGH

EXPECT
  selected target = Manolo
  explanation names trust(Antonio -> Manolo) as a material reason

RUN B
  change exactly one input:
  trust(Antonio -> Manolo) = LOW

EXPECT
  selected target = Paco
  no tie-breaker is needed to produce the flip
  actor/world/goal/candidates/seed/non-relationship state are unchanged
```

PASS proves that a relationship edge can affect behavior rather than only dialogue. It does not prove a runtime algorithm or numeric scale.

### CF-02 — Affinity has independent useful behaviour

```text
GIVEN the same actor, seed, social-invitation action family and two otherwise eligible targets
AND trust, fear, structural roles and obligations are fixed and non-discriminating
AND affinity is explicitly a relevant influence for this low-stakes social choice

RUN A: affinity(Antonio -> Manolo) materially exceeds the fixed alternative
RUN B: change only affinity(Antonio -> Manolo) so the fixed alternative is preferred

EXPECT target/reason may change
AND no trust/fear/role/obligation change explains the result.
```

This is the anti-inflation justification for retaining affinity as distinct from trust.

### CF-03 — Fear has independent useful behaviour

```text
GIVEN Antonio has a legitimate problem with Paco
AND both DIRECT_CONFRONT and SEEK_MEDIATION are otherwise eligible
AND trust, affinity, structural roles, obligations, world state and seed are fixed
AND this action family declares fear as a deterrence influence

RUN A: fear(Antonio -> Paco) = LOW
EXPECT DIRECT_CONFRONT may be chosen

RUN B: change only fear(Antonio -> Paco) = HIGH
EXPECT SEEK_MEDIATION or another eligible non-direct response may be chosen
AND explanation names fear as the material changed reason.
```

This is the anti-inflation justification for retaining fear as distinct from trust/affinity.

### NC-01 — Required asymmetry control: A→B must not imply B→A

```text
GIVEN Antonio has already chosen a proposal to Manolo
AND Manolo genuinely owns an optional response decision
AND response semantics consume Manolo -> Antonio state

BASE
  trust(Antonio -> Manolo) = HIGH
  trust(Manolo -> Antonio) = LOW

MUTATION
  change only trust(Antonio -> Manolo) HIGH -> LOW

PASS
  trust(Manolo -> Antonio) remains LOW
  Manolo's relationship input is not silently overwritten/mirrored
  any receiver response change must arise from Manolo-authorized inputs, not from reusing Antonio's edge

FAIL
  B -> A is auto-mutated
  OR receiver code substitutes A -> B for B -> A
  OR one symmetric score makes the two directions observationally inseparable.
```

### NC-02 — Structural role must not rewrite affect by implication

Change only an explicit employment/authority relation while holding `affinity/trust/fear` fixed.

PASS: an action's eligibility/duty/priority may change because of the structural role, while affect values stay unchanged. FAIL if becoming someone's employee/boss automatically means “likes/trusts/fears +N” without a separately caused relationship effect.

### NC-03 — Obligation consumption

Give Paco one identifiable active favour owed to Carmen and make that obligation a declared reason for one eligible helpful action.

PASS: after the obligation is legitimately fulfilled/consumed/discharged, that same obligation cannot continue to contribute as if still active unless a new obligation is created. FAIL if the reason remains forever as an inert tag.

### NC-04 — No private-third-party omniscience

Hold canonical world truth `trust(Carmen -> Paco)` fixed. Remove Antonio's legitimate knowledge path to that private state while keeping every Antonio-owned input fixed.

PASS: Antonio cannot target/avoid Paco for the stated reason “Carmen trusts Paco” merely because the engine has that edge. FAIL if graph truth leaks into actor reasoning as ambient omniscience.

### NC-05 — Inherited bounded-discovery seam

If a future relationship query supplies targets to a PA-02 decision, adding a very large number of irrelevant distant actors/edges outside the authorized query scope must not require a fallback that enumerates the global population first.

This is not a new PA-03 proof of indexing architecture. It is an inherited PA-02 composition requirement: a graph-backed provider must preserve bounded source/query semantics rather than hide `AllPersistentActors -> filter relationship/local` behind a small returned set.

## 9. Minimum explanation contract for relationship-dependent decisions

A future relationship-dependent decision must be explainable without exposing implementation-private formulas or an infinite biography.

At minimum, the semantic trace needs enough information to answer:

1. **Who owned the decision?** Initiator/receiver actor identity at the semantic level.
2. **What semantic decision was made?** Action family and, when relevant, selected target/response.
3. **Which relationship input materially mattered?** Named consumer such as `trust`, `affinity`, `fear`, structural role or obligation—not just `socialScore +17`.
4. **Whose direction/endpoints were read?** `A -> B` versus `B -> A`, or explicit structural/obligation endpoint roles.
5. **What current semantic state was consumed?** A bounded value/band/label/current obligation state sufficient for the decision explanation; exact numeric representation may remain internal.
6. **Why was that state legitimate?** Current source/reason or authoritative tie/obligation identity where needed for provenance.
7. **How did it affect the choice?** Eligibility gate, target ranking, willingness, avoidance, response or another named effect.
8. **What remained unchanged in a proof fixture?** For counterfactual validation, record the declared changed relationship input and the fixed comparison surface sufficiently to rule out correlated causes/tie-break dependence.

For a receiver-owned choice, the explanation must use the receiver's relevant edge/state. Reusing initiator-side state as the receiver's reason fails the ownership boundary.

The explanation contract does not require showing private debug information to the player. Player UX/redaction is deferred.

## 10. Relationship update semantics without ownership theft

PA-03 allows many systems to *cause* relationship changes through a shared consequence seam, but it does not decide the source systems' truth.

Examples:

- **player action:** PA-09 may resolve an action that causes Antonio to lose trust in the player; the relationship effect uses the same semantic relationship owner as an NPC-caused equivalent rather than a player-only flag;
- **activity:** PA-08 may resolve cooperation/betrayal/success/failure and emit a structured relationship consequence; PA-03 does not decide activity success;
- **rumour/belief:** PA-04/05 may change what an actor believes or learns; a later owned consequence may change trust, but PA-03 does not equate hearing a rumour with automatic canonical trust loss;
- **memory:** PA-06 may preserve/compact the event history that later justifies or recalls a relationship; PA-03 keeps only current relationship state plus bounded provenance needed for current explanation;
- **work:** PA-07 may create employment/debt/resource situations; PA-03 can represent relevant current social tie/obligation semantics without simulating wages or production;
- **governance:** PA-12 may change official roles/rules/resources; authority changes do not directly set affinity/trust/fear by decree. Subsequent actor experiences may cause separate effects through normal causal paths.

The governing rule is: **cause owner resolves the event; relationship owner applies relationship semantics; actor owner later chooses behavior.**

## 11. Future H4 consumer notes

A future H4/runtime consumer should treat this result as constraints, not an implementation blueprint.

It must eventually demonstrate:

- directed actor stance with explicit A→B/B→A semantics;
- structural ties/endpoint roles distinct from affect;
- lifecycle-bearing obligations distinct from permanent tags;
- relationship effects capable of changing real action/target/eligibility/willingness/response;
- receiver-owned social response reading receiver-owned relationship state where response is genuinely optional;
- bounded/local/indexable relationship target sources compatible with accepted PA-02 discovery guarantees;
- deterministic/seedable semantic fixtures where exactly one declared relationship input can be changed;
- explanations naming the material relationship consumer and direction/endpoints;
- legitimate knowledge boundaries for private third-party relationships;
- shared consequence paths so player- and NPC-caused social consequences do not require incompatible relationship systems;
- durable current state/persistence sufficient for the product, with empirical scale/tuning proof owned by the runtime phase.

H4 is free to choose data structures, numeric ranges, storage/index strategy, query APIs, caches, ECS/object layout, serializers and decision algorithm as long as these product semantics remain falsifiable.

## 12. Explicitly deferred proof

- numeric ranges, weights, thresholds, hysteresis constants or decay rates;

## 13. Failure modes this result forbids

| Failure mode | Why it fails PA-03 |
|---|---|

| one friendship/opinion super-score | destroys causal distinctions and explanation |

| family/employment/authority encoded only as affinity modifiers | structural semantics disappear into affect |
