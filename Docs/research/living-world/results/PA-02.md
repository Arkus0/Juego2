## 2. Canonical Juego2 finding

NPC agency is **actor-owned choice under bounded, authorized information and opportunity**, not merely visible activity.

A qualifying conceptual path is:

```text
actor-accessible state
+ role / obligation / pressure / opportunity
+ current routine / commitment / context
        ↓
actor-originated problem or goal
        ↓
relevant action family
        ↓
bounded target/opportunity source
        ↓
cheap eligibility
        ↓
small explainable choice surface
        ↓
commitment / execution attempt
        ↓
world action OR social proposal
        ↓
receiver-owned choice when a response is genuinely optional
        ↓
structured outcome through the normal owning systems
        ↓
resolve / continue / fail / replan with bounded recovery
```

The actor does not need to invent every goal from nothing. Authored roles, obligations, long-horizon objectives, world conditions and player-created circumstances may create pressure or opportunity. The key ownership boundary is that a purported autonomous proof cannot arrive with the actor's meaningful action already selected by a quest, director, timetable or authored event.

Player presence is optional. The player may cause circumstances, witness the decision, become a candidate participant, interrupt it, exploit the consequence or miss it entirely. The NPC decision remains valid only if its stated reasons and information are owned by the actor rather than by hidden player/quest truth.

## 4. Bounded discovery / anti-global-scan requirements

“Filter before scoring” is insufficient if the filter itself scans the world.

A future qualifying decision path must conceptually provide a bound **before** expensive eligibility/scoring work:

```text
active actor problem/goal
 -> relevant action family/families
 -> target/opportunity source with explicit scope
 -> bounded enumeration/query
 -> cheap eligibility
 -> bounded candidate set
 -> explainable comparison / selection
```

Required semantic requirements:

1. **Scope first.** A local/known/role/place/opportunity query must not depend on enumerating all persistent actors merely to discover its small relevant set.
2. **Declared bound or bounded source.** The consumer must be able to state why discovery cost is independent of unrelated global population growth for the tested query class.
3. **Deterministic cap behavior.** If a query can hit a cap, selection/truncation and the fact that the cap was hit must be explainable rather than silently order-dependent.
4. **Inspectable work.** Future runtime proof should expose equivalent diagnostics for targets/opportunities enumerated, candidates surviving cheap eligibility, candidates compared/scored, source/scope identity and whether a budget/cap was hit.
5. **No semantic dependence on irrelevant decoys.** Actors outside the authorized source/scope cannot alter the decision merely because they exist in the global population.
6. **Failure remains explicit.** If no eligible bounded candidate exists, the actor may wait, fail, abandon, choose another authorized action or replan under bounded rules. It must not fall back to an accidental world-wide scan.

These are semantic/test requirements, not a command/API/schema specification.

## 6. Social action ownership

For a social action where the receiver genuinely has a choice, the initiator cannot own the receiver's answer.

Conceptually:

```text
Initiator decision
- reason/problem/goal
- eligible initiator alternatives
- chosen proposal/action family
- chosen target
- initiator reasons
        ↓
proposal / attempted interaction
        ↓
Receiver decision
- receiver-accessible state
- eligible response alternatives
- chosen response
- receiver reasons
        ↓
structured outcome(s)
```

A future implementation may package this differently, but the semantic ownership must remain falsifiable.

The following is not sufficient receiver agency:

```text
initiator/action executor reads receiver state
 -> executor directly picks ACCEPT or REFUSE
```

Not every action needs a receiver choice. A non-optional physical/world effect should not invent a fake decision boundary. The rule applies when the product claims the receiver is choosing among meaningful responses.

## 7. Commitment, failure and replanning guardrails

Agency requires persistence long enough for choices to mean something.

Future consumers need semantics for:

- action/goal commitment;
- allowed invalidation/interrupt reasons;
- cooldown/backoff where repetition would spam;
- bounded re-evaluation cadence or event-driven wakeup;
- bounded replan attempts;
- explicit success/failure/abandon/defer outcome;
- safe cleanup/reconciliation when an action becomes impossible;
- re-evaluation after a reservation/interruption rather than blind stale resume.

Exact numeric budgets and authority order are deferred. The research requirement is that tiny/noisy score changes or irrelevant actors cannot make the actor oscillate indefinitely.

## 8. Juego2 reconciliation — player-first causal city

The donor research was primarily framed around proving NPC autonomy. Juego2 adds several product constraints.

### Shared causal world

NPC-originated and player-originated semantically equivalent actions should be able to reach compatible consequence owners. PA-02 does not design player controls or PA-09 semantics, but it rejects a future architecture in which NPC agency has rich structured outcomes while equivalent player acts only toggle bespoke quest flags.

### Player creates causes, not mind control

A player action may create a grievance, opportunity, absence, changed resource, blocked access, invitation, institutional condition or other legitimate input. The NPC still reacts through its own authorized decision inputs.

A hidden `playerDidX -> force NPC action Y` branch does not become agency merely because the player caused the circumstance.

### Continuation without the player

A player-created circumstance may seed later actor decisions after the player leaves. Conversely, PA-02's positive proof must work with the player absent so the simulation is not camera-triggered.

### Positive and ordinary initiative

Agency cannot be demonstrated only by confrontation, crime or drama. Useful candidate families include asking, offering help, inviting, reporting, coordinating, avoiding, maintaining, cooperating or solving an ordinary problem where context permits. Conflict remains valid; it is not the only interesting output.

### No hidden player knowledge

NPCs cannot react to player intent, quest state or actions they have no legitimate path to know about. PA-04/PA-05 later own knowledge/communication details.

### Intentional transformation compatibility

Persistent player/world/institutional change may alter the opportunity surface from which NPCs decide. Agency must not contain a hidden normality force-field that silently restores old options or forces actors back to initial behavior. Recovery may exist through later owners; it must be causal rather than an unconditional reset.

### Governance compatibility

Institutional policy changes eligibility, resources, access, schedules or opportunities. It does not directly write every actor's preference/response. Independent actor reaction remains the PA-02 contribution to the eventual macro→micro loop.

## 9. Product scenarios and negative controls

### P1 — Actor→actor positive scenario: ordinary coordination

```text
GIVEN the player is absent
AND Antonio has an accepted ordinary morning responsibility
AND the current task is delayed or blocked in a way Antonio can legitimately know
AND Manolo is present in Antonio's bounded local/known/role-relevant target source
AND Antonio has no higher reservation preventing action

WHEN Antonio forms a problem/goal such as ResolveMorningDelay
THEN the goal does not preselect one action
AND at least two semantically valid alternatives exist, e.g.
  CONTINUE_ALONE
  ASK(Manolo, help_with_task)
  DEFER_OR_REPORT(...) where context permits
AND target discovery does not scan the global PersistentActor population
AND Antonio chooses for named, actor-accessible reasons
AND the choice creates a commitment or explicit attempted interaction.

IF ASK(Manolo, ...) is chosen and Manolo has a meaningful choice
THEN Antonio's decision ends at proposal/action/target
AND Manolo independently chooses among his eligible responses
AND the result changes at least one meaningful later actor/world condition
AND the causal path remains inspectable.
```

A stronger future stress fixture should continue through multiple distinct structured effects—for example proposal/attempt, receiver commitment or refusal consequence, and initiator resolution/replan—without treating the donor's old fixed `3+` count as a universal law for every action.

### NC-01 — No-player-trigger / fake agency control

```text
GIVEN the same actor-accessible state, problem, relevant targets and seed
WHEN player presence and any player/quest trigger are absent
THEN Antonio can still form and resolve the autonomous decision.
```

FAIL if the action exists only because entering a trigger volume, opening dialogue, starting a quest or a scripted event directly selects Antonio's action.

### NC-02 — Anti-global-scan decoys

```text
BASE:
  bounded relevant actor/opportunity source contains the same useful candidates

STRESS:
  add >=10,000 irrelevant distant PersistentActor records outside that source/scope

PASS:
  semantic decision remains unchanged
  relevant enumeration remains unchanged or under the same fixed scope/query cap
  cheap-filter survivors remain unchanged
  compared/scored candidates remain unchanged
  no scoped query path enumerates the whole PersistentActor population first.
```

If the implementation does `AllPersistentActors -> filter local`, PA-02 fails even when only two final candidates are scored.

### NC-03 — Receiver ownership

Keep Antonio's initiator decision/proposal semantically identical. Change only an authorized Manolo input that should affect his response.

PASS: Manolo's eligible response/reason/choice may change without rewriting Antonio's already-made decision. FAIL if the initiator/action executor secretly chooses Manolo's response.

### NC-04 — Schedule is not agency

If PA-01 contains `08:12 maintenance` and the implementation turns that directly into `08:12 ASK MANOLO`, with no actor-owned problem/alternatives/choice, the schedule is scripting the claimed agency and PA-02 fails.

### NC-05 — No omniscient grievance

Remove Antonio's legitimate knowledge that links Manolo to the relevant problem while keeping canonical world truth unchanged.

PASS: Antonio cannot target Manolo for that specific reason merely because the engine knows the truth.

### NC-06 — Irrelevant-decoy determinism

Run equivalent canonical scenario input/seed twice while changing only opaque IDs or ordering of irrelevant records outside the authorized query scope.

PASS: the normalized semantic decision trace is equivalent. Global persistent/event identifiers need not be identical.

### NC-07 — Replan/thrashing control

Create a temporarily invalid target or small competing score fluctuation.

PASS: the actor obeys commitment/interrupt/backoff/replan limits and reaches success, defer, failure or stable abandonment within the declared future budget. Infinite flip-flop fails.

## 10. Deterministic / explainable trace requirement

Important autonomous decisions should eventually be explainable at the semantic level. A future trace/debug surface should be able to answer, where applicable:

```text
which actor decided?
what problem/goal/opportunity was active and why?
what authorized source exposed candidate actions/targets?
what was enumerated / eligible / rejected?
what named reasons affected the choice?
what was chosen?
what commitment/interruption state applied?
if social, where did initiator ownership stop and receiver ownership begin?
what structured outcome followed?
what causal origin/source is relevant?
```

Determinism means equivalent canonical inputs plus the same declared seed/order semantics produce the same normalized semantic choice/trace. It does not require globally allocated opaque IDs to be identical across runs.

This trace is privileged diagnostic causality. It must not automatically become actor knowledge or player-visible omniscience.

## 11. PersistentActor-grade agency vs ambient presence

PA-02 requires an explicit product distinction between actors that can carry durable causal state/commitments and cheaper ambient population/presence.

- A persistent actor used for an agency proof may own actor-accessible state, goals/problems, commitments, causal outcomes and explainable decisions.
- Ambient population may be cheaper, aggregated, decorative or use narrower behavior semantics.
- Ambient presence does not become a qualifying persistent autonomous actor merely by assigning an “agency level” flag; identity/state promotion requires an explicit later-owned operation if the product needs it.
- Narrative importance may affect budgets/content availability without creating a second incompatible AI universe.

The donor `ROUTINE/SOCIAL/ACTIVE` profile names are not frozen as Juego2 architecture. H3/H4/H7 may choose different representations while preserving the distinction.
