## 0. Question and recommendation

PA-09 asks:

> How can meaningful player actions alter material, social or institutional world state and produce later consequences through the same causal contracts used by NPCs, without quest-specific magic, omniscient reactions or a parallel player-state universe?

**Recommendation:** treat the player as a **first-class initiator of semantic world actions**, not as a privileged writer of downstream state.

```text
player or NPC initiator
        ↓
semantic world action with real target/context/preconditions
        ↓
owned immediate world/material/activity outcome
        ↓
legitimate perception / communication / experience
        ↓
normal owners update their own state
        ↓
PA-02 actors make later choices for their own reasons
        ↓
chain may continue after initiating player leaves
```

The identity of the initiator can matter. Capability, authority, relationship, witnesses, consent and context can matter. What must **not** matter is an invisible distinction where an NPC uses the Living World and the player uses `questFlagPlayerDidX` for the same semantic act.

PA-09 also adopts the embodied-actions amendment: dialogue is one action family, not the universal adapter. A small number of high-value physical/social actions should be able to create causal world changes without implying a giant “everything is interactive” sandbox.

This is research/product guidance, not an input scheme, action API, combat design, crime simulation, save format or runtime architecture.

## 2. Semantic player/NPC action equivalence

PA-09 adopts an **origin-neutral consequence rule**:

> When a player and an NPC perform semantically equivalent accepted world actions under equivalent capability/authority/context, the immediate owned world outcome must enter compatible causal owners.

Conceptual action description:

```text
initiator identity and actor kind
semantic action family
real target / place / resource / participant
preconditions / authority / consent where relevant
immediate owned outcome
observable facts / witness opportunity
bounded causal reason
```

This is not a proposed API. It is the minimum information needed to test that the player is not using a separate semantic universe.

### Legitimate differences

Player and NPC variants may differ because of:

- capability/skill;
- legal/institutional authority;
- possession/ownership/access;
- relationship/obligation;
- physical context;
- consent;
- witnesses and what they can perceive;
- actor-specific later memory/relationship effects.

They may not differ simply because one initiator is controlled by the player and therefore bypasses normal owners.

## 3. High-value intervention families

PA-09 does not freeze controller verbs. It recommends semantic families that can earn implementation only when they create reusable consequences.

### 3.1 Material / opportunity

- give / return / take when ownership matters;
- carry / deliver a relevant resource;
- occupy / reserve / release a scarce opportunity;
- enable / disable / block / clear a bounded useful state;
- help / hinder / repair where a concrete service/material owner exists.

### 3.2 Social / embodied

- invite / refuse / accompany / follow for a bounded purpose;
- greet/signal/show/offer where interpretation can matter;
- join/leave a group or socially meaningful space;
- physically assist/intervene/protect where context warrants it.

Dialogue may negotiate or explain these actions. It is not required merely because the engine lacks another representation.

### 3.3 Activity

- join an existing activity;
- invite/challenge;
- occupy/release activity capacity;
- produce an activity-local result that PA-08 converts to a structured outcome.

### 3.4 Institutional

Future mayoral/policy actions may alter rules, access, service windows or opportunities through their owning governance model. Authority changes conditions; it does not directly choose citizens' beliefs or actions.

### 3.5 Martial / conflict — future family only

PA-09 preserves the amendment requirement that later physical conflict support contextual/graded aftermath and rejoin the same consequence universe. It does **not** research combat controls, damage or crime law here.

A future combat result that matters socially/materially must be able to emit ordinary owned facts such as injury, witnessed conduct, damaged property, protection/help or access consequences instead of living in a separate combat-only flag set.

## 4. Decisive composed scenario — player → NPC → NPC without quest magic

The B1 canonical research fixture composes the three units at Bar F01.

### Starting state

- Bar F01 is inside an operating window.
- A coarse named `bar-service-stock` dependency is currently missing after a delayed delivery.
- PA-07 therefore exposes an affected service as degraded/unavailable.
- Carmen is a worker with normal expected work intent; PA-07 does not choose her response.
- Antonio later has a social/leisure opportunity at the bar.
- Manolo is a possible participant in a card activity.

### Player intervention

The player, through an allowed **non-dialogue embodied action**, carries/returns the delayed service stock to its valid service point.

Immediate result:

- PA-07 material owner records the same semantic restoration that an authorized NPC helper/delivery action would record;
- no `playerRestoredBar=true` quest flag is needed.

### Autonomous continuation

After the player leaves:

1. Carmen can perceive/encounter restored service state and PA-02 may choose normal service work rather than an unavailable-resource fallback.
2. That makes the relevant bar/service/activity opportunity available under PA-07/08 conditions.
3. Antonio later encounters the available opportunity and PA-02 may choose the card/social activity.
4. Antonio may invite Manolo; Manolo owns his own accept/refuse decision.
5. The card session, if played, emits only PA-08's bounded structured outcome.

The chain survives with the player absent because each transition is carried by shared state/opportunity and actor-owned decisions, not by a script attached to the initial player input.

This satisfies the workpack's required bounded `player → NPC → NPC` chain and its non-dialogue intervention requirement at research level.

## 5. Player-vs-NPC semantic-equivalence counterfactual

Run the same starting state twice.

### Run P — player helper

Player validly returns the missing service stock.

### Run N — NPC helper

An eligible NPC validly returns the same service stock under equivalent authority/capability/material conditions.

### Required invariant

The PA-07 material/service state transition is semantically equivalent:

```text
missing dependency -> restored dependency
service degraded/unavailable -> service condition restored as allowed
```

Downstream actors may still react differently **only** if they legitimately know the initiator identity or have different relationship/role/context toward that initiator.

Forbidden difference:

```text
NPC path -> material owner -> actor decisions
player path -> private quest flag -> bespoke reactions
```

This counterfactual is the primary negative oracle for the “two semantic worlds” failure.

## 6. Perception, knowledge and conditional reaction

A player action does not imply universal knowledge.

### Directly material consequences

Some changes can be encountered independently of knowing who caused them. An actor may discover that a door is blocked, a resource is restored or a service is open by normal perception/use.

### Social attribution

Who did it, why, and what it means socially require legitimate access:

- direct witness/perception;
- participant experience;
- explicit communication;
- public/institutional record where applicable;
- bounded inference only if a future owner accepts it.

The engine's causal trace may know the player was responsible. PA-04/05 forbid turning that privileged provenance into actor knowledge automatically.

Therefore the same material intervention can produce:

- a material consequence for everyone who encounters the changed state;
- a social consequence only for actors who legitimately perceive/learn attribution.

## 7. Embodied agency findings

### Dialogue is not mandatory for meaning

The Bar F01 carry/return fixture already proves the semantic need: restoring a shared dependency should not require selecting “I have delivered the stock” from a conversation if the actual gameplay action is physically carrying/returning it.

Other high-value future candidates include:

- occupy/release a chair/table/spot;
- accompany someone;
- return an object;
- help unload/setup;
- stand between/intervene in a conflict;
- block/clear a route where content supports it.

### Context owns interpretation

The same physical act may mean something different because of ownership, consent, role, relationship, location or current danger. PA-09 therefore rejects global mappings such as:

```text
TAKE -> theft
FOLLOW -> suspicious
PUSH -> assault
WAVE -> affinity + 1
```

The semantic action carries context; downstream owners decide the legitimate consequence.

### Affordance density over prop quantity

A hundred pickupable decorative objects do not satisfy embodied agency if they cannot change any meaningful opportunity or human response. Prefer a smaller set of actions that repeatedly interact with people, places, capacity and material state.

## 8. Recovery and intentional transformation

PA-09 preserves both sides of the cross-cutting transformation contract.

### Ordinary intervention

A small mistake or temporary disruption may produce:

- service degradation;
- waiting/substitution;
- request to stop/leave;
- relationship or obligation input where legitimately perceived;
- restitution/repair opportunity;
- later recovery through normal actors/resources.

No unconditional reset is required, but neither should one low-salience action automatically produce a runaway campaign collapse.

### Sustained intervention

Repeated meaningful actions can keep changing owned state after feedback. Stabilizers must not secretly erase those causes to preserve the default town. Transformation may be constructive or destructive.

PA-09 owns **expressive causal entry**, not final propagation/concurrency budgets. PA-10/13 and later runtime work own stabilization and scale.

## 9. Legibility without omniscience

A meaningful player-caused aftermath needs two distinct views:

### Engine/debug causal trace

May know the full semantic chain for verification:

```text
player action
 -> PA-07 material state change
 -> Carmen decision
 -> PA-08 opportunity
 -> Antonio decision
 -> Manolo response
```

### Player-facing evidence

Must be composed only from things the player can legitimately observe/learn:

- service is now functioning;
- Carmen is no longer seeking a fallback;
- people use the bar/table;
- an NPC mentions the help if they know it;
- changed access/opportunity becomes visible.

Player-facing legibility is required; omniscient causal debug text is not automatically player knowledge.

## 10. Quest-magic negative fixture

The following implementation shape **fails PA-09 even if the visible scene looks correct**:

```text
onPlayerReturnsStock:
    questFlags["player_fixed_bar"] = true
    Carmen.relationshipToPlayer += 10
    Antonio.nextActivity = "cards"
    Manolo.dialogueBranch = "bar_fixed"
```

It fails because:

- material truth is private to a quest flag;
- relationship owner is bypassed;
- actor choices are commanded;
- downstream continuation depends on bespoke script;
- an equivalent NPC helper would need a different path.

A quest may still observe or constrain systemic state for authored narrative. It cannot be the sole carrier of ordinary causal truth that shared systems already own.

## 12. Frozen-plan acceptance disposition

The A11B-1..12 and embodied A11B-13..21 scenarios are answered at research-model level as follows:

- equivalent player/NPC action: origin-neutral owner transition;
- material change alters multiple NPC choices: B1 Bar F01 chain;
- witnessed social act / non-witness ignorance: inherited PA-04/05 boundary retained;
- player leaves: chain continues through shared state and PA-02 decisions;
- context changes outcome: identity/context/authority/witness are explicit inputs, private player flag is not;
- minigame consequence: PA-08 structured outcome seam;
- NPC activity without player: PA-08 requirement;
- low-stakes session: material help + social/activity participation can alter future state without story/combat;
- no omniscient retaliation: actor-safe knowledge required;
- story/systemic composition: story may reserve/constrain but not erase accepted causal history by magic;
- save/load: **semantic continuity required but runtime proof deferred**;
- anti-museum: multiple material/activity/social embodied action families are now required research inputs;
- non-dialogue causality: Bar F01 carry/return fixture;
- follow/accompany/contextual signals/graded force/mayoral action: semantic requirements retained, concrete gameplay proof deferred;
- no prop-sandbox false positive: explicitly rejected.

## 14. Unit conclusion

PA-09 turns the Living World from something the player watches into something the player can **touch causally**.

The simplest decisive test is:

> If an NPC can validly change a shared material/activity state and cause later people to act differently, can the player perform the semantically equivalent world action through the same owner — and can the consequences continue after the player leaves?

For the accepted research model, the answer is yes. Any future implementation that needs a private player-only quest path for the equivalent action reopens PA-09's core claim.
