## 2. Canonical Juego2 finding

A normal day is a **stable expected routine expressed as semantic intent**, not executable scene choreography.

```text
normal schedule / selected ordinary context
        ↓
expected activity intent
        ↓
compatible place / affordance / opportunity
        ↓
travel + participation/execution owned downstream
        ↓
actual actor state
        ↓
interruption / invalidation / completion
        ↓
cleanup + re-evaluate current time/context
```

Expected and actual are distinct:

- **expected routine** = what the actor would normally intend under the selected ordinary context;
- **actual state** = what really happened after travel, availability, interruptions, player/world perturbations and later autonomous decisions;
- a valid deviation must not rewrite the authored normal routine merely to hide the deviation.

That separation gives later systems a baseline against which absence, lateness, changed access, resource shortage, interruption or durable world change can become meaningful.

## 4. Juego2 reconciliation

### Playable causal city

Routine is the baseline that makes player intervention legible. A player may interrupt an actor, occupy a scarce opportunity, alter access or change an accepted material condition. The **actual** day responds to changed conditions through normal owners; PA-01 does not create a privileged player-only schedule path.

### Activities / minigames

A schedule may express an ordinary intent such as socialising, eating, fishing or playing cards, but PA-01 does not own the activity's internal game rules or general participation decision logic.

Compatibility requirements:

- participation may consume time/place/capacity;
- a place/resource may be unavailable;
- interruption has a recovery shape;
- ordinary activities may exist without player activation;
- after an activity, normal routine resumes through re-evaluation, not stale execution replay.

PA-08 remains owner of activity integration/rules/outcomes.

### Player causal agency

Player-caused opportunity/material changes are normal inputs to actual routine resolution. If a legitimate player action makes a resource/place unavailable, the authored intent remains stable while actual resolution may wait, choose an allowed fallback, fail explicitly or yield to a later authorized decision owner.

PA-01 does not directly mutate beliefs, relationships or autonomous goals.

### Intentional transformation

Schedule data must not protect the initial town by fiat. If later accepted owners create a durable change—service closure, changed access/opening rules, damaged/repaired facilities, changed recurring opportunities—the actor's day must be re-evaluable against that new context. A stable changed context may eventually imply a different selected normal routine.

PA-01 does not decide persistence thresholds, anti-chaos budgets or whether transformation is constructive/destructive.

### Governance compatibility

Institutional changes to opening windows, access, service, capacity or public-space use are explicit conditions, not mind control. PA-01 supplies baseline intent + changed conditions; later actor owners decide reactions.

## 5. Required semantic invariants

1. **Intent survives route realization.** Changing only the valid route/path used to reach a compatible destination cannot rewrite authored schedule intent.
2. **No waypoint screenplay.** Scene paths, animations or bespoke coroutines cannot define normal intent.
3. **Re-evaluate after interruption.** On release, current time/context is evaluated; stale execution is not blindly resumed.
4. **Opportunity is fallible.** Valid intent can encounter unavailable place/capacity/resource and must have explicit downstream fallback/failure semantics where needed.
5. **Expected != actual.** Deviations do not mutate the expected routine merely to make state look consistent.
6. **Routine != agency.** PA-02 owns actor-originated action/goal selection.
7. **Observed travel stays coherent.** Later runtime cannot depend on visible teleport at time-slot boundaries.
8. **Off-screen fidelity may be cheaper; causal meaning may not.** Abstract progression may omit hidden path/animation detail but must preserve the state needed for coherent re-entry.
9. **Context overrides are explainable.** Selected variants need identifiable reason/provenance rather than scattered ad-hoc conditions.
10. **Normality is not immortality.** Persistent accepted changes may invalidate old opportunities; schedule must not erase them to restore the starting town.

## 6. Product scenarios / negative controls

### P1 — Normal understandable day

```text
GIVEN a normal work → meal → leisure → home routine
AND compatible places with finite opportunities
WHEN the day proceeds without exceptional causes
THEN expected intent is queryable
AND actual activity/place is compatible
AND travel consumes causal time where applicable
AND no unique waypoint screenplay is required.
```

### P2 — Player perturbs an opportunity

```text
GIVEN an actor normally intends to use a finite opportunity at time T
WHEN a legitimate player action makes it unavailable
THEN authored schedule intent at T remains unchanged
AND actual resolution uses an allowed fallback, waits/fails explicitly, or yields to a later authorized owner
AND removing the perturbation does not require rewriting schedule data.
```

### P3 — Interruption crosses a boundary

```text
GIVEN an actor is executing one normal activity
WHEN an accepted interruption spans into another routine period
THEN transient claims are safely released/suspended
AND current time/context is re-evaluated after release
AND obsolete pre-boundary execution is not blindly resumed.
```

### P4 — Durable town change

```text
GIVEN a place/service used by baseline routine becomes durably unavailable through a later accepted owner
WHEN the actor reaches a dependent period
THEN actual resolution sees the changed condition
AND any selected normal-context variant identifies why it differs
AND no hidden reset restores the old opportunity merely because it was initial content.
```

### NC-01 — Required route-only negative control

```text
GIVEN unchanged actor, time, context, activity intent and destination compatibility
WHEN only valid route realization A is replaced by valid route realization B
THEN authored schedule intent remains semantically unchanged.
```

If route implementation rewrites schedule intent, **PA-01 fails**.

### NC-02 — Stale resume

If an interruption crosses from WORK time into MEAL time and the system resumes the exact old WORK execution without current re-evaluation, **PA-01 fails**.

### NC-03 — Player-only activity portal

If an accepted ordinary leisure activity exists only as a dormant player prompt and cannot be considered/occupied by NPC routine without player activation, it cannot satisfy PA-01 + PA-08 ordinary-life integration.

### NC-04 — Schedule force-field

If a later accepted persistent world/institutional change invalidates an old opportunity and schedule silently restores/ignores that change solely to preserve the initial day, **PA-01 fails**.
