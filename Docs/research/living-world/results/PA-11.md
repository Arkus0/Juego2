## 0. Question and recommendation

PA-11 asks:

> How can the player reconstruct a meaningful event that happened elsewhere or earlier from legitimate partial evidence, without an omniscient quest marker, a truth-oracle witness or a notebook that silently solves the simulation?

**Recommendation:** separate **causal truth**, **evidence production**, **actor/player knowledge** and **player conclusion**.

```text
PA-10 / domain owners produce a real causal event
        ↓
ordinary world change + bounded potential trace channels
        ↓
actual witnesses / records / physical state / routine anomalies
        ↓
player legitimately obtains some subset
        ↓
player may infer a cause with uncertainty
```

PA-11 may require reconstructibility for selected important events. It must not rewrite upstream causality to manufacture the answer after the fact, expose engine-only lineage, or force every event to leave permanent clues.

## 2. Four truth layers that must not collapse

### 2.1 Canonical / debug causal truth

Engine/debug tooling may know the complete accepted chain:

```text
root action -> material delta -> actor decision -> transfer -> later consequence
```

This is for validation/explainability tooling. It is **not automatically player knowledge**.

### 2.2 Actor-local knowledge / belief / memory

PA-04/05/06 remain authoritative for what an NPC perceived, was told, believes or remembers. A participant may know only one step; a bystander may misattribute it; a later listener may inherit a distorted report.

### 2.3 Player-obtained evidence

The player may observe a physical state, inspect a legitimate record, hear testimony, notice a routine anomaly, or directly witness part of the chain. The evidence carries source/context/time enough to remain distinguishable from engine truth.

### 2.4 Player conclusion

The player may infer a hypothesis. PA-11 does not require the UI to convert two clues into a canonical answer automatically. A future notebook may retain observations and links, but final UI design is deferred.

## 3. Evidence-channel families

A useful minimum set is plural rather than universal. Candidate families are:

- **direct witness / testimony** — bounded by actual perception, knowledge and memory;
- **physical/material trace** — changed object/place/resource condition caused by normal owners;
- **documentary/institutional record** — only where the represented activity/service/governance action naturally produces one;
- **routine / absence / timing anomaly** — discrepancy between a legitimate expected pattern and observed reality;
- **social/information trace** — a report or rumour with explicit provenance, never ambient global knowledge;
- **persistent world consequence** — closure, moved resource, changed access, damaged/repaired condition or other state whose existence is itself useful evidence.

No event needs every family. The requirement is that an event deliberately intended to be **reconstructible** has a viable evidence ecology rather than one magic mandatory clue.

## 4. Independence means causal/provenance independence

Two UI entries are not independent merely because they are displayed separately.

Examples:

- three NPCs repeating the same rumour from Carmen are **one provenance family** for corroboration purposes;
- Carmen's first-hand testimony plus a transfer container/source mark produced by the actual material transfer can be independently useful;
- a record generated from the same witness statement is not independent unless it also reflects a separate owned fact;
- a routine anomaly can support a hypothesis without duplicating a direct witness if the anomaly follows from schedule/world state rather than that testimony.

The acceptance oracle asks for at least two **independently useful legitimate channels**, not two copies of the same engine lineage.

## 5. Canonical missed-event reconstruction fixture

Detailed evidence is mirrored in `Docs/evidence/WP-PA-B2/BATCH_CAUSAL_FIXTURES.md`.

Use the PA-10 Bar F01 chain while the player is absent:

1. Carmen encounters depleted `bar-service-stock` and chooses to request a bounded replacement from Manolo.
2. Manolo legitimately receives the request and chooses to help.
3. A real transfer restores Bar F01 service stock and temporarily changes Manolo's local material/service opportunity.
4. Later actors encounter the changed opportunities; the chain reaches a stable terminal state.

The player returns after the transfer.

### Channel A — first-hand testimony

Carmen may report only what she legitimately perceived/remembered: the shortage, that she requested help from Manolo, and that replacement stock arrived if she observed it. Her statement is not populated from PA-10's hidden lineage.

### Channel B — physical/material provenance

For this fixture, the replacement container/lot carries a mundane source mark associated with Manolo's side of the transfer. This is **fixture content**, not a required universal inventory schema. It exists because the material transfer physically moved a marked source item, not because PA-11 spawned a clue.

Either channel is useful alone; together they support the bounded inference that Manolo's side supplied the replacement after Carmen's request. They still need not reveal Manolo's private motive, exact decision inputs or every downstream consequence.

### Optional Channel C — routine anomaly

A player who knows Manolo's normal service pattern may notice temporary depletion/delay on his side. This supports the transfer hypothesis without revealing the cause automatically.

## 6. False / stale testimony case

Antonio did not witness the request or transfer. He arrived after service was restored and formed the plausible but false belief that the scheduled supplier finally delivered it.

If asked, Antonio reports that belief with his source/time/provenance. He does **not** query canonical event truth and auto-correct to “Manolo transferred it.”

Later legitimate communication may correct Antonio, leave him uncertain, or never reach him. The discrepancy is valid gameplay state, not a UI defect.

## 7. Routine anomaly as evidence, not solution

The player may know that Manolo normally has a compatible stock/service opportunity available during a window. Finding it unexpectedly depleted/delayed after the Bar F01 shortage is a useful anomaly.

The anomaly can suggest:

- transfer/help;
- another customer/use;
- ordinary delivery delay;
- some other material cause.

PA-11 therefore preserves the observation without automatically attaching the canonical reason. This is especially valuable in a small town where learned routines make the environment legible.

## 8. Loss of one clue channel

A reconstructible event must not secretly depend on one indestructible clue.

For the fixture:

- if the marked container has already been cleared, Carmen's first-hand account plus Manolo-side routine/material anomaly can still support a bounded inference;
- if Carmen is unavailable, the physical trace plus a legitimate Manolo-side witness/record/anomaly may remain useful;
- if enough independent channels genuinely disappear, the event may remain uncertain **provided the design declares that uncertainty rather than spawning an ad-hoc clue**.

The contract is resilience or explicit uncertainty, not guaranteed omniscient solvability.

## 9. Trace production and retention

PA-11 rejects both extremes:

```text
no durable traces for anything important
vs
permanent evidence bundle for every trivial world event
```

A practical semantic policy is salience/reconstructibility-based:

- trivial ordinary events may leave only current world state and short-lived actor experience;
- selected reconstructible events need enough independent potential evidence to remain investigable for their intended window;
- high-value institutional/material consequences may naturally persist through their domain state/records;
- testimony persists only through legitimate actor memory/information owners;
- obsolete traces may expire/compact according to future PA-13 budgets, provided accepted reconstructibility windows are not silently destroyed.

PA-11 freezes the need for bounded retention, not numeric durations or storage formats.

## 10. Notebook / player-knowledge boundary

A future notebook is a **consumer**, not semantic authority.

It may retain, subject to later UX decisions:

- observed fact/content;
- source/channel;
- observed/reported time or uncertainty;
- place/person/object association legitimately learned;
- player-authored or explicitly marked hypotheses.

It must not:

- reveal hidden causal parents because debug tooling knows them;
- mark false testimony as false before the player has evidence;
- mutate NPC beliefs when the player learns something;
- promote repeated rumour copies into independent corroboration;
- solve ambiguous evidence into canonical conclusions by default.

## 11. PA-10 composition rule

PA-10 must leave **real consequence surfaces** for PA-11 to consume, but neither unit may game the other:

```text
PA-10:
  preserve true owned deltas + actual witness/perception opportunities + internal provenance

PA-11:
  select/retain legitimate evidence channels from those deltas
  WITHOUT reading internal provenance as player knowledge
```

A chain that materially transforms the world while leaving no possible perception/trace surface is a PA-10/11 composition failure. A PA-11 system that creates the “correct clue” by changing what happened is also a failure.

## 12. Negative controls

### N11-1 — Omniscient marker

FAIL if UI points to the correct actor/place/cause solely because engine lineage knows it.

### N11-2 — Witness truth oracle

FAIL if testimony is regenerated from canonical truth rather than actor-local belief/knowledge/memory.

### N11-3 — Evidence for everything

FAIL if every trivial event creates multiple persistent records/clues merely to guarantee inspectability.

### N11-4 — Single mandatory clue

FAIL if an event advertised as reconstructible becomes categorically unsolvable whenever one arbitrary clue is unavailable, with no alternate route or explicit uncertainty contract.

### N11-5 — Duplicate-source corroboration

FAIL if multiple copies of one original rumour/testimony satisfy the two-independent-channel requirement.

### N11-6 — Auto-solved notebook

FAIL if evidence is automatically converted into a canonical conclusion not actually earned.

### N11-7 — Trace immortality

FAIL if trivial evidence can grow without bounded retention/compaction pressure.

### N11-8 — Simulation rewritten for clue

FAIL if PA-11 changes upstream actor/event outcomes to manufacture an investigation solution.

### N11-9 — Player/NPC knowledge aliasing

FAIL if learning a fact in the player notebook automatically updates actor beliefs, or vice versa, without legitimate information flow.
