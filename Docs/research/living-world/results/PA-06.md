## 0. Question and authority

What must an actor retain about personally accessible past experience so it can alter a later autonomous decision for an explainable reason, while routine life stays bounded and current truth, belief and relationship state each keep their own owner?

This is a **research recommendation**, not runtime proof. The accepted PA-01..05 findings, the PA roadmap, the playable-causal-city and intentional-transformation amendments, and the frozen donor `Arkus0/Juego/Docs/living-city-research/PA-06_PLAN_MEMORY_CONSEQUENCES.md` are inputs. The donor plan supplies preregistered questions A06-1..6, hypotheses H06-1..5 and failure modes F06-1..8; its old M9/M10 and PA-08/09 owner labels are not imported. In Juego2, autonomous events are PA-10 and player-facing investigation is PA-11.

**Recommendation:** retain selected actor-accessible experience as a bounded *causal witness* for future decisions. A memory says what this actor experienced and why that experience may still matter. It does not assert current world truth, current belief, or current relationship value. An active consequence requires an owner and a compact reason link even if its original episode is summarized. The actor-owned decision must actually consult the eligible memory input in a declared action family; decorative recollection is insufficient.

## 3. Selected-memory semantic model

### 3.1 Candidate, selection and minimal retained witness

Only an actor-accessible experience is eligible: witnessed/perceived, directly undergone, legitimately communicated, or legitimately learned from a source. An off-screen event may produce memory for an off-screen actor if that actor has the same explicit access cause. Merely existing in global event history, near a location, or in privileged rumour lineage does not qualify. Player origin is not an automatic promotion rule.

An experience candidate is evaluated against **declared dimensions**, not an unbounded emotional score: (a) personal stake/severity and irreversibility, (b) connection to an active goal, role, commitment or consequential relationship, (c) novelty versus repeated same-kind experiences, (d) likely future decision relevance, (e) player-visible causal legibility where justified, and (f) whether another owner already retains enough current-state reason. Negative, positive and neutral experiences use the same questions. The selector must be able to reject a candidate with a reason such as `ROUTINE_NO_FUTURE_CONSUMER`, `DUPLICATE_GROUPED`, or `NO_ACTOR_ACCESS`.

Recommendation for a selected memory's *semantic* minimum, not a storage schema:

```text
experiencing actor + access cause
bounded event/cause reference or stable summarized witness
experienced participants/subject/context as actually accessible
time/order sufficient for later causal comparison
meaning/relevance category and candidate consumer(s)
current retention tier and why it remains eligible
if it still explains an active effect: effect owner + bounded reason link
```

This is neither a free-form story transcript nor a source of current truth. A cause reference may point to compacted engine history; the retained actor-safe summary must never be silently enriched from privileged history. If the source record is pruned, an active consequence keeps a self-contained *minimum reason witness* or an owner-controlled equivalent, never a dangling identifier whose loss makes explanation impossible.

### 3.2 Retention and compaction

Use semantic tiers rather than an identical expiry clock for all candidates:

1. **Transient:** routine encounter/outcome with no plausible declared future consumer. It is not an individual persistent memory.
2. **Pending/short-lived:** a live commitment, recent unusual interaction or unresolved experience can remain briefly while relevance is tested; it may later be dropped or promoted.
3. **Selected episode:** an experience with a declared future decision/explanation seam. Keep a bounded actor-accessible witness while that seam matters.
4. **Summary/active reason:** repeated similar episodes may become a count/range or representative reason; current active consequences keep their causal minimum without replaying each episode.

Compaction can remove detail, merge repeats, reduce activation or retire a memory independently. **Forgetting an episode, revising a belief, repairing a relationship and ending an obligation are different operations.** A memory's declining decision weight cannot silently mutate another owner's state. Conversely, a corrected belief or reconciled relationship does not require the historical experience to disappear; it can remain as a bounded, now non-operative explanation until its retention reason ends.

Repeated ordinary interactions must be groupable without producing one durable record per occurrence. A repeated meaningful pattern may be retained as a bounded summary if a declared consumer can use it, with occurrence range and sampled cause sufficient for explanation. A flood of identical low-stakes off-screen experiences must not crowd out a rare severe/helpful event merely by count. No specific slot count, TTL, numeric weight or compaction algorithm is selected by this research.

### 3.3 Decision influence and trace

For a later autonomous choice, a declared PA-02 action family receives an actor-safe memory input alongside current goal, opportunity, belief and relationship inputs. The trace identifies: experience/access cause; why it survived selection; which action family consumed it; current relevance and any repair/recontextualization; eligible alternatives; and the material choice difference in a paired case. The memory does not preselect the action. A historical experience can change a target, priority, willingness or eligible response only through that declared consumer.

For source privacy, tooling may also show privileged event lineage separately. An actor-facing explanation or future player-facing trace must include only what the actor or player legitimately knows through its own access path. `reasonRef` is evidence linkage, not an epistemic grant.

## 4. Consequences, recovery and sustained change

- **Gratitude/help:** a selected player or NPC act of help can make an actor more willing to volunteer, invite or rely on that person later. PA-03 owns any current affinity/trust change; PA-02 owns the later choice. An isolated helpful act need not create an eternal bonus.
- **Betrayal/humiliation/threat:** a serious directly experienced act may persist longer and change avoidance, mediation or cooperation. Severity alone does not force hostility in every action family. A reconciliation, protection, restitution, changed access or new experience can reduce current relevance through normal owners.
- **Habit/shared activity:** repeated ordinary contact need not generate hundreds of episodes. A bounded pattern summary may become relevant if it actually changes a later activity or social choice. Mere recurrence with no consumer is discarded.
- **Severe lasting experience:** recovery does not mean amnesia. A lasting injury, betrayal or public consequence may remain relevant while corresponding world/relationship state persists. No universal timer restores the starting town.
- **Deliberate transformation:** sustained player action can generate multiple distinct high-impact experiences and change ongoing owners. Anti-chaos selection bounds processing and storage; it must not erase strong causal history merely to protect the default town. Intent is inferred from actions/state, never a hidden `playerWantsChaos` flag.

## 6. Causal fixtures and hard negatives

The complete research-level setup, paired controls and expected outputs are in `Docs/evidence/WP-PA-06/MEMORY_CAUSAL_FIXTURES.md`. The decisive positive is `CF-01`: a selected player-caused act of help on day 1 changes Antonio's day-3 autonomous partner choice after the immediate trigger is gone, with all other inputs fixed. The decisive boundedness negative is `NC-01`: 100 trivial ordinary experiences do not yield 100 persistent individual memories or alter the same later choice. `CF-02` requires the causal minimum to survive compaction/save-load/FULL↔ABSTRACT. `NC-02/03` guard corrected belief and later relationship repair; `NC-04` guards hidden provenance; `CF-03` distinguishes a severe persistent experience from a causal recovery path.

These are falsifiable **semantic fixture specifications**, not claims that current Unity/game runtime executes them. If a future runtime can explain a decision only by replaying complete biography, the PA-06 recommendation fails its acceptance condition.

## 7. H4/H7 consumers and deferred questions

**H4 candidate:** implement a selected actor-accessible memory seam, deterministic/seedable causal decision traces, owner-safe consequence application, save/load reason continuity and a compact explanation query. H4 chooses identifiers, persistence, schema, exact querying and proves actual decision changes and no duplicate authority.

**H7 candidate:** preserve the same semantic outcomes across FULL/ABSTRACT transitions for declared cases, including off-screen player/NPC-caused experiences with real access paths; profile selection, retention, lookup and compaction under realistic actor/event volumes. H7 may choose cheaper representation only when decision and reason equivalence is demonstrated for the claim.

Deferred empirical questions: representative content distribution; salience false positives/negatives; retention tiers/windows; how much active-reason pinning is needed; save footprint, load/replay determinism, compaction cost and lookup latency; population-scale off-screen growth; useful visibility without trace spam; effect of repeated activities; sensitivity to rare severe events; tuning of recovery versus lasting transformation. None receives a numeric target from the donor game examples.

## 8. Result and review boundary

The proposed answer is a **selected, access-gated, bounded autobiographical witness** that can supply an explicit reason to a later actor-owned decision. It preserves enough cause for an active consequence after compaction, while current truth, belief and relationship values remain with their existing owners. Ordinary events may disappear or summarize; severe experience may remain relevant; recovery changes relevance through causes rather than amnesia or automatic reset.

Independent Juego2 review passed on the exact candidate above. This accepted PA-06 research is a future design input only and names `WP-PA-07` next; it does not certify runtime memory, save budgets or H4/H7 implementation.
