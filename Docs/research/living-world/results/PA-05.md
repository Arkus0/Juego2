## 0. Boundary

This is the compact Juego2 revalidation of the corrected independently reviewed `Arkus0/Juego` PA-05 study. It preserves the donor's product findings, failure modes and decisive FAIL→repair regression while stripping donor M9/M10 routing, runtime classes, storage schemas, API topology, old milestone ownership and numeric tuning.

PA-05 answers:

> How can one actor deliberately transfer an assertion to another actor through a real opportunity/channel so information can spread selectively, with delay, falsehood and causal traceability, without global broadcast, truth mutation, automatic retransmission or omniscient provenance leakage?

PA-05 does **not** choose the sender's generic action-selection algorithm, own receiver belief semantics, define autobiographical memory, implement dialogue, choose final persistence/schema/API, set product relay constants, or prove population-scale performance.

### The causal donor failure that must remain closed

The failed donor candidate allowed engine-known `lineageId/originRef` to influence whether a receiver treated reports as sharing a source. That is legitimate engine/debug knowledge but not necessarily legitimate **actor** knowledge. If Carmen hears the same claim from Manolo and Paco and neither tells her that Antonio was their source, the engine knowing both reports descend from Antonio cannot make Carmen epistemically smarter.

The corrected donor split two authorities:

```text
PRIVILEGED CAUSAL / DEBUG LINEAGE
  engine-known root / parent / hop / technical identity
  -> replay, debug, budgets, technical loop/dedup guards
  -> NOT actor belief/confidence/corroboration/action by default

ACTOR-ACCESSIBLE / REPORTED PROVENANCE
  immediate speaker
  explicitly reported source
  source/public record actually learned or accessed
  -> may enter receiver-side epistemic reasoning
```

Juego2 preserves this distinction as a mandatory negative regression, not a documentation note.

## 3. Minimal information-flow contract

The minimum adopted model is:

```text
PA-02 / authored owner
sender decides to communicate
        ↓
valid communication opportunity / channel
        ↓
PA-05 semantic transfer edge
sender -> receiver + asserted claim + actor-visible transfer context
        ↓
receiver interprets using receiver-accessible inputs only
        ↓
PA-04 COMMUNICATION acquisition / revision
        ↓
receiver may later make a NEW decision to retell — or not
```

Parallel engine-only metadata may associate the transfer with a privileged causal lineage for replay, debugging, persistence validation, configured budgets and technical loop/dedup guards.

That parallel lane does not become actor knowledge merely because it exists.

## 4. Findings

### PA-05-H01 — A rumour hop is an explicit social action edge, not background synchronization

**Decision:** `ADOPT`

One actor-to-actor information hop requires an identifiable sender, receiver, asserted claim and valid opportunity/channel. A relationship edge, canonical truth mutation or belief change is not itself delivery.

Forbidden baseline:

```text
OnBeliefChanged(actor, X):
    foreach socialNeighbor:
        copy(X)
```

That destroys PA-02 sender motive, PA-04 acquisition causality and PA-03 relationship meaning.

A public notice or witnessed event also does not silently synthesize a rumour edge. PA-04 `PUBLIC` / `PERCEPTION` acquisition remain separate causes; PA-05 owns only actor-to-actor communication transport.

### PA-05-H02 — Communication opportunity/channel is causal

**Decision:** `ADOPT`

Social adjacency means an actor may be relevant; it does not mean telepathic delivery.

A transfer therefore needs a channel/opportunity that can fail or occur later. Examples are conceptual, not frozen enums:

- direct conversation;
- eavesdroppable communication;
- an explicit remote channel;
- a structured ABSTRACT encounter/opportunity.

ABSTRACT simulation need not instantiate loaded GameObjects, but it still needs participants, time/context and a rule explaining why delivery happened then rather than everywhere immediately.

This makes missed information and temporal delay real gameplay state.

### PA-05-H03 — The receiver owns interpretation

**Decision:** `ADOPT`

Transport delivers an assertion. Receiver-side PA-04 acquisition/revision decides what epistemic state results.

Receiver interpretation may consume only actor-accessible inputs supplied by their owners, such as:

- current receiver belief;
- immediate speaker;
- actor-accessible reported provenance;
- PA-03 relationship/context exposed through an authorized seam;
- channel/context cues the receiver can actually access.

Transport must not decide receiver acceptance on behalf of the receiver, and hidden truth/lineage/deception intent must not enter the actor-facing query merely because the engine has them.

PA-05 does not define a universal confidence or corroboration formula.

### PA-05-H04 — Receiving information does not authorize retransmission

**Decision:** `ADOPT`

A receiver acquiring a belief creates no automatic outgoing transfer.

Retelling is a later actor-owned action decision with its own motive, target, opportunity, commitment/budget and explain trace. The actor may never retell because it chooses another action, lacks a target/opportunity, withholds the information, hits an authorized technical guard, or the information is no longer relevant under a future owner-provided rule.

A capped random fanout is still a broadcast manager, not agency.

### PA-05-H05 — Privileged causal lineage and actor-accessible provenance are different authorities

**Decision:** `ADOPT`

Conceptual privileged engine lineage may include equivalents of:

```text
lineageId
rootCauseRef / originEventRef
parentTransferRef
hopCount
```

Its allowed purposes include replay, debug/causal trace, persistence validation, configured relay guards and technical loop/dedup control.

Actor-accessible provenance instead means only what the actor legitimately learned, for example:

```text
immediateSpeaker = Manolo
reportedSource = Antonio      // only if communicated
publicSourceRef = notice_17   // only if accessed/learned
```

Mandatory rule:

> With canonical state, delivered reports, receiver-visible provenance, relationship/context inputs, receiver rule, seed and every other actor-visible input held fixed, changing only privileged engine lineage MUST NOT change receiver belief/confidence/corroboration/dialogue/action output.

Positive counterpart:

> If Manolo and Paco explicitly tell Carmen that Antonio was their common source, that common-source fact is now actor-accessible and a declared receiver rule may legitimately treat the reports differently.

Operational dedup and epistemic corroboration are therefore separate concerns.

### PA-05-H06 — False and partial assertions may propagate without mutating truth

**Decision:** `ADOPT`

A transfer carries an asserted claim, not a privileged truth value.

Example:

```text
WorldFact(X) = true
Antonio asserts X = false
Manolo receives through a valid communication edge
PA-04 receiver rule accepts/revises
Manolo ActorBelief(X) = false
WorldFact(X) remains true
```

The sender's deception intent and engine comparison to canonical truth remain privileged unless the receiver has an accessible cause to infer/learn them.

A generic stochastic mutation engine is not required for the baseline. Explicit lies/partial/structured claims already prove the needed authority split.

### PA-05-H07 — Guardrails bound propagation but do not define propagation policy

**Decision:** `ADOPT configurable dimensions / LATER exact constants`

Useful guard dimensions may include:

- hop/depth cap in a fixture or policy;
- actor/proposition/lineage repeat/cooldown guard;
- transfer eligibility time window;
- PA-02 action/initiative budget;
- bounded debug/event-history retention;
- future salience input if an accepted owner supplies it.

The crucial distinction is:

```text
actor decision + opportunity -> possible relay
budget/guard -> may permit/stop that operation
```

not:

```text
global manager randomly propagates -> cap makes it acceptable
```

A technical hidden-lineage guard may stop a transfer for an engine reason. It must not silently alter a receiver's belief about source independence or expose hidden lineage as actor-facing evidence.

No global product constants are justified here.

### PA-05-H08 — Public/perception acquisition and actor-to-actor transfer remain distinct

**Decision:** `ADOPT`

```text
A witnesses E
-> PA-04 PERCEPTION acquisition

A tells B about E
-> PA-05 transfer A -> B
-> PA-04 COMMUNICATION acquisition for B

B accesses official source N
-> PA-04 PUBLIC acquisition
```

The existence of public truth/source or a witness record does not make every actor know it. Likewise, an engine-known origin does not become reported provenance unless communicated/acquired.

### PA-05-H09 — Dialogue and behaviour consume actor belief/provenance; they do not own rumour state

**Decision:** `ADAPT consumer seam`

Future authored dialogue/behaviour may query PA-04 actor belief and actor-accessible provenance. It should not introduce parallel mutable `heardRumour_X`, quest-only knowledge flags or dialogue-local truth copies when the semantic state already exists in PA-04.

Privileged transfer lineage is tooling authority by default, not dialogue state.

### PA-05-H10 — Transfer history is not autobiographical memory

**Decision:** `ADAPT boundary / LATER retention policy`

PA-05 needs enough causal metadata to support active-chain explanation and technical correctness, but an append-only transcript in every actor is outside this workpack.

PA-06 owns what becomes lasting autobiographical memory, salience, forgetting and compaction. Future persistence may compact privileged transfer history, but must not manufacture actor provenance from hidden lineage or silently erase actor-accessible provenance required by a current belief's semantics.

## 5. Juego2 player-causality reconciliation

The cross-cutting Playable Causal City rule requires the player to inhabit the same causal world rather than a quest-only information universe.

PA-05 therefore accepts these semantic player cases:

- **originate:** the player may assert a claim through a valid communication action/channel;
- **repeat:** the player may relay a claim they learned, with only provenance they actually expose becoming receiver-accessible;
- **conceal:** the player may withhold a claim or affect a normal source/opportunity when another owning mechanic permits it; there is no global `make_everyone_forget` shortcut;
- **expose:** the player may communicate or make an authorized public source available through its owner; actors still need legitimate acquisition;
- **investigate:** later PA-11/integration may expose traces the player can lawfully inspect without granting NPC or player omniscience.

The player-originated causal path must look semantically like:

```text
player action
 -> communication opportunity
 -> asserted-claim transfer
 -> receiver-owned PA-04 revision
 -> receiver may later act/retell independently
```

not:

```text
quest says player revealed X
 -> set knows_X on town
```

This preserves PA-09 generic player-action ownership: PA-05 proves compatibility with first-class player causality but does not define the full player interaction system.

## 7. Failure modes / hard negative gates

A future consumer falsifies PA-05 if it introduces any of these without reopening the finding:

1. **Global broadcast:** one truth/belief mutation writes many actors' beliefs without per-acquisition cause.
2. **Rumour-as-truth:** repetition mutates canonical WorldFact.
3. **Automatic echo:** receive automatically creates an outgoing relay.
4. **Gossip without motive:** encounter mechanically emits all known claims.
5. **Telepathic delivery:** relationship adjacency alone delivers information.
6. **Sender-owned receiver result:** transfer decides receiver acceptance.
7. **Privileged provenance leak:** hidden root/parent/lineage changes receiver epistemic output with actor-visible inputs fixed.
8. **Hidden lie/truth leak:** receiver branches on engine `isLie/isFalse/isStale` without accessible cause.
9. **Budget theatre:** capped global diffusion is called actor agency.
10. **Provenance explosion:** every ActorBelief carries full recursive engine history.
11. **Rumour owns memory:** transfer log becomes permanent actor biography.
12. **Dialogue duplication:** mutable rumour flags can diverge from PA-04 belief state.
13. **Player-only information universe:** player actions bypass normal acquisition/transport owners.
14. **Hidden global scan:** a small delivered set is produced by unconstrained enumeration of the whole population/social graph.

## 12. Result

The corrected donor finding remains valid after reconciliation with accepted Juego2 PA-01..04 and the current playable-causal-city amendments:

> Information spreads through explicit, motivated, opportunity-bound actor-to-actor transfers; the receiver owns epistemic interpretation; receiving never implies automatic retelling; canonical truth never changes because a claim is repeated; and privileged engine lineage may bound/explain technical propagation but cannot alter actor epistemology unless provenance is legitimately acquired.

The player fits the same model as a first-class causal source/relay rather than a privileged quest broadcaster.
