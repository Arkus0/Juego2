### Concrete conditions that would justify reopening an inherited guarantee

Reopen CITY-00/01 only if the programme demonstrates that satisfying the CITY-02 contract materially requires one of the following:

1. a systemic location whose required ordinary access cannot be represented by the accepted CITY-01 graph without adding/reclassifying a route or crossing;
2. a required district/domain that cannot plausibly exist within the accepted landmass/scale envelope without violating protected quiet fabric or the port/valley identity;
3. a necessary access relation whose physical address contradicts the accepted crossing or public/service access classes;
4. evidence that the accepted mobility model makes the required district revisitation criterion internally impossible rather than merely less convenient.

A desire for shorter travel, more spectacle, more open interiors or easier content placement is not sufficient to reopen accepted geography/mobility.
