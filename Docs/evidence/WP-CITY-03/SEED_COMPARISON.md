## 6. Selection negative controls

Selected result fails if any of these substitutions are made:

1. **Drop X1 to save area:** violates inherited seed identity and old-bridge traversal.
2. **Treat X5 as permanent redundancy:** violates accepted low-water-only semantics.
3. **Turn shared court into public shortcut:** violates semi-private/place-local boundary and creates false route choice.
4. **Remove shop service role because I1 is shallow:** violates CITY-05 `{public,service}` functional binding.
5. **Open an ordinary C/S1 house to create a home test:** violates I0/default ordinary-frontage scope.
6. **Count a PA-dependent discovery route as implemented:** violates `FUTURE_OWNER_CONDITIONAL` boundary.
7. **Continue dry from W.LANDING to Puerto:** violates CITY-00 water/landmass topology.
8. **Promote another I3 hero:** violates CITY-02/06 hero selectivity.
9. **Drop Arroyo crossing or landing seam to shrink the boundary:** no longer the retained seed accepted by CITY-00.
10. **Move bar/shop/civic slots during local blockout for convenience:** turns CITY-04 back into city design instead of validation.

No negative control exposes a predecessor contradiction in Candidate B.
