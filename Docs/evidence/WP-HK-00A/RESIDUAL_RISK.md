## Blocking residual risk

None known inside the declared WP-HK-00A claim/trust boundary.

## Accepted non-blocking residuals

1. **Prose cannot mechanically prove every future semantic interpretation.** The architecture oracle protects the six mandatory causal boundaries and named DAG relationships, but independent Reviewer judgment is still required for semantic consistency.
2. **External upstream projects can change after the 2026-09-19 audit snapshot.** This WP intentionally does not treat research observations as adoption evidence. Exact version/commit, license and linkage/distribution mode must be revalidated at the later adoption point.
3. **HK01/HK04/HK07 can still implement the accepted architecture incorrectly.** Their workpacks remain foundational and must prove their own canonical contract, transaction and transport conformance behaviour rather than inheriting PASS from this architecture WP.
4. **H1 engine abstractions are not selected yet.** This is deliberate scope: HK00A freezes that Unity is downstream and replaceable, not the final future multi-engine API.
5. **Unusual license interpretation may require human/legal review.** The policy fails closed on unknown/ambiguous terms instead of claiming automated legal certainty.

## Proof-budget decision

No additional proof machinery is justified for these residuals. Expanding HK00A into runtime conformance, legal automation, generic policy parsing or future-engine design would cross the WP boundary without closing an acceptance gap.
