# WP-HK-00A — Residual Risk

## Blocking residual risk

None known inside the declared WP-HK-00A claim/trust boundary after repair cycle 1.

The first independent review identified one blocking ambiguity: a legitimate engine-scoped public capability had no explicitly accepted ownership/composition path that preserved both H0 engine-neutrality and one canonical semantic authority. Repair cycle 1 closes that architecture gap by defining an Arkus-owned scoped composition path and causal controls against both invalid alternatives.

## Accepted non-blocking residuals

1. **Prose cannot mechanically prove every future semantic interpretation.** The architecture oracle protects the eight mandatory causal boundaries and named DAG relationships, but independent Reviewer judgment is still required for semantic consistency.
2. **External upstream projects can change after the 2026-09-19 audit snapshot.** This WP intentionally does not treat research observations as adoption evidence. Exact version/commit, license and linkage/distribution mode must be revalidated at the later adoption point.
3. **HK01/HK04/HK07/H1 can still implement the accepted architecture incorrectly.** Their workpacks remain foundational/blocked as applicable and must prove their own contract composition, transaction, transport and engine-bridge conformance behaviour rather than inheriting PASS from this architecture WP.
4. **The exact canonical composer API/serialization is not selected here.** HK00A freezes ownership and invariants; HK01 owns the concrete engine-neutral contract/composition representation and synthetic-provider conformance tests.
5. **Concrete H1 engine abstractions and Unity capability definitions are not selected yet.** This is deliberate scope. H1 must instantiate the accepted scoped-composition boundary and may not reopen it with Unity runtime types in the kernel or a Unity-owned public registry.
6. **Unusual license interpretation may require human/legal review.** The policy fails closed on unknown/ambiguous terms instead of claiming automated legal certainty.

## Proof-budget decision

The two new negative controls correspond directly to the independent Reviewer's material in-claim defect class. No broader runtime conformance, Unity API design, legal automation, generic policy parsing or future-engine implementation was added. Expanding beyond this would cross the WP boundary without closing an additional acceptance gap.
