## Residual risks (outside the claim)

- Unselected pack content, finished character clothing/wardrobe, production variants, bespoke Cantabrian architecture/props, missing gameplay animations, production LOD/performance, visual polish and final animation behaviour remain H2/ART/CITY production concerns.
- The rendered capture is supplementary and hosted-rendered; it is not a visual-quality or parity oracle. Pixel output across GPUs/drivers is not claimed.
- Import expectations use a declared, uniform conversion (FBX handedness flip, centimetre→metre unit scale, single-root collapse). A future Unity importer that changes these conventions is detected (Stage 0 fails) but is not predicted.
- Historical exact-SHA freeze verifiers of already accepted WPs (for example `scripts/h1-05-evidence-check.py`, `scripts/h1-04-public-conformance.py`, `scripts/ctx-dw-h1-0{1,2}-verify-exact-sha.sh`, `scripts/ctx-dw-h1-02-proof.py`) pin the pre-H1-11 catalogue counts/blobs/projection digest of their own frozen candidates (and several also enforce a baseline-diff scope). They remain bound to those candidates and are not re-run on later `main`; the live suites (Main Safety including the CTX-DW-H1 lifecycle/identity/selective-adoption tests, H1-07..H1-10 Unity workflows) carry the regression.
- CTX-DW-H1 derived projection: refreshed to the extended authority (`Docs/evidence/WP-CTX-DW-H1-01/LIFECYCLE.md`, digest `bc9af3fc…`, 294 facts) because its accepted lifecycle requires a new identity whenever the pinned authority blobs change. The CTX-DW-H1-02 selective-adoption dispositions classify capability classes that are unchanged by additional catalogue rows; its recorded evaluation identity stays historical.
- Cross-machine portability of the checkpoint environment fingerprint remains the accepted H1-10 residual.
