### SDK implementation behavior

The exact SDK's documented MCP JSON-RPC/stdio mechanics are part of the trusted infrastructure base permitted by the foundational proof standard. Arkus tests its semantic boundary and replaceability; it does not re-prove arbitrary upstream implementation internals or corruption.

### Vendor/client interoperability

### HTTP/cloud/authentication

HK07B is local stdio only. HTTP/SSE/cloud hosting, authentication, tenancy, encryption and remote service operations remain forbidden/out of scope here.

### Process-local durability and concurrency

The production projection still uses the accepted HK07A process-local portable session. Durable storage, crash recovery, multi-process locking and distributed concurrency are not added by MCP. Snapshot/journal portability remains the accepted recovery mechanism.

### Admission-only cancellation/timeouts

HK07B preserves HK07A's accepted rule: cancellation/deadline may prevent dispatch during admission; after synchronous canonical dispatch begins, its truthful outcome wins. Forced interruption/resource quotas are later operational work.

### Interaction efficiency

Pagination, batching, compact schemas/results, stale-state recovery ergonomics and correction loops remain HK08A/HK08B ownership. MCP transports the currently accepted shapes rather than pre-cooking those semantics.
