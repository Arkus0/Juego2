## Content-shape probe

The effective proof checkpoints and rebuilds the accepted Potes market-corner hierarchy: `plaza.potes > market.potes > {bar.potes, workshop.potes}` using the H1-04 Quaternius facade prefab (managed prefab variants) and, on the bar, the facade mesh with its renderer material, a UAL1 animator clip and a `faces` canonical link. Findings:

1. The accepted realization digest embeds generation-local derivative-prefab locators, so a raw digest can never show clean-rebuild parity. Classified as an H1-10-owned normalization, not an H1-05/06 reopen (those digests are used within one generation).
2. A renderer material on the facade *prefab* root fails `projection.component-material-slot-cardinality`; the accepted H1-07 shape places components on the mesh (`asset`) node. Out of boundary for H1-10 (H1-07-owned, working as accepted); the probe uses the accepted shape.
