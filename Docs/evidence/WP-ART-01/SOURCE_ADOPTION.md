All five selected distributions state CC0 1.0 and permit commercial use; the exact bundled license bytes are the adoption evidence. Source URLs identify the vendors' pack pages; they do not replace the local exact license and content pins. CC0 imposes no required attribution; Arkus nevertheless preserves source and derivative lineage for auditing/notice generation. Art assets are project authoring inputs and optional game-content dependencies, not Arkus kernel/runtime code or semantic authority. Arkus/CITY retains identity, graph, transaction, place and access meaning; replacing a pack requires remapping ART IDs/reviewing compositions, not changing those contracts. Security/update owner: Juego2 asset owner. Distribution is external local read-only source → ignored selected Unity copies → reviewed Juego2 derivatives/benchmarks. No hosted service is required.

`SOURCE_LOCK.json` is a file-level pin for the three newly selected packs, whose vendor labels are not used as reproducible version identifiers. The Source archive and UAL pins inherit H1-04; ART-01 does not claim H1-04 admitted all their content. If a later build ships selected original bytes, include this ledger in the product SBOM/third-party record. Render-pipeline material conversion and effective scale are ART composition concerns, not a change to upstream bytes.
