## Structural and compatibility findings

Inspected the actual glTF JSON/BIN and imported the four Unity FBX plus `Regular_Male/Female_OnlyHead.fbx` in Blender 5.2.1. Each FBX has one 65-bone armature and skinned meshes. For each sex, the outfit FBX bone-name list **exactly equals** its Regular OnlyHead FBX list; the four glTF outfits likewise have one 65-joint skin and the same joint-name order as the corresponding Regular OnlyHead glTF. Male inverse-bind matrices match byte-for-byte in parsed float values. Female matrices differ only by exported floating-point noise (maximum absolute element delta `4.62e-7`); their named rest transforms likewise differ by tiny numeric noise. All 20 modular glTF parts carry the same 65-joint list and rest transform as their complete same-sex/same-family outfit. This is strong structural evidence for a Regular-head combination, not proof of a final fit.

The four outfits contain no heads. The two Peasant outfits each have four skinned mesh groups (`Arms`, `Body`, `Feet`, `Legs`); the Ranger outfits expose additional hood, shoulder and belt/bracer mesh groups. The Unity FBX exports and glTF exports are separate representations of this local pack. The free folder contains no .blend sources or animation clips. Peasant/Ranger materials use base color, normal and ORM maps; the Ranger glTF also references Regular male/female skin material. The free texture folder includes two Peasant and two Ranger base-color choices, plus base-skin maps; these are source colorways, not a Juego2 palette. Unity FBX material/texture remapping, URP shader behavior and transparency must be validated on the H2F baseline.

**Compatibility limit:** matching bones and included split exports make Regular-head assembly plausible. They do not prove a Unity `Humanoid` Avatar import, UAL/GC2 retarget, neckline/seam fit, skin weighting during motion, hair/hood clearance, teen/superhero body fit, cross-sex part mixing, footwear/leg seam fit, LOD or performance. The bundled README warns that a full Regular body will clip. ART-CHAR should test head-only assembly, then idle/walk/sit/arm-reach deformations and one deliberately invalid full-body overlay. Do not label any production prefab compatible until those effective tests pass.

## Source-facing visual triage

Classification is for **separable geometry as raw material** in a late-1990s/early-2000s northern-Spain port town. `USABLE` means the geometry may work with minimal treatment, **not** that it has passed Unity/animation validation. No minimum pack-use quota applies. Observations come from the four actual glTF outfits rendered in neutral Blender preview, not the vendor collage of additional paid outfits.

## Coverage effect and ART-CHAR handoff

This source offers a **partial** path for generic trousers, sleeves, a work-shirt seed and wet-weather/work-boot seed. It may diversify workers, fishermen, workshop staff, hospitality/service staff and ordinary residents only **after** contemporary top/footwear, head fit and animated deformation are proven. It does not supply a coherent 1999–2003 wardrobe for youth, older residents, nightlife, commercial/service uniforms, coats/rainwear, headwear, modern shoes or varied hair. It cannot replace the existing `clothed_scale.forastero.v1` ART-01 benchmark reference or close any environment/structural gap.

For `WP-ART-CHAR-01`, evaluate the exact shortlisted parts above against Regular male/female head-only bases; define any reusable toggle/belt/strap-removal and recolor operations with lineage; test one valid and one intentionally invalid assembly, Humanoid/UAL/GC2 retarget, seams and motion. `WP-ART-CHAR-02` owns repeatable recipes, variants, population batch and throughput. If the shortlist proves too costly or visually false, reject it without penalty; Quaternius authorship alone is not a reason to retain a piece.
