# ART-01 derivative, source and material ledger

`KIT_COMPOSITION_MANIFEST.json` is the machine-readable ART companion for every bounded source model and admitted derivative. `Tools/art01_build_manifest.py` regenerates it from `SOURCE_LOCK.json` and the normalized Unity audit. It records stable logical ID, source file hash/license, effective bounds, root scale and rotation, pivot/facing, hosts, repeat/snap, connections, material family, collision role, incompatibilities and readiness. These are ART composition fields. They do not redefine H1 catalogue IDs, Unity GUIDs as canonical identity, or CITY place/route semantics.

## Exact source and provenance

The H1-04 accepted Medieval Village Source/Unity URP archive hash remains pinned in `SOURCE_LOCK.json`; the locally selected Nature, Props, Base Characters and UAL1 files and exact CC0 license files are separately pinned there. `SOURCE_ADOPTION.md` records the adoption decision. `Tools/art01_import_sources.py verify` fails on changed archive/file/license bytes. Its `import` mode copies **only** 51 selected files into ignored `Assets/Arkus/ART/External`, keeps deterministic Unity meta GUIDs, and never edits the owner source vault. The frozen repository contains ART-owned derivatives and metadata, not a copy of the entire Quaternius packs.

## Importer and material decisions

The effective Unity audit before normalization exposed wall meshes at ~200 × 312 × 40 cm, roof 8×14 at ~995 × 678 × 1570 cm, Nature trees at ~431 × 726 × 457 cm and a Props Barrel visually ~69 × 89 × 69 **metres** when its root ×100 was naïvely retained without importer normalization. The scoped ART postprocessor applies `globalScale=0.01` to Medieval/Nature/Props and leaves the Props prefab root ×100 intact. The normalized audit shows wall 2.00 × 3.122689 × 0.406 m, roof 8×14 9.952 × 6.781 × 15.706 m, CommonTree 4.311 × 7.265 × 4.578 m and Barrel 0.698 × 0.898 × 0.698 m. Props preserve their imported X≈270° root rotation; wrapper yaw supplies street facing. This resolves #233's root-scale/pivot discovery without editing purchased bytes.

`Art01Materials.cs` maps each selected source material by name to explicit built-in Standard materials; unknown names fail closed. Stone is grey, upper render restrained lime grey, timber dark, shutters muted green, tile darkened, cobble cool/wet and grass ground desaturated. The active H1 Unity project is built-in render pipeline, so Standard is valid here. #233 showed that those same source Standard materials become incompatible/magenta in its URP project; a future URP consumer must perform an explicit pipeline-specific remap and cannot silently reinterpret these built-in materials as URP-ready. The raw `Grass.png` is a foliage texture, not a ground-surface texture; the authored scenic bank uses a solid ground material rather than stretching that alpha image into stripes.

## Rejected and source-only pieces

Alpine half-timber/plaster-grid and chalet/steep roof forms are rejected as direct keeper defaults even when Quaternius supplies them. `Wall_UnevenBrick_Window_Thin_Round` is source-only/rejected for this rectangular retained opening. Raw Base Character/UAL mannequin is `SOURCE_ONLY`; it cannot be presented as a final player-facing outfit. Balcony, chimney, round-rock floor tile, closed shutter and short grass are measured selected inputs but not falsely claimed as required benchmark construction. Props or plants outside the locked 51-file subset were not ingested. These decisions, including their material and metric reasons, appear in the demand matrix and per-piece manifest.
