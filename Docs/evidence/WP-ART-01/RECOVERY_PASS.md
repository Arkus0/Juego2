## Real-asset diagnostic and visual judgment

`RECOVERY_DONOR_PROBE.json` and [`donor_timber_partition_probe.png`](recovery_diagnostic/donor_timber_partition_probe.png) show the exact `Wall_WoodBrick_Straight` source and a material-partition extraction of its timber faces: **53 of 55 source polygons** survive as separable top frieze and lower wainscot geometry. The plaster and masonry faces are removed in the diagnostic derivative; the source FBX is unchanged. This is a concrete donor route, not a claim that all 53 faces should ship or that every timber wall can be split the same way.

The repository-owned [`Tools/art01_recovery_diagnostic.py`](../../../Tools/art01_recovery_diagnostic.py) replays the three diagnostic captures and donor JSON in Blender 5.2.1 from the exact accepted Source ZIP and `RECOVERY_SOURCE_SURVEY.json`. Invoke it with `blender --background --factory-startup --python Tools/art01_recovery_diagnostic.py -- <absolute accepted Source ZIP> <absolute Docs/evidence/WP-ART-01/recovery_diagnostic directory>`. It verifies archive and member SHA-256 before importing any FBX and writes only derived images/JSON, never source model bytes into the repository.

[`source_plaster_window.png`](recovery_diagnostic/source_plaster_window.png) shows the previously rejected plaster family with a genuine recessed window void. [`third_person_recovery_diagnostic_oblique.png`](recovery_diagnostic/third_person_recovery_diagnostic_oblique.png) uses actual Source plaster wall/door/window modules, source roof modules, source frame/leaf/window/balcony and the extracted WoodBrick timber wainscot on a second facade. It has full wall depth, two storeys, pitched roof, gable infill, a threshold and two building volumes at a 1.65 m camera with a 1.78 m scale marker. This is the requested **source → adapted/donor component → building/street assembly → third-person diagnostic** chain. It is not a final Unity/H2F capture, an ART kit import or a CITY keeper.

The diagnostic also exposes remaining quality work: repetitive same-height house rhythm, plain roadway, provisional primitive scale marker/road, unreviewed door swing and gable fit, no port/work/commercial context, no northern lighting/material baseline and no fresh-author reproduction. It reads too much like a generic medieval kit sample to pass the final scene-level oracle. Label: **PROXY_VISUAL / PREFOUNDATION DIAGNOSTIC, NOT KEEPER_READY**. Phase B must build the recovered family in the accepted H2F Unity project, with real source/derived IDs, verified joins/collision, varied human-scale composition and port-town identity; inspect it independently in third person and capture the effective result.
