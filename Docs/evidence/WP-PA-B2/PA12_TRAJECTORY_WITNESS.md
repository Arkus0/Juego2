## Frozen initial state

Runs `A` and `B` begin from equivalent semantic town state:

- municipal room `R1` has four weekly evening windows `W1..W4` with one finite activity allocation each;
- Bar F01 has its accepted ordinary service/activity opportunity;
- community cards/activity `C1` is eligible to use `R1` when access + setup support exist;
- private/selective group use `P1` is also eligible under the same municipal primitive family;
- Antonio has compatible leisure availability for two of the four windows and knows `C1`;
- Manolo has one conflicting obligation and one compatible window;
- Elena (fixture actor) normally seeks one of the `R1` windows for a separate bounded use;
- no actor has a policy-support/opposition flag;
- no ideology/town-mode flag exists.

The same actor/routine/relationship/knowledge/material owners apply in both runs.

## Run A — broad shared provision

Player sustains these concrete municipal choices across the four-window horizon:

1. `W1`, `W2`, `W4`: grant `R1` access to open community activity `C1`;
2. provide the one bounded setup resource required by `C1` in those windows;
3. when Elena loses `W2`, player uses a normal local/office interaction to allocate her compatible use to otherwise-free `W3`;
4. player physically helps restore the setup resource once after a normal material interruption.

### Required research witness at horizon end

- `C1` had at least **two actually usable shared windows** after actor/material conditions were applied;
- Antonio had and chose at least **one** `C1` participation opportunity through PA-02;
- Manolo encountered the same rule but declined at least **one** conflicting window through his own obligation/context rather than a governance role flag;
- Elena retained a compatible municipal opportunity through `W3` after the reallocation;
- at least one later PA-08/PA-10 opportunity exists because `C1` actually ran, not because `sharedTown=true`;
- no global private attitude was directly changed by the policy.

## Run B — concentrated/selective access

From the same initial state, player sustains these concrete choices:

1. `W1`, `W2`, `W4`: grant `R1` to narrower/selective use `P1` rather than `C1`;
2. do **not** provide the `C1` setup resource from municipal allocation;
3. keep Elena's displaced `W2` use unresolved after receiving the request rather than reallocating `W3`;
4. after a legitimate complaint/request, locally reinforce the existing allocation rather than amending it.

### Required research witness at horizon end

- `C1` had **zero** municipally enabled `R1` windows in `W1/W2/W4` and therefore did not generate the same two shared-activity occurrences as Run A;
- Antonio lacked at least one participation opportunity that existed in Run A and therefore made a materially different later leisure choice through PA-02;
- Manolo's conflicting obligation remains actor-owned and does not magically turn into policy opposition;
- Elena still lacks the displaced opportunity at the observation checkpoint and may choose a bounded substitute/request/withdrawal through PA-02;
- later place/activity/service pressure differs from Run A because actual access/capacity histories differ;
- no global private attitude was directly changed by the policy.
