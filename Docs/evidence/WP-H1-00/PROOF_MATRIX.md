## Central claim

For the complete H1-00 input tuple — immutable canonical anchor/snapshot digest, binding version, catalogue fingerprint and bridge/toolchain profile — the neutral contract produces deterministic normalized plans, staged generations, versioned portable observations and bridge receipts. The reference materializer publishes only after successful staged observation, retries idempotently, reports the required drift states from effective managed-resource evidence, and never becomes canonical write authority.

The public machine-readable receipt for this WP is `ProjectionPortableData.Receipt(plan, receipt, observation)`. It fails closed unless the receipt matches the exact plan identities and normalized observation digest, then exposes the canonical anchor structurally plus diagnostics and publication status. The immutable `ProjectionReceipt` calculation object remains the digest/provenance core; the portable record is the cross-boundary contract artifact.

## Independently evaluated universe

H1-00 does not claim completeness over an unknown future Unity object universe. Its finite proof universe is the contract explicitly owned by `WP-H1-00`: tuple members, required drift states, plan resources supplied to one reference projection, the six workpack-named causal defect classes, and the reviewer-discovered ambiguity-order determinism class inside the already-owned normalized-observation guarantee.

For drift behavior the oracle does not infer effective state from the plan registry alone: `ReferenceMaterializer.Observe` accepts an effective managed-resource inventory distinct from the expected plan, and tests independently remove, add and alter effective resources. Therefore a plan entry disappearing or a new effective entry appearing cannot make the drift obligation disappear with it. Ambiguous effective inventories are normalized by a total ordinal key (`ResourceId`, then full normalized resource representation), so equal multisets with duplicate IDs cannot leak caller enumeration order into `EffectiveDigest` or `ObservationDigest`. The neutral project graph is evaluated by the built assembly/reference closure plus the explicit no-project/package-dependency invariant, not by a self-declared engine list.

## Reviewer FAIL reconciliation

Independent review of frozen SHA `ed2f0532c4f2107f697728a7fa7774323445a307` found that `EffectiveDigest` sorted only by `ResourceId`. Duplicate resources sharing an ID but differing in normalized content could therefore retain caller order, making equivalent ambiguous inventories produce different observation digests. The freeze was withdrawn. The repair adds a deterministic secondary ordering by the full normalized resource representation, a direct `[A,B]` ↔ `[B,A]` equality test, and a causal mutant that removes only that tie-breaker and must make the new test RED. This remains entirely inside H1-00's pre-existing normalized-observation guarantee and does not reopen H0 or H1 architecture.
