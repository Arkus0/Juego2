Research date 2026-09-27; read with `CAPABILITY_SURVEY.md`, `CANDIDATE_REGISTER.csv`, `CANDIDATE_REGISTER_ADDENDUM.csv` and `RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`. A `SPIKE_REQUIRED` here is a bounded H2F-01 selection question, **not** permission to install anything on the production project in H2F-00. Each result must distinguish technical failure, visual rejection and missing manual input. ART-01's current PR #234 is active, so its later accepted kit may close an asset gap without falsifying this snapshot.

## Owner cost constraint for H2F-01/02 handoff

Start with already-owned Quaternius, native Unity and credible free/open alternatives. **No paid asset or package is a default selection, mandatory spike input or authorized purchase.** Reconsider a paid candidate only when the free route leaves a *critical keeper capability blocked* or a bounded comparison shows a **large, concrete reduction in authoring time or work** for the actual Juego2 slice, with integration, license and replacement costs included. Record the before/after steps or time assumptions; a vague claim that a tool is convenient is insufficient. Prefer a lawful trial/demo or published technical documentation to screen it; if a purchase is needed to test/adopt, present the specific SKU, cost, expected gain and residual risk for the owner's explicit decision first. An optional paid candidate can remain `DEFER` without blocking H2F-01/GATE when the free route satisfies the requirement.

A Source feature already proved by ART-01 may satisfy the corresponding input/visual question if its effective evidence matches; H2F-01 still decides the foundation integration. S01–S07 use already-owned/native/free inputs by default. A paid candidate stays deferred unless the owner cost constraint above is evidenced and the owner approves any purchase.
