# Setting note — fictional Potes / Liébana

Status: **locked for art direction** (not a full design doc)  
Date: 2026-09-19 (river/port section reconciled with accepted CITY-00 on 2026-09-24)

## Anchor (now)

The playable home town is a **fictionalized Potes in Liébana** (Cantabria):

- Mountain valley, stone streets, market-town density
- Humid, overcast-biased light; green slopes and rock silhouettes on the skyline
- Social/plaza core for living-world systems
- **Not** a 1:1 rebuild of real Potes
- **Not** primarily a coastal Castro-style harbour town

Visual bible v0.1.3+ and `Docs/art/Refs/` follow this anchor. v0.2 adds the proposed alt-Liébana register (Bible §13) on top of this anchor without changing it.

## River and port (now structural)

Accepted `WP-CITY-00` (`Docs/production/CITY_SPATIAL_CONSTITUTION.md`) makes water structural rather than a later add-on: a valley **Río** and an incised **Arroyo** meet at the tip of the old quarter; a small **Puerto Fluvial** sits downstream on the far bank. The retained seed (`CITY_PRODUCT_SEED.md`) already includes a short Río edge, the old bridge X1 and the X5 ford.

Rules for water and port (unchanged in spirit):

- Keeps **valley** identity (inland waterway), not "suddenly on the Cantabrian Sea".
- Same material language as the town (stone, dark tile, muted props); river `#3F5A5E`.
- Working river craft only (raft / small craft / ferry); not an open-sea marina, not viking/cruise.
- The port does not become the hero read of the town.

## Implications for art (recap)

| Prefer now | Later OK | Avoid |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Valley town fabric (Potes refs); river edge, old bridge, ford | Puerto yards, ferry, light river traffic | Mediterranean / alpine chalet |
| Market, bar, workshop life | Port work life | Fantasy keep / thatch |
| Overcast + damp green | Occasional clearer weather event | Palm / tropical nature |
