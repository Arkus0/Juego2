## 1. Decision and boundary

The final setting is **Villa Bruma**, a fictional large northern-Spain port town around 1999–2002. The name is a working fiction label, not a claim about a real municipality. A working maritime port and sheltered urban waterfront give it a coastal identity. Four or five compact **production neighbourhoods** should feel socially connected by recurring faces, work, errands and rumours. They are not formal administrative districts and all five need not be built for the first block, H2-GATE or first gameplay slice.

The first keeper urban build is **B0 Mercado–Muelle**, an ordinary commercial block touching a public port-edge seam. It gives the future slice a lodging start, a shop/witness interior, an activity home, a port-facing lead, a public observation/chase circuit and a changed return destination. B0 is one block and one boundary seam, not a miniature claim that the complete town is already resident, simulated or playable.

The inland Cuña de Confluencia/Puente Viejo/Casco/Bar seed remains accepted pilot evidence. Its Río, Arroyo, X1..X7, W/E/O edges, F01..F08/S01..S03, exact polygons, route costs and CITY-04 scene **do not appear in Villa Bruma's graph**. A graphic resemblance, name or reused asset cannot move any of them to the sea.

The loops `A–S–E–O–V–A` and `E–P–Q–O–E` are real *proposed* edge cycles. `L–A–S–E–P` is the direct public commercial-to-port walk; `L–V–O–E–P` is a second public approach. The shop is a destination, never an edge through its rear service door. No public route uses the controlled work yard, beach, water or scenic envelope. If `B04` closes, `E–O–Q–P` remains a longer public pedestrian approach while B05/B06/B07 are open; the closure removes the direct approach and does not turn a service yard into a detour. If both B04 and the Q–O public ascent close, P is disconnected from this B0 public graph and that loss must be reported.

**Topological third-person review:** From L, the court A and commercial frontage establish the everyday direction. The shop door at S should be visible as a public threshold before the turn at E reveals port sound/working frontage. E offers a deliberate choice: descend to P or use O for an overview. P–Q keeps water readable behind a continuous physical rail/boundary. Q–O changes level and sightline; O–E returns to the commercial street without a hidden doorway. The alternate L–V–O–E arrives at the same port-facing decision from a quiet lane. In a third-person camera, the E turn, Q–O ascent, O overlook and the public/service threshold at P are the four critical readability checks; a plan drawing cannot prove occlusion, comfort or pursuit timing. URBAN-01 must inspect them in playable space.

## 6. Residency and seam rule for B0

- **Active graphical set:** `N.MERCADO` B0 plus the minimum public `N.MUELLE` seam around P–Q and any sightline/continuity buffer actually seen while the player uses E–P–Q–O. The buffer is a presentation/continuity need, not a second complete resident neighbourhood.
- **Graphical boundary:** collision, camera view, sound and possible following at P/Q must have a truthful buffer; a player pursuing an actor across the seam cannot watch that actor disappear or teleport across a visible boundary. When follow-through is required, hold the relevant seam resident until a lawful handoff.
- **Abstract outside state:** off-screen people are not hidden full GameObjects with Animator/NavMesh/GC2 logic. Canonical Arkus/Juego2 identity, schedule segment, current zone/POI, meaningful deviations and durable consequences survive. Coarse clock/events advance the off-screen position; later GC2 WPs define actual runtime transitions.
- **Return:** unloading the graphical block cannot erase a consequential clue, access change or encounter. Future gameplay makes the state visible at the same S/L/P threshold on revisit. B0 currently reserves those physical surfaces only.
- **No framework claim:** this brief defines a residency boundary and handoff question. `GC2-05/06` and `DISTRICT-01` own effective routine/materialization and scaling measurements. No universal streamer or whole-town simultaneous-NPC target is approved here.
