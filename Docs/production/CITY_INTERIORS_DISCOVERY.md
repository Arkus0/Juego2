8. **Discovery exposes truth; it does not manufacture truth from proximity.** Approaching a marker, crossing a trigger or inspecting hidden engine lineage is not a valid discovery cause by itself.
9. **Quiet/ordinary places may remain ordinary.** A second layer can be social, spatial, material, temporal or institutional; it does not require a secret room, clue, incident or reward.
10. **Extraordinary/cultural material is optional and non-load-bearing.** Martial-arts/kung-fu/Hong-Kong/Chinese-cinema influence may appear rarely, but no required city function, route, institutional explanation or seed acceptance depends on discovering it.

## 2. Interior and discovery vocabulary

### 2.1 Spatial vocabulary

- **Interior family** — a reusable planning topology with bounded zone/threshold/access-role rules. It is not a prefab or exact room plan.
- **Meaningful zone** — a spatial area with a distinct use/access reading: public counter room, work floor, common room, private room cluster, staff/records zone, service bay, etc. Decorative alcoves do not count automatically.
- **Threshold** — a meaningful transition between access/use conditions. It may be an exterior↔interior threshold or an internal public↔private/service/semi-private transition.
- **Role-bearing threshold** — a threshold explicitly carrying one inherited access role. Its spatial form may additionally be `rear`, `vertical`, `court`, `staff`, `storage`, `landing`, etc.
- **Minor support pocket** — storage recess, screened counter-back, tiny staff bay or equivalent support area that does not create another independently navigable meaningful zone.
- **Interior layer** — a meaningful secondary relation beyond the primary public/use surface: service/back, private/staff, semi-private/social, vertical, rear/court or other bounded relation.
- **Hero overlay** — a reviewed extra layering applied to a reusable interior family without replacing that family or making a new universal archetype.

### 2.2 Discovery vocabulary

- **Discovery opportunity** — a reviewed place/fact/state surface that can be recontextualised through one or more truthful routes. It is not a quest objective.
- **Discovery route** — the causal route by which a player could truthfully learn about, gain context for, or obtain access to an opportunity.
- **Authored substrate** — stable spatial/object/environment information deliberately authored into the place.
- **Truth owner** — the owner whose state makes a discovery claim true. For a static spatial fact this may be CITY/ART; for a schedule, belief, material state or policy it is a later runtime/Living World owner.
- **Route status**:
  - `AUTHORED_SPATIAL_NOW` — the route can be specified by CITY-06 using accepted spatial/access facts without claiming future runtime semantics;
  - `FUTURE_OWNER_CONDITIONAL` — the route is a valid future opportunity only if the named later owner implements and validates the required semantics;
  - `NOT_A_ROUTE` — flavour, duplicate presentation, omniscient engine truth or an unowned hypothetical cause that cannot count toward multi-route claims.
- **Recontextualisation** — the same place/fact reads differently because truthful state or knowledge changed; it does not require opening a new room.

Two presentations of the same causal source are one route. A note and an icon generated from the same hidden omniscient flag are not independent routes.

## 3. Interior-depth allocation rules

Zone/threshold counts below are **planning bands**, not final metric geometry. Realized geometry may vary inside the band while preserving semantic depth and access roles.

### 3.1 `I0 NONE`

Promise:

- no enterable enclosed interior;
- façade doors/windows may be visual only;
- exterior/shared-court/garden/market/paseo depth may still be playable where `S2/S3` already requires it.

CITY-06 may attach exterior discovery opportunities to I0 places only when they do not imply an enclosed room. Examples include a changing market apron, a shared garden threshold, a work yard surface or a quiet observation edge.

Automatic promotion from an asset interior is forbidden.

### 3.2 `I1 SHALLOW`

Promise:

- **one primary meaningful enclosed zone**;
- zero or one **minor support pocket** where needed;
- inherited required access roles remain explicit, but a required role may terminate at an exterior threshold, service edge or screened pocket rather than forcing another full room;
- normally one public/use-facing interior reading and no independent secondary circulation loop;
- no hidden-room requirement.

An `I1` proposal becomes invalid if its “support pockets” accumulate into multiple independently navigable zones, a second circulation path or a deep public/private/service sequence equivalent to `I2`.

Special inherited constraint: `loc.ribera.service_yard` remains exterior-led; one shallow enclosed support room is sufficient and is the maximum CITY-02 need.

### 3.3 `I2 DEEP`

Promise:

- **two to four meaningful zones**;
- at least **two meaningful thresholds** including the exterior threshold(s);
- at least one secondary access/use layer distinct from the primary room;
- every inherited required access role remains legible and non-substitutable;
- the topology should come from a reusable family before any one-off overlay is proposed.

`I2` does not require a basement, roof, secret room, loop, hidden passage or bespoke cinematic reveal.

### 3.4 `I3 HERO`

Promise for `loc.casco.bar` only:

- reusable `if.social_house` base plus `hero.bar_layered` overlay;
- **four to six meaningful zones or equivalent spatial layers** across at least two distinct access/use relations;
- `public`, `service` and `semi-private` roles are all explicit and required;
- at least one secondary route/read of the place that is spatially truthful without needing a future NPC system;
- multiple reviewed discovery-opportunity surfaces, while future systemic routes remain conditional;
- no requirement for a basement, roof or “secret room” merely to prove hero depth.

The hero overlay may use vertical/court/rear forms, but those forms never replace the inherited `semi-private` role.

### 3.5 `S2..S4` versus `I0..I3`

`S` and `I` remain orthogonal:

- an `S3/I0` shared court can be spatially deep with no enclosed interior;
- an `S2/I1` shop can have one shallow room without becoming spatially deep;
- an `S3/I2` workplace may have multiple zones but no hero treatment;
- `S4/I3` is reserved to the casco bar under CITY-02.

No CITY-06 rule derives `I` from `A..D` or `S`.

### 5.1 Role multiplicity is not room multiplicity

An important anti-inflation rule:

```text
required access roles != required room count
```

A shallow place can preserve three roles with distinct thresholds, screening, counter/back relation or exterior service access while still owning only one meaningful enclosed zone. Conversely, a deep place may have several zones without inventing new access roles.

### 5.2 Internal circulation and CITY-01

Internal circulation is place-local. It may connect role-bearing thresholds inside the place, but:

- it never counts as an ordinary public CITY-01 alternate route;
- a public entrance plus rear service door does not create a public through-building shortcut;
- semi-private/shared circulation remains conditional by semantics even if geometry is physically walkable;
- a private/service route does not satisfy public route redundancy.

## 8. Discovery taxonomy and truth rules

### 8.1 Authored discovery

An **authored** opportunity has a stable authored substrate whose truth does not require an unimplemented future simulation.

Valid examples at CITY-06 level:

- a service/back threshold visibly exists;
- a public notice/document surface exists;
- a workbench/loading/storage arrangement communicates place function;
- a stair/landing/court reveals another spatial layer;
- an institutional/private boundary is legible;
- architectural wear, blocked former use or material arrangement can carry later reviewed historical content.

CITY-06 may reserve the surface/opportunity. Final narrative text, named lore or quest meaning remains with later explicit owners.

### 8.2 Systemic discovery

A **systemic** opportunity exists only when a later causal owner makes the relevant state true and exposes it through ordinary world semantics.

Examples and current future-owner categories:

| Future cause | Future owner category from accepted PA plan | CITY-06 posture |
|---|---|---|
| actor follows daily routine / is present or absent | `PA-01 Daily Life`, later H3+ runtime | conditional opportunity only |
| actor independently chooses or interrupts behaviour | `PA-02 NPC Agency`, later runtime | conditional opportunity only |
| invitation / relationship-gated context | `PA-03 Social Graph` + later runtime access owner | conditional opportunity only |
| actor knows/does not know information | `PA-04 Knowledge/Beliefs` | conditional opportunity only |
| information spreads socially | `PA-05 Rumours` | conditional opportunity only |
| a prior event materially changes later context | `PA-06 Memory & Consequences` | conditional opportunity only |
| goods/work/material dependency changes place state | `PA-07 Work / Material Dependencies` | conditional opportunity only |
| player action changes access/state through shared semantics | `PA-09 Player Causal Agency` | conditional opportunity only |
| investigation makes evidence legible without omniscience | `PA-11 Investigation / Legibility` | conditional opportunity only |
| municipal policy/resource/access consequence | `PA-12 Governance` | conditional opportunity only |

This table assigns no runtime API and does not claim any PA result has already implemented these behaviours.

### 8.3 Hybrid discovery

A **hybrid** opportunity combines an authored substrate with one or more conditional systemic routes.

Example shape:

```text
authored spatial truth: workshop has a material/service surface
+
future conditional truth: a later material dependency changes what occupies it
+
future legibility route: investigation/actor knowledge exposes why it changed
```

Hybrid is preferred for important discoveries **when later systems genuinely support it** because the place remains meaningful even before/without the systemic route. If the future owner never materializes, the authored substrate must still make sense.

### 8.4 Multi-route rule

An opportunity is `MULTI_ROUTE_READY` only if:

1. at least one route is `AUTHORED_SPATIAL_NOW` **or** the opportunity is explicitly marked future-only and is not used to satisfy current spatial acceptance;
2. each additional route names a distinct causal source, not merely another presentation of the same source;
3. every future conditional route names its owner category and required state;
4. no route depends on hidden engine truth unavailable to the player/actor semantics;
5. route diversity does not weaken inherited access roles.

`MULTI_ROUTE_READY` is preproduction readiness, not runtime proof.

- **key/access** → only a future access-state mechanism may realize this; CITY-06 reserves thresholds but does **not** require keys or an inventory system;
- **overheard information** → future knowledge/rumour/investigation owner;
- **document** → authored document **surface** may exist now; final information truth/knowledge effect remains later-owned;
- **schedule change** → future daily-life/consequence owner;
- **municipal consequence** → future governance owner;
- **return-after-change** → future memory/consequence/player-agency/material owner.

A motif with no accepted future owner category is not counted as a route.

## 11. Optional rare martial/cinema strand

The city may contain a small identity-bearing strand influenced by martial arts, kung-fu, Hong Kong or Chinese cinema, but it remains **optional, local and non-explanatory**.

Rules:

1. A CITY-03 seed candidate may contain **zero** such opportunities with no acceptance penalty.
2. If the selected seed contains the strand, it should contain at most **one primary opportunity**; secondary visual traces must remain minor and cannot become a district-wide theme skin.
3. The opportunity must attach to an already coherent ordinary place — for example a personal/cultural trace in a social interior, a poster/object/display surface, or another later-reviewed local connection — rather than spawning a new universal faction/institution by default.
4. No required A/B function, crossing, governance fact, economic explanation, family history or city-wide mystery depends on it.
5. Missing the strand cannot make any ordinary route, place, activity or scenario incoherent.
6. Future narrative/cultural claims require their own explicit owner; CITY-06 only reserves an optional opportunity surface.
7. “Secret martial room in every district”, universal kung-fu lineage, exoticised visual replacement of the Cantabrian/Liébana identity, or using the strand as shorthand for all hidden content is forbidden.

The strand is therefore an accent, not the town's ontology.

## 12. Negative-content and anti-inflation rules

CITY-06 rejects the following patterns:

- **secret quota:** every building/interior receives a hidden room or collectible;
- **door inflation:** `I0` façades become open because an asset provides interior geometry;
- **depth inflation:** I1 support pockets accumulate into I2-like suites without reopening the programme;
- **hero inflation:** I2 places gain bespoke hero layering because a dramatic reveal is attractive;
- **route counting by presentation:** two UI markers, two copies of the same note or two camera angles are claimed as independent discovery routes;
- **unowned systemic magic:** an NPC happens to know, appear, invite, unlock or change something with no future causal owner;
- **omniscient clue:** hidden engine state, canonical IDs, provenance lineage or off-screen truth is exposed directly as character/player knowledge;
- **access laundering:** service/private/semi-private paths become ordinary public shortcuts to improve route count;
- **loot-room equation:** “discovery quality” is measured by number of hidden rewards/rooms;
- **quiet-place contamination:** every quiet route or domestic edge becomes an incident/clue dispenser;
- **theme-park districts:** each district receives its own unrelated secret language rather than sharing grounded families;
- **martial monoculture:** rare cultural material becomes the explanation for the city, all institutions or all secrets;
- **retroactive truth:** a later systemic event rewrites authored spatial history without an owner for that consequence;
- **seed gaming:** CITY-03 selects a candidate first and then weakens/rewrites CITY-06 obligations to fit it.

## 14. CITY-03 seed-selection constraints

CITY-03 owns the exact seed and may compare genuine alternatives. CITY-06 supplies comparison constraints rather than choosing a winner.

Every candidate seed comparison must report:

1. which accepted `I1..I3` places fall inside the hard playable boundary and which remain outside;
2. which reusable interior families those places consume and whether any one-off overlay is required;
3. inherited access-role sets and whether the proposed boundary leaves every required threshold realizable;
4. which exterior-led I0/S2–S3 second layers are included;
5. discovery opportunities included, separated into `AUTHORED_SPATIAL_NOW` and `FUTURE_OWNER_CONDITIONAL` routes;
6. quiet/ordinary places with **no** discovery/secret requirement;
7. whether the candidate includes the optional extraordinary/cultural strand (`0` is valid and neutral);
8. any concrete predecessor reopen signal.

### 14.1 Selected-seed minimum depth mix

The selected seed must contain:

- at least one `I2` or `I3` deep interior;
- at least one `I1` shallow interior **or** exterior-led `S2/S3` second-layer place;
- at least one ordinary/quiet place that remains valuable without a secret/incident;
- at least one discovery opportunity with one `AUTHORED_SPATIAL_NOW` route and at least one genuinely different plausible future conditional route **where a named later owner exists**;
- no more than the single inherited `I3` hero commitment, and only if `loc.casco.bar` is actually inside the selected seed.

If the bar is outside the selected seed, CITY-03 does not invent a replacement I3 hero interior.

### 14.2 Reuse / bespoke balance

CITY-03 compares, but does not mechanically optimize, the ratio between:

- reusable-family instances;
- one-off overlays;
- shallow versus deep interiors;
- exterior-led second layers.

A candidate dominated by bespoke deep interiors is disfavoured unless another accepted product requirement justifies it. A candidate does not gain points merely by maximizing open doors.

### 14.3 Exact handoff required from CITY-03

For the selected seed, `CITY_PRODUCT_SEED.md` must enumerate:

```text
place_id
interior_depth
interior_family_or_NONE
hero_overlay_or_NONE
required_access_roles
selected_zone_topology_posture
selected_discovery_opportunity_ids
route_statuses (authored vs future-conditional)
retained_vs_temporary presentation notes
```

This lets CITY-04 build exactly the selected spatial promise without designing a new discovery system in Unity.

## 15. Review / rejection rules

A CITY-06 proposal is invalid if any of these occur:

- any inherited CITY-05 required access role is weakened, omitted or replaced by a form qualifier;
- internal circulation creates a new public city-graph route or publicises a restricted service/private path;
- an I0 place gains a promised enclosed interior without an explicit predecessor reopen;
- an I1 place is silently designed at I2 depth;
- any I2 place is promoted to hero by default;
- any place other than `loc.casco.bar` receives an I3 commitment;
- the fourteen I1–I3 backlog entries are not all allocated;
- a major district has no meaningful second-layer support at all;
- “second layer” is equated with basement/roof/hidden room;
- a systemic route is counted without naming a future causal owner and required state;
- a future conditional route is presented as runtime proof;
- two presentations of one cause are counted as independent routes;
- hidden engine truth is used as player/actor knowledge;
- quiet/ordinary fabric is converted into mandatory incident/secret content;
- extraordinary/martial/cinema material is required for city coherence or overwhelms the grounded setting identity;
- seed selection is performed here or a candidate seed is used to retroactively redefine the interior/discovery grammar;
- Unity implementation, quest scripting, dialogue, schedules, beliefs, governance or save semantics are authored here.
