# Keeper City — Play-design amendment proposal

Version: 0.2 — 2026-09-25  
Status: **PROPOSED / NON-CANONICAL — output of `WP-CITY-09`; requires later owner-specific amendments before any structural item becomes accepted truth.**  
Class: PRODUCT / GAME-SPACE DESIGN (NON-FOUNDATIONAL)

This document proposes a player-experience layer for the accepted retained CITY seed. It does **not** modify the CITY-00 geography, CITY-01 edge ledger, CITY-02 place programme, CITY-03 exact seed, CITY-05 exterior grammar, CITY-06 interior/discovery grammar or CITY-04 validation contract.

The accepted documents remain source of truth until their causal owners are amended independently.

---

## 0. Problem statement

The accepted CITY chain is strong at truthfulness: geography, access, crossings, place depth, construction grammar, discovery causality and an exact retained seed are all owned. The remaining design question is different:

> How should the retained town piece feel to learn, cross, read and remember as a player without weakening the accepted city truth?

This proposal supplies vocabulary and candidate amendments for that question.

The guiding principle is:

> **The town must remain truthful when nobody is looking, and become easier to learn and more rewarding to traverse when the player is.**

That principle does not authorize new topology on its own.

---

## 1. Accepted facts this proposal must not reinterpret

1. CITY-00 owns landmasses and crossings. No proposal here creates a current eighth crossing or a dry Wedge→Puerto continuation.
2. CITY-01 owns the semantic movement graph. Its route costs are **planning weights / target hypotheses**, not measured times and not NPC schedule semantics.
3. CITY-03 owns the exact retained seed. Only the represented edges, masks, crossings, sites and receiving stubs in that accepted document exist for current CITY-04 construction.
4. CITY-04 builds **only** that bounded seed unless a predecessor is explicitly amended first.
5. In the current seed, X5 is the only represented crossing from the Wedge to the `E.X5` receiving component. Closing X5 therefore disconnects that represented component. That is current truth, not a defect to conceal with an invented detour.
6. ART owns visual/setting direction. This proposal does not adopt the alt-Liébana material from closed PR #198.
7. Living World / PA owners own runtime schedules, beliefs, dialogue, event semantics and actor behaviour. Spatial support may be proposed here; runtime truth may not.

## 2. Player-experience vocabulary

These are design lenses, not accepted topology.

### PD-01 — Route learning

Prefer route structures that let players form a mental map through repeated choices rather than repeated out-and-back traversal.

**Current-seed check:** record where the exact seed already offers a meaningful route choice and where it necessarily terminates at an expansion seam or receiving stub.

**Post-amendment hypothesis:** where an accepted owner later adds loops, each loop should be legible as a genuine graph cycle rather than a visual near-loop.

### PD-02 — Seam and terminus payoff

A seed seam may remain a terminus. The design goal is that reaching it still communicates continuation, identity or reward: a view, a bench, visible work, a landmark, a crossing state, sound or a clearly legible future route.

A payoff does **not** turn a seam into an edge.

### PD-03 — Truthful shortcuts

A shortcut candidate must be a real, owner-approved graph edge with an explicit access/state rule before it is counted in connectivity or acceptance.

Examples worth evaluating later: covered passage, stair connector, one-way drop with an owned state transition. No player-only teleports.

### PD-04 — Closure truth

A closure test asks what the accepted graph actually does.

- If an accepted alternate exists inside the represented seed, walk it and measure it.
- If no accepted alternate exists inside the represented seed, record the expected disconnection.
- Never construct an out-of-seed route merely so a detour test can pass.

For the current exact seed, **closing X5 disconnects `E.X5`**. A future predecessor amendment may change the represented topology; until then, that disconnection is the correct CITY-04 observation.

### PD-05 — Landmark hierarchy

Use three scales of orientation cue:

- L1: whole-seed or whole-town landmark candidate;
- L2: district/route-family signature;
- L3: local turn-at cue such as a fountain, sign, tree, bench or threshold.

Candidate L1 proposals remain non-canonical until the owning place/exterior documents are amended.

### PD-06 — Framed views

Protect a small set of views that help orientation and memory. Candidate view positions may be named before greybox; their visibility and quality remain hypotheses until measured physically.

### PD-07 — Door/readability grammar

Enterable, non-enterable, public, service and private thresholds should be distinguishable at ordinary play distance without contradicting the accepted CITY-05/06 role set.

This proposal does not invent a new access role.

### PD-08 — Two measurement domains, not two semantic clocks

Keep three concepts distinct:

1. **CITY-01 planning route weight** — an accepted semantic planning hypothesis used for topology/cost reasoning.
2. **Measured player traversal time** — a future CITY-04 physical observation at walk/jog speeds for represented geometry.
3. **Runtime schedule time** — future Living World / PA semantics, outside CITY-09.

No one of these is renamed into another.

### PD-09 — Traversal rhythm

Measure long stretches with no new spatial cue, choice, reveal or affordance. Any target spacing is a hypothesis to calibrate in greybox rather than an accepted constant.

### PD-10 — Comfort

Prefer readable stairs, natural boundaries, non-trapping edges, legible changes of level and collision that does not contradict visual form. CITY-04 may record comfort defects but must route semantic changes back to the appropriate owner.

### PD-11 — Reusable stage types

Spatial design may reserve or identify reusable situation shapes without asserting that gameplay exists there.

Candidate types:

| Stage type | Spatial need |
|---|---|
| Confrontation | readable linear span, two approaches, room for observers |
| Plaza/crowd | open floor, edges, vantage, multiple exits |
| Chase | turns, level change, alternate visual lines |
| Follow/search | branching and reconvergence |
| Conversation | semi-sheltered edge or nook |
| Observation | elevated or protected vantage |
| Practice/play | bounded open ground |
| Home base | central return point with several approach relations |

The stage label is spatial support only; it creates no combat, quest or NPC behaviour.

### PD-12 — Composed views

A bounded set of postcard candidates can make the city memorable and can double as orientation evidence. Their final art treatment belongs elsewhere.

### PD-13 — Perceived continuation

The exact seed should feel like a piece of a larger town. Soft-envelope roofscape, sound, continuing street alignment and distant activity may support that read, but may never be counted as playable topology.

### PD-14 — Villa density hypothesis

The ordinary fabric should read as a compact Cantabrian market town rather than a scattered hamlet. Candidate metrics include:

- continuous street walls in the core;
- closed or near-closed blocks where appropriate;
- narrow frontage rhythm;
- a stepped storey gradient;
- visible continuation beyond seed seams.

Any frontage ratio, lot-width band, house count or storey target remains an **illustrative hypothesis** until its causal owner accepts it and CITY-04 can falsify it physically.

## 3. Candidate amendment package

None of P1–P7 is current truth.

### P1 — Civic L1 landmark at F02

Candidate: give the accepted `loc.plaza.ayuntamiento` / F02 civic anchor a stronger vertical silhouette so it can serve as an L1 orientation cue.

Desired evidence after adoption: sightlines from the old bridge, landing-side area, X5 approach and commercial seam.

**Owner impact before adoption:**
- CITY-05: reviewed one-off exterior/landmark posture;
- CITY-06 only if interior-depth/top-access implications change;
- CITY-03 only if the exact retained massing/site commitment changes materially.

### P2 — Represented paseo segment + market stairs

Candidate: represent a seed-local portion of the already accepted low paseo/sirga alignment and add a public stepped connector from that paseo toward the plaza/market level.

Purpose: create a more learnable low route and, if the final accepted graph supports it, genuine cycles rather than spokes.

**Owner impact before adoption:**
- CITY-01: edge/junction/access ledger;
- CITY-03: represented-edge set, exact walkway authorization and geometry inside the retained seed;
- CITY-05: stair/junction family only if existing grammar cannot realize the accepted relation.

Until those owners change, CITY-04 must not build or count this route.

### P3 — Covered passage and one-way drop candidates

Candidate A: a covered public passage under ordinary fabric connecting two already nearby public areas.  
Candidate B: a one-way drop/gate relation from the upper route toward the paseo, with explicit state/access semantics.

Purpose: route learning and earned shortcuts.

**Owner impact before adoption:**
- CITY-01: new graph edge(s), direction/state/access;
- CITY-03: exact represented geometry;
- CITY-05: covered-passage / retaining-edge assembly if needed.

### P4 — Seam payoffs

Candidate payoffs that do not themselves create topology:

| Current seam / terminus | Candidate payoff |
|---|---|
| `W.LANDING` | confluence view + seating / crossing-read cue |
| `E.X5` receiving stub | make the low-water state visually legible; do not imply another crossing |
| `O.X1` receiving stub | framed town view and ordinary far-bank activity |
| W.SHOP commercial cut | visible continuation of street life beyond the hard seed |
| future low-route seam | visible/sound continuation toward Ribera without counting it as playable |

**Owner impact:** CITY-03 for exact site/seam commitments; CITY-05 for physical families; CITY-02 only if a payoff becomes a programmed place rather than ambient structure.

### P5 — Bolera del Puente candidate

Candidate: a small ordinary `corro de bolos` / open play ground on dry Orilla-sur land near the X1 far side, used as a memorable far-bank destination and view point.

This is ordinary civic/cultural space. It does not create a martial-discovery commitment, gameplay system or runtime event.

**Owner impact before adoption:**
- CITY-02: new programmed place if promoted beyond ambient fabric;
- CITY-03: exact open-site region;
- CITY-05: site/exterior realization if the existing grammar is insufficient.

### P6 — Mirador de la Cuesta candidate

Candidate: a small widening/rest point on the Cuesta with a controlled view toward the confluence and lower route.

**Owner impact before adoption:** CITY-03 for exact retained geometry/site commitment; CITY-02 only if promoted to a programmed place.

### P7 — Stage map candidate

After any required owner amendments, candidate mappings could include:

| Stage type | Candidate location |
|---|---|
| Confrontation | old-bridge span/approach |
| Plaza/crowd | plaza/market edge |
| Chase | Cuesta and accepted level-change connectors |
| Follow/search | accepted Casco branching plus any later accepted passage |
| Conversation | shared-court / bar-edge / landing-side nook where access permits |
| Observation | accepted civic landmark or Cuesta mirador if adopted |
| Practice/play | bolera if adopted |
| Home base | existing bar anchor |

A stage map is not permission to implement gameplay.

## 5. CITY-04 observations valid on the current exact seed

These can be discussed without changing the current CITY-04 contract. They are **not added required runs by this proposal**.

1. Record where a first-time tester can and cannot orient without a map.
2. Measure player traversal time separately from CITY-01 planning weights while retaining both values and labels.
3. Record the longest stretch with no new spatial cue or choice.
4. Record which termini feel like intentional expansion seams versus accidental dead ends.
5. Check whether existing public/service/private thresholds are readable at greybox fidelity.
6. Record view opportunities from the old bridge, landing side and existing elevation changes.
7. Close X5 and record the **expected disconnection of `E.X5`**; do not invent or traverse an alternate route outside the seed.
8. Record whether soft-envelope treatment makes the seed feel like part of a larger city without counting that envelope as playable.

These observations may motivate later amendments. They do not pre-authorize them.

## 6. Checks that are invalid until a predecessor amendment is accepted

Do **not** add these to CITY-04 acceptance while the current exact seed remains unchanged:

- requiring three city-scale loops;
- requiring every public anchor to sit on a loop;
- walking an X5 detour that leaves the exact retained seed;
- timing the proposed paseo, stairs, covered passage or one-way drop;
- requiring the Bolera del Puente as a playable destination;
- requiring sightlines from a tower that has not been accepted by its owner;
- requiring five specific postcard cameras whose supporting geometry is not yet accepted;
- requiring quantified closed-block frontage or house-count targets not yet owned by CITY-03/05.

After an accepted amendment, the amended owner defines which of these become binding and how CITY-04 consumes them.

## 7. Candidate post-amendment hypotheses

If the causal owners later adopt the relevant structure, the following are useful hypotheses to falsify, not promises:

- multiple genuine graph cycles through the central seed;
- at least one memorable L1 orientation cue;
- no accidental seam that reads as an unfinished level edge;
- a separate measured-player-time table for represented routes;
- consistent threshold readability at ordinary play distance;
- several protected composed views;
- compact continuous ordinary fabric that reads as a villa rather than isolated houses;
- stage-shaped spaces distributed selectively rather than turning every street into a set piece.

Exact counts and thresholds should be introduced only when an owner can justify them against physical scale and production cost.
