## Purpose

H0 is not merely historical context. Its accepted guarantees are the base H1 consumes, and its failed candidates expose the failure modes most likely to recur at the engine boundary. This retrospective records the causal lessons that shape the H1 architecture and workpack split.

## Required lessons

### 1. Causal ownership

`WP-HK-10` initially introduced public thrown-handler semantics from a closure WP. Review correctly rejected the ownership even though the behavior was useful. The accepted repair reopened `WP-HK-01`, the dispatcher/error owner, and made HK10 consume that guarantee.

H1 consequence: closure and parity workpacks may expose omissions but may not invent bridge, identity, synchronization or product semantics to pass. A gap returns to the workpack that owns the relevant contract.

### 2. Contract inheritance

Accepted guarantees compose forward. HK07B consumed HK07A's neutral projection contract; HK08B consumed HK04/HK05/HK06 lineage and mutation authority; HK09B consumed HK07A framing and HK08 interaction shapes. Re-proving an accepted predecessor without concrete contradictory evidence wastes proof budget and obscures ownership.

H1 consequence: each WP names the exact H0/H1 guarantees it consumes, the delta it owns, and the concrete evidence that alone may reopen a predecessor.

### 3. Correct split

The original HK06/HK07 and HK08/HK09 umbrellas mixed claims with different authorities and proof methods. Splitting journal, snapshot/rebase, replay, reference transport, MCP, interaction primitives, conflict recovery, host authority and resource integrity made failures attributable and acceptance composable.

H1 consequence: toolchain, bridge contract, scoped producer, host authority, catalogue identity, scene materialization, prefab/asset realization, component realization, validation, reconciliation, reconstruction and the final gate are separate only where each has an independently falsifiable claim. Functions and test classes are not workpacks.

### 4. Contract versioning

HK08A changed `authoring.journal.read@1.0` from complete to paged semantics without changing the version. The accepted repair restored v1 and introduced v2.

H1 consequence: Unity binding schemas, catalogue snapshots, bridge plans, observation digests and receipts are versioned contracts. A public semantic change requires compatible evolution or a new major version; moving the regression oracle with the implementation is not compatibility evidence.

### 5. Representativity

HK08B's abstract benchmark was useful but could not replace the mandatory Juego2 content-shape probe. HK08A also found that a toy batch ceiling hid the representative 96-operation shape.

H1 consequence: unit fixtures may prove local mechanics, but scene/prefab/asset/component claims use a bounded Potes/Liebana slice. The final parity slice includes hierarchy, references, prefabs, materials, components and animation; it is not only a cube scene. A probe remains an omission detector, never a completeness oracle.

### 6. Effective path and policy seams

HK09A originally enforced host policy only in the normal production composer. A valid alternate composition-to-projection path skipped the guarantee. The accepted repair enforced policy at the effective neutral-projection seam.

H1 consequence: Unity host authority is checked where every Unity projection crosses from canonical composition into editor/project effects. Tests of a helper class or the preferred entrypoint alone are insufficient.

### 7. Do not redefine predecessors

HK09B initially widened frozen JSONL v1 framing and changed its error code, then changed the inherited test to agree. Review correctly required the new neutral envelope to fit below the accepted transport contract.

H1 consequence: Unity constraints do not silently redefine H0 capability identity, validation, transaction, provenance, snapshot, replay, recovery or transport semantics. A real incompatibility triggers an explicit versioned owner change.

### 8. Proof universe

HK-GATE initially reconciled only broad residual categories while claiming the complete HK10 universe, and its omission control protected a label rather than the actual execution. Earlier H0 work likewise showed that registries cannot prove their own completeness.

H1 consequence: catalogue completeness, managed-artifact coverage, component adapter coverage, gate stages and residual reconciliation use an independently enumerated or effective universe. Removing a real asset, component adapter or gate execution may not make its proof obligation disappear.

### 9. Proof budget

HK05's two failures in one causal class triggered a re-audit. The correct result was dependency-local ambiguity handling, not a growing set of special cases. HK10 kept twelve causal controls rather than an unbounded mutation collection.

H1 consequence: each negative control maps to a realistic false-green class in the WP claim. Two failures in the same class or two proof-growth cycles without product progress trigger re-audit.

### 10. Gates and trials

The HK-GATE fresh AI trial was appropriate because the gate owned the new claim that a fresh public client could use the whole H0 surface. It would have been wasteful in every predecessor.

H1 consequence: ordinary WPs use deterministic proof. One fresh independent AI-agent trial belongs at `WP-H1-GATE`, where the new claim is public discovery and operation of the Unity bridge. It complements rather than replaces the deterministic parity scenario.

## Additional material lessons from real H0 repairs

- **Optional values need injective identity encoding.** HK02A's global-vs-`"-"` collision shows that nullable engine/catalogue identities and fingerprints require explicit discriminants and length framing.
- **Local uncertainty must remain local.** HK05's first repair hid unrelated diagnostics. Unity ambiguity in one object, asset or component must not suppress independent diagnostics elsewhere.
- **Adapter constraints stay adapter-local.** HK07B's MCP name ceiling was solved with transport-local handles while preserving canonical identity. Unity paths, GUIDs, file IDs and `GlobalObjectId` are likewise bridge locators, not canonical IDs.
- **Authorities must tell the truth.** HK06B distinguished canonical rebase from canonical mutation; HK06C distinguished replay. H1 similarly distinguishes canonical mutation, Unity materialization, Unity observation and import proposal instead of calling them one transaction.
- **Publication evidence must match the claimed durability level.** HK09B proved process-local staged publication without claiming WAL/power-loss durability. H1 must state whether a generation is only editor-process safe, restart-recoverable or durable, and test only the claimed boundary.
- **The actual consumer decides the oracle.** HK06A's normalized replay envelope and HK-GATE's real stage omission both failed when evidence checked declarations rather than effective behavior. H1 parity compares normalized Unity observation against the bridge plan actually materialized.

## H1 planning rule distilled

For every H1 claim, identify one semantic owner, one effective seam, one independently bounded proof universe and one explicit residual boundary. If those four cannot be stated cleanly, the workpack is not ready to implement.
