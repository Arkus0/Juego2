Prove the **minimum district-residency model** needed by actual play: active local NPCs are fully realized through Unity/GC2, while at least one recurring routine-bearing NPC can leave the active residency set, advance through an abstract schedule/POI state, and later re-materialize at a valid place/time with identity and consequential state intact. If the player follows through a visible seam, continuity must remain legible instead of visibly teleporting the actor.

Routine coverage and systemic depth are separate: a Tier-C person may have a lightweight recurring schedule without receiving bespoke memory/history. The whole-town planning target of roughly 60–100 routine-bearing NPCs is **not** a requirement to load or prove that count here.
