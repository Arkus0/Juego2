# CITY-08 supersession amendment — move duplicate authoring proof to H2-GATE

Status: **PROPOSED / PROCESS_ONLY**  
Date: 2026-09-25

## Decision

`WP-CITY-08 — LOCAL Arkus authoring proof + reuse closure` is superseded as an executable CITY workpack after this amendment is accepted.

Its useful acceptance claim is not deleted. It is transferred once to `WP-H2-GATE`, where the same public authoring flow can be judged on the final H2 keeper/visual/occupied slice rather than on an earlier duplicate fixture.

Transferred claims:

- fresh capable author uses accepted public surfaces rather than source/hidden registries;
- composition metadata/dependencies are discoverable;
- missing dependencies fail visibly;
- bounded correction is inspectable;
- at least one reuse/variant preserves constraints;
- reuse has observable lower authoring effort than first legal assembly.

## Why

The reviewed H2 direction already requires:

`inspect -> discover -> semantic edit -> validate/apply -> Unity materialize -> inspect/play -> reconcile`

Running CITY-08 before H2 and then running the same class of authoring proof again at H2-GATE creates review/evidence duplication without a separate authority boundary.

CITY therefore closes its execution spine at `WP-CITY-07` keeper realization. H2 owns the later integrated authoring/reuse benchmark.

## Non-claims

This amendment does not make CITY-07 optional, weaken CITY constraints, pre-accept H2 authoring capability, or move CITY geography authority into H2.
