## Purpose

Reduce duplicated implementation and proof work in the remaining CITY execution chain without weakening product-design quality, removing any acceptance criterion, changing the CITY DAG, redefining accepted CITY semantics, or reopening accepted H1/CITY work.

The amendment applies the same causal-ownership discipline already accepted for the remaining H1 chain: each workpack owns only its genuinely new claim; accepted predecessor guarantees are consumed through their public/evidenced interfaces; concrete contradictions reopen the causal predecessor rather than spawning a duplicate local mechanism.

This amendment does **not** shorten or rewrite accepted design knowledge in CITY-00/01/02/03/05/06/09. Product specificity, spatial intent, environment grammar, interior/discovery intent and the accepted play-design proposal package remain intact.

## Binding compression rule

For `WP-CITY-04`, `WP-CITY-07` and `WP-CITY-08`:

- an accepted predecessor guarantee is `CONSUMED / INTEGRATION_ONLY` unless the current workpack introduces a genuinely new semantic/product claim over it;
- the Worker must not create a parallel bridge, catalogue, canonical-authority path, validation oracle, discovery registry, authoring transaction path or duplicate CITY semantic source merely to make local evidence easier;
- a direct falsification of the current workpack's owned claim is repaired in the current workpack;
- evidence that an accepted predecessor guarantee is false or inapplicable is `PREDECESSOR_CONTRADICTION` and routes back to that owner;
- runner, workflow, capture, logging, checkout, unavailable tooling or similar non-semantic execution failures are `EVIDENCE_OR_INFRA_BLOCKED`: they may prevent PASS until the bounded proof runs, but they do not justify speculative product/bridge redesign.

A consumed guarantee still has to be exercised where the current integration scenario depends on it. `INTEGRATION_ONLY` means "use and observe the accepted capability", never "skip the integration".

## WP-CITY-04 — compressed ownership

CITY-04 owns the first **physical falsification** of the exact accepted CITY-03 retained seed at greybox fidelity.

Its complete new claim is:

1. the exact CITY-03 seed can be realized as the bounded greybox without silently changing CITY semantics;
2. the required human traversal runs and measurements are executed on that exact represented seed;
3. scale, route choice, sightlines, elevation/crossings/access, quiet/busy contrast and expansion seams receive a truthful physical verdict;
4. observed deviations are recorded and routed to the causal CITY owner rather than locally improvised away;
5. the resulting PASS/REVISE verdict truthfully states whether the structure is worth carrying into keeper realization.

Consumed / integration-only:

- CITY-00 constitutional facts;
- CITY-01 mobility hypotheses and planning weights;
- CITY-02 place/depth/access promises;
- CITY-03 exact retained-seed topology/envelope/seams;
- CITY-05 street/parcel/building grammar;
- CITY-06 interior/access/discovery placement constraints;
- accepted H1 scene/prefab/component/diagnostic capabilities available after the declared H1 prerequisite.

CITY-04 must not become a second H1 bridge acceptance campaign, a second CITY topology owner, or an implementation of Living World/gameplay semantics.

### Minimum sufficient CITY-04 proof

PASS still requires the existing required human runs, walk-time/route evidence, captures/notes, deviation ledger and every existing acceptance criterion. The proof may rely on accepted H1 machinery for realization/inspection; it need not independently re-prove generic bridge/catalogue/component/diagnostic correctness.

A bridge or catalogue defect discovered while realizing the greybox is not a CITY-04 semantic defect unless CITY-04 itself supplied the wrong CITY intent. It is routed to the accepted bridge/catalogue owner and may block CITY-04 completion until repaired upstream.

## Failure routing

For the three amended workpacks, classify before repairing:

### `CITY_CURRENT_OWNED_FAIL`

Evidence directly falsifies the current workpack's bounded new claim as listed above. Repair remains in that CITY workpack.

### `PREDECESSOR_CONTRADICTION`

Evidence shows an accepted CITY/H1/H0/asset/catalogue guarantee consumed by the scenario is false or inapplicable. Reopen or repair the causal owner under its existing rules; do not duplicate the mechanism inside CITY.

### `EVIDENCE_OR_INFRA_BLOCKED`

The required bounded proof did not execute or persist because of runner/tooling/workflow/capture/logging/checkout or another non-semantic execution failure. This can prevent PASS but does not establish a product-design or keeper-authoring defect.

## Resulting remaining CITY chain

```text
CITY-04  exact-seed greybox + physical falsification
  -> CITY-07  keeper-realization fidelity
  -> CITY-08  public-authoring reuse / cost closure
```

The chain remains deliberately strong: paper design is physically falsified, the validated structure becomes keeper, then the keeper slice is used to prove that accepted public authoring surfaces make further city production materially cheaper. The compression removes redundant ownership and duplicated proof, not product quality or falsification pressure.
