## Claim and work

Realize one actual keeper block of the large port town / villa portuaria from the accepted URBAN-00 brief and ART-URBAN-01 keeper-ready kit. Reuse admitted CITY-07/H2/ART assembly and gameplay surfaces where applicable; independently validate new coast/topology, accessible route, measured scale/elevation, commercial threshold/interior and playable follow/chase path.

Provide named slots for a witness, shop/activity, clue and return-state presentation, but do not implement the later investigation/living-world systems merely to fill them.

Retain the block in the shipping world and mark which facades/interiors are functional. Establish the concrete district/neighbourhood residency seam used by the later living-block proof: what geometry and NPC presentation remain active near an exit, what can unload, and how a recurring actor can cross or be re-materialized without a visible continuity break. This WP does not need to prove final multi-neighbourhood streaming or whole-town population.

## Pre-H2-GATE role

This WP is deliberately moved **before** `WP-H2-GATE`. The expanded visual-production Gate must inspect the actual final-setting block rather than approving only the historical inland pilot. This does not pull persistent NPC behaviour, investigation or combat before H2-GATE; it only moves the physical/visual keeper block needed to lock the real product look.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

**Mandatory evidence:** reviewed block geometry/route/access measurements, continuous third-person walk-through and threshold, visual comparison against accepted port-town ART direction with `KEEPER_READY` coverage, named future gameplay anchors, residency/seam note and first edit/reuse record.

**Evaluation:** physical checks plus owner visual/play inspection.

**FAIL if:** pilot Liébana geometry is passed off as coast; dressing hides proxy boxes; required access or pursuit route fails; CITY semantics change without named owner review; the block's scale reads as an unintended major-city environment; or the block becomes a hidden gameplay-system implementation just because future anchors are named.

**Allowed residuals:** adjacent blocks, later neighbourhoods, final town population and the actual witness/shop/clue systemic behavior. Retained visual gaps required for the first production lock are blockers, not residuals.
