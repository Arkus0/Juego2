## Objective

Choose the spatial constitution of the final keeper city before local scene production: overall size band, district families, river/port/rural structure, expansion directions and the rules that make the city large enough to feel like a place without diluting reactivity.

This WP answers **what city are we making**, not how Unity will render it.

## Starting hypotheses to challenge

- final dense urban fabric may land around 0.8–1.2 km²;
- total playable envelope with river/roads/rural edge may land around 1.5–2.5 km²;
- a representative cross-city walk may feel right around 12–18 minutes;
- 6–8 district families may be enough to create genuine spatial/social contrast;
- the first retained seed may be around 0.10–0.15 km².

The Worker MUST treat these as falsifiable planning hypotheses, not numbers to rationalise after the fact.

## Required spatial ingredients

The selected constitution must explicitly place and relate:

1. old quarter / historic lanes;
2. civic core / town hall / plaza-market family;
3. commercial everyday spine;
4. at least one substantial residential family beyond the old quarter;
5. river corridor and waterfront paths;
6. old bridge and future crossing strategy;
7. **fluvial-port / wharf-storage-work district** sized to the fictional setting;
8. workshops/service/peripheral edge;
9. outward road/bus/valley connections;
10. rural edge: paths, slopes, huertas/green transition;
11. expansion seams for later production;
12. at least one intentionally quiet spatial family.

## Work

1. Reconcile the current Production Blueprint topology with the newer city-size/port direction.
2. Produce at least **three materially different city topologies**, not cosmetic variants.
3. For each option, evaluate:
   - route loops vs bottlenecks;
   - district identity;
   - river/crossing logic;
   - port plausibility and usefulness;
   - expansion cost;
   - likely reactive-location density;
   - actor schedule/travel plausibility;
   - player follow/search/investigation value;
   - mayoral access/routing consequences;
   - ability to preserve a retained product seed.
4. Select one preferred constitution and record rejected alternatives + reasons.
5. Define a scale envelope and explicit conditions that would shrink/expand it later.
6. Identify what is playable fabric versus scenic/backdrop envelope.

## Deliverables

- `Docs/production/CITY_SPATIAL_CONSTITUTION.md`
- selected semantic district graph;
- alternative-topology comparison matrix;
- scale/travel-time hypothesis table;
- list of permanent spatial invariants;
- list of unresolved questions deferred to later CITY work.

## Acceptance

- ≥3 genuine topology alternatives compared.
- Selected topology contains loops and cannot be described as one hub with spokes.
- River shapes movement rather than functioning only as scenery.
- Port has a gameplay/material/social reason to exist and a setting-appropriate scale.
- Old quarter, civic/commercial, residential, port/work edge and rural edge are spatially distinct.
- At least two credible outward/expansion directions exist.
- Long-route scale is sufficient that an actor can genuinely be “elsewhere in town”.
- Selected size is justified through density/travel/content cost, not comparison vanity.
- Demo/product seed can occupy a real retained part of the selected constitution.
- No Unity scene, asset import or runtime contract is created.

## Negative gates

FAIL if the selected plan:

- is effectively four streets plus scenery;
- chases GTA/Kuttenberg acreage without a reactive-density argument;
- puts every meaningful route through the plaza;
- uses the river/port as decorative labels only;
- requires demolishing/replacing the first serious demo district to reach final form;
- pre-decides implementation authority that belongs to H1/H2 review.

## Definition of Done

The city has a selected, reviewable spatial constitution and scale envelope strong enough for downstream CITY work to calculate movement/topology without inventing a different city.
