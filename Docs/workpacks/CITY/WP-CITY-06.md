## Objective

Turn CITY-02 interior priorities and CITY-05 shells into selective interior/access/discovery patterns. Give important places second and third layers of meaning without requiring Rockstar/Larian quantities of bespoke hidden rooms or stealing runtime semantics from Living World.

## Discovery layers

Use selectively, not as a checklist for every building:

- public surface;
- private/semi-private space;
- service/back-of-house routes;
- vertical spaces such as upper floors/roofs where useful;
- basements/storage/rear courts where useful;
- social/temporal discovery;
- institutional/historical discovery;
- rare extraordinary/cultural discovery.

## Secret/discovery classes

- **Authored:** a deliberately designed place, object or fact.
- **Systemic:** emerges from schedules, access, relationships, knowledge, material state or consequences owned by later systems.
- **Hybrid:** authored substrate with multiple truthful systemic routes to discovery.

Hybrid discovery is preferred for important discoveries when the relevant later systems can genuinely support it.

## Acceptance

- Every major district can support a meaningful second layer beyond superficial traversal, but not necessarily an underground/roof layer.
- Important places can be discovered/recontextualised through more than one truthful route where later systems allow.
- The city remains coherent if extraordinary/martial material is completely missed.
- Ordinary homes/workplaces remain valuable; “secret” does not mean only treasure room.
- Detailed interior promises remain compatible with CITY-02 S-depth budgets and CITY-05 shells.
- No quest scripting, dialogue, NPC belief semantics or runtime implementation is authored here.

## Definition of Done

`CITY-03` can compare retained-seed candidates using reviewed shell, interior-depth and discovery requirements instead of choosing a seed first and inventing its content afterward.

## Negative gates

FAIL if every building needs a secret, hidden-room quantity is used as quality, systemic discovery is claimed without a real future causal owner, or martial/cinema material overwhelms the grounded Cantabrian-valley identity.
