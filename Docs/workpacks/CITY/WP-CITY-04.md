# WP-CITY-04 — LOCAL retained-seed greybox + traversal validation

Status: **FROZEN PLAN / DORMANT UNTIL PREREQUISITES**  
Class: PRODUCT / LOCAL ENGINE VALIDATION (NON-FOUNDATIONAL)  
Mode: **LOCAL / UNITY**  
Depends on: `WP-CITY-03` PASS + `WP-H1-08` PASS
Blocks: `WP-CITY-07` only

## Contract continuity

Accepted CITY-00 defers real measurements to `CITY-01/CITY-04`, and the pre-existing CITY plan assigns retained-seed blockout/validation to `CITY-04`. This WP preserves that meaning.

## Objective

Make the exact CITY-03 retained seed physically walkable in Unity at greybox fidelity and falsify paper assumptions about scale, route choice, sightlines, elevation, crossings, access and expansion seams before keeper art/content investment.

This is the first CITY WP permitted to create serious scene geometry.

## Scope

Greybox/blockout only unless already-adopted representative assets are explicitly within the accepted bridge/asset boundary at execution time. Unity realizes and measures the plan; it does not become the source of CITY semantics.

## Work

- build only the bounded CITY-03 greybox;
- measure CITY-01 representative route times and alternates;
- validate slopes/stairs/bridge widths/river separation and landmark visibility;
- validate follow/search branching and service-route readability;
- validate CITY-02 depth/access promises at blockout scale without implementing Living World behaviour;
- validate CITY-05 parcel/building envelopes and CITY-06 interior/exterior transition placement where represented;
- test expansion-seam physical plausibility and perceived city continuation;
- record where paper assumptions fail and route each failure back to its causal owner rather than silently improvising.

## Required human runs

At minimum:

1. cross the seed casually without objective;
2. follow a proxy actor taking route A;
3. repeat with route B;
4. travel bridge ↔ bar ↔ market/plaza;
5. approach/leave via future port seam;
6. approach/leave via residential/rural seam;
7. test one blocked/changing route and alternate path;
8. compare quiet and busy spatial zones;
9. test one planned service/private threshold at greybox fidelity.

## Deliverables

- measured walk-time table tied back to CITY-01 hypotheses;
- screenshots/overhead blockout captures;
- human traversal notes;
- route/sightline/scale defects;
- owner-tagged deviation ledger (`CITY-01`, `02`, `05`, `06`, or `03`);
- retained-seed **PASS / REVISE** verdict.

## Acceptance

- Measured travel remains inside product intent or deviations are explicitly routed for revision.
- The seed feels like a piece of a larger city rather than an enclosed test level.
- At least one meaningful route choice is perceptible without a map overlay.
- Old bridge/river geometry genuinely affects movement.
- Port and rural/residential seams read as plausible continuations.
- Quiet/busy spatial contrast is visible.
- No major retained street/POI placement requires foreseeable demolition to expand.
- No geometry is treated as keeper solely because it exists in Unity.

## Failure response

Ordinary greybox failures reopen the downstream owner that produced the falsified hypothesis. If physical evidence appears to contradict an accepted CITY-00 fact, STOP and raise a separate explicit predecessor-amendment proposal; CITY-04 cannot reinterpret CITY-00 locally.

## Definition of Done

The exact seed has a measured spatial verdict and an owner-tagged defect ledger. `CITY-07` receives a validated structure worth realizing, or the programme returns to the specific downstream WP whose hypothesis failed.

## Forbidden before prerequisites

Do not begin before `WP-CITY-03` and `WP-H1-08` have each passed, merged and completed DocSync. This bounded greybox may consume accepted H1 scene/prefab/component/diagnostic surfaces, but it may not become H1 acceptance evidence by implication. No final art/polish, broad asset production, keeper realization, Living World/gameplay implementation or new bridge semantics.
