## Purpose

Clarify that `WP-CITY-07` is not a literal greybox-to-assets transcription pass.

CITY-04 owns physical falsification of the exact accepted seed. Its greybox may legitimately contain blockout-only proxy decomposition: road slabs, curb volumes, parcel blocks, retaining masses, temporary boxes and other overlapping primitives used to make planning geometry measurable. Passing CITY-04 proves that the accepted seed is physically viable; it does not make every primitive or exact local coordinate a shipping asset decision.

CITY-07 must convert that validated structure into the first keeper **third-person game-space** of the shipping city.

## Precedence and compatibility

This amendment is narrower and later than `CITY_REMAINING_COMPRESSION_AMENDMENT.md`.

It does not repeal the compression rule, change the CITY DAG or reopen accepted CITY-00/01/02/03/05/06/09 or H1 semantics. It only clarifies the CITY-07 phrase “preserve the CITY-04 validated structure”.

For CITY-07, **preserve structure means preserve accepted semantic structure and validated physical conclusions, not literal greybox primitive decomposition or exact local blockout coordinates**.

If this amendment conflicts with an interpretation of the earlier compression text that would require literal greybox geometry to survive into the keeper, this amendment controls for CITY-07 only.

## What remains invariant

CITY-07 must preserve, unless the causal owner is explicitly amended:

- CITY-00 geography and crossing truth;

- CITY-02 programmed places, depth/access obligations and ordinary/quiet reserve;
- CITY-03 hard playable envelope, represented-edge set, masks/overlays, retained sites, expansion seams and retained/temporary declarations;

- ART/SETTING/VISUAL_BIBLE authority;

CITY-07 may not use “better level design” as a reason to weaken any of those guarantees silently.

## New CITY-07-owned claim

CITY-07 owns the **keeper game-space realization** of the validated seed.

That claim has two simultaneous obligations:

1. semantic fidelity to the accepted CITY structure; and
2. intentional embodied spatial composition for third-person play.

A keeper realization is insufficient if it is semantically correct but merely reproduces the greybox as a flat, visibly layered planning model with real textures/assets placed on top.

## Authorized local realization freedom

Within accepted semantic envelopes and CITY-05 planning bands, CITY-07 may choose final local geometry rather than copy blockout geometry literally.

This includes authority to:

- consolidate multiple blockout road/curb/ground/parcel proxies into a coherent retained street assembly;
- delete temporary slabs/cubes/volumes that represented planning facts but are not needed in the keeper;
- remove accidental mesh overlap, coplanar surfaces, z-fighting, duplicate collision and duplicate traversable surfaces;
- select realized street width within the accepted family band;
- adjust local curb/wall/retaining placement, threshold depth, façade alignment, setback and junction shaping within accepted site/parcel constraints;
- articulate local grade, retaining, landings and level transitions consistently with the accepted elevation/access class;
- use irregularity, compression/expansion, occlusion/reveal, local pockets and framed approaches to improve third-person spatial rhythm;
- emphasize already accepted anchors/landmarks through sightline and composition without inventing a new site or changing their programme authority;
- protect readable public/service/private thresholds already owned by CITY-02/05/06;
- shape spatial support for future following, searching, conversation, observation, chase or confrontation situations without claiming those gameplay systems exist;
- use Unity-native terrain, meshes, prefabs, colliders and composition techniques appropriate to a shipping scene.

The key boundary is semantic: if the same accepted route, place, access, boundary and programme facts remain true, local keeper geometry may differ materially from greybox proxy form.

## Flatness and “urban plan” failure mode

CITY-07 must explicitly evaluate whether CITY-04's blockout reads at human third-person scale as an authored game-space rather than a top-down urban plan extruded into 3D.

The realization may use local vertical articulation where the accepted elevation class and CITY-05 slope/retaining grammar permit it. It may also use building setback, frontage rhythm, junction irregularity, wall/edge placement, view framing and surface consolidation to create spatial cadence.

It may **not** invent a hill, stair route, shortcut or new level relation that would change the accepted route/elevation/access semantics.

Therefore “less flat” means richer lawful realization inside the accepted spatial grammar, not arbitrary terrain deformation.

## Minimum proof

CITY-07 PASS must include a bounded representative chain that proves the distinction between greybox validation and keeper game-space realization.

At minimum:

1. choose one representative street → building threshold → interior chain;
2. preserve and list the accepted semantic constraints governing it;
3. record the greybox representation before keeper transformation;
4. replace/consolidate the relevant proxy geometry with accepted assets/compositions;
5. remove accidental overlaps/coplanar stacks/duplicate collision on the representative traversable path;
6. perform a third-person traversal inspection focused on legibility, spatial cadence, sightlines, thresholds, level treatment and collision comfort;
7. show that any local geometry changes stay inside the accepted CITY-05 bands and do not mutate graph/site/access/boundary semantics;
8. record any desirable improvement that exceeds CITY-07 authority as an owner-tagged upstream amendment need rather than implementing it silently.

This representative chain is the minimum positive realization example. It is **not** sufficient evidence for material keeper changes elsewhere in the retained seed.

## Differential preservation of CITY-04 physical conclusions

Because CITY-07 may materially alter widths, junction shaping, slopes, retaining walls, setbacks, sightlines, threshold geometry and other local form, every material keeper geometry change must declare its possible effect on the physical conclusions inherited from CITY-04.

Maintain a keeper-change impact ledger. For every material change, record:

1. the changed element(s) and bounded spatial area;
2. the CITY-04 conclusion families that could plausibly be affected;
3. for each affected family, the bounded measurement or human inspection repeated on the keeper candidate and its result; or
4. for each unaffected family, a concrete invariant witness showing why the conclusion cannot have changed.

The impact review must at least consider CITY-04 conclusions about travel time and route choice/alternates; clear widths and crossing separation; grade/elevation/retaining treatment; sightlines and landmark/route/service readability; CITY-02 depth/access promises; CITY-05 parcel/building envelope, setback/frontage and clearance; CITY-06 thresholds/interior-exterior transitions; expansion seams and perceived continuation/scale; and quiet/busy spatial contrast where relevant.

Revalidation is **differential**, not a full replay. Repeat only the measurement or inspection that the changed geometry can affect. Related edits may be batched and checked once on the final keeper candidate. If no affected conclusion is claimed, the invariant witness must be strong enough to make that claim falsifiable — for example unchanged route/path domain, unchanged measured width/grade/separation, unchanged sightline occluder domain, unchanged threshold geometry or unchanged seam geometry.

Remaining inside a CITY-05 planning band is a necessary constraint but is not, by itself, proof that a CITY-04 conclusion remained true. A legal width change can still change travel behavior, a legal setback can still alter a sightline, and a legal wall/grade adjustment can still alter route readability or movement comfort.

CITY-07 cannot PASS while any material keeper geometry change lacks either affected-conclusion remeasurement/reinspection or a reviewable invariant witness. If the bounded check falsifies an accepted predecessor conclusion, route it to the causal owner. If accepted semantics remain true but CITY-07's own realization caused the failure, repair it in CITY-07.

## Failure routing

### `CITY07_GAME_SPACE_FAIL`

Use when accepted semantics are preserved but the keeper realization still behaves like an accidental blockout transcription: incoherent surface stacking, unresolved collision, unreadable thresholds, avoidable flat/monotonous composition, poor local sightline composition or other defects that CITY-07 is authorized to fix within existing constraints.

Repair in CITY-07.

### `UPSTREAM_SEMANTIC_CHANGE_REQUIRED`

Use when a desired fix requires changing graph connectivity, crossing truth, a programmed place/site, hard boundary, route/elevation/access class, clearance/access obligation or another accepted predecessor semantic.

Do not implement it locally. Route an explicit amendment to the causal owner.

### `PREDECESSOR_CONTRADICTION`

Use when effective evidence shows an accepted predecessor guarantee is false or inapplicable, rather than merely aesthetically/game-spatially suboptimal.

Route to the causal predecessor under the existing compression rule.

### `EVIDENCE_OR_INFRA_BLOCKED`

Use for runner/tooling/workflow/capture/logging/checkout failure. This may block PASS but does not authorize semantic redesign.

## Negative gates

FAIL if CITY-07:

- treats every CITY-04 cube/slab/surface as keeper geometry merely because the greybox passed;
- keeps accidental stacked road/ground/collider geometry hidden beneath final assets;
- uses polish to conceal z-fighting, duplicate traversable surfaces or collision contradiction;
- lets a material keeper geometry change outside the representative chain escape CITY-04 conclusion-impact classification;
- assumes an affected CITY-04 conclusion remains true solely because the changed geometry is still inside an accepted CITY-05 band;
- changes a route, crossing, site, hard boundary, access role or elevation class without upstream authority;
- adopts a CITY-09 structural proposal by implication;
- turns Unity scene state into CITY semantic authority;
- or claims gameplay/Living World semantics merely because the space was shaped to support them later.

## Result

The remaining CITY chain becomes:

```text
CITY-04  exact-seed greybox + physical falsification
  -> CITY-07  semantic-faithful keeper game-space realization + differential preservation of affected physical conclusions
  -> CITY-08  public-authoring reuse / cost closure
```

CITY-04 tells us whether the planned seed physically works. CITY-07 makes that valid seed worth shipping and playing in third person while proving that its lawful local tuning did not silently invalidate the physical conclusions it inherited. CITY-08 then proves that further production over the keeper can be authored efficiently through the accepted public stack.
