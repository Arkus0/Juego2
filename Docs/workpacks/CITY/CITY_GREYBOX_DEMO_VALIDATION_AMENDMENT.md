# CITY greybox-to-demo validation allocation amendment

Status: CANDIDATE — effective only after independent review, merge and DocSync
Class: PROCESS / WORKPACK-CONTRACT AMENDMENT
Affected workpacks: `WP-CITY-04`, `WP-CITY-07`, `WP-CITY-08`

## Reason for the change

On 2026-09-25 the human directing CITY-04 traversed the Unity greybox and reported that its underlying structure looked promising and walking felt comfortable, but the cube-only scene was too visually ambiguous to judge travel times or the lived scale of the town. The earlier 1.4 m/s probe speed felt too slow. The seed appeared small and sparse if mistaken for the whole town. The human explicitly declined further timed greybox playtesting and requested a thorough test of a real, asset-rich demo in CITY-07/08.

The accepted CITY-03 source defines `seed.confluence_civic_commercial` as the **first bounded retained piece** of the shipping city, not the entire town. Its eight F01–F08 retained frontage slots and five expansion seams are not a completeness claim about the future city. The owner's small/sparse impression remains a concrete demo question: the realized seed must read as a useful first district with credible continuation.

## Revised ownership and gate meaning

### CITY-04 — exact greybox scene handoff

CITY-04 owns construction of the exact CITY-03 seed as a playable Unity greybox, effective collider/crossing/land-water checks, reproducible scene/capture evidence, and an owner-tagged ledger of any **observed** structural or projection deviations. It may record informal human impressions, but no human timing or nine-run scenario campaign is a CITY-04 acceptance gate. `CITY-04 PASS` means the bounded scene is a faithful, inspectable technical handoff for later demo realization; it does **not** mean the travel hypotheses, full perceptual route choice, town scale, density, access clarity or expansion feel have passed human evaluation. No geometry becomes keeper merely because CITY-04 passes.

The CITY-04 movement controller is a local traversal probe, not a canonical gameplay-speed decision. Its default changes to 3.5 m/s, with Shift at 5.0 m/s, solely to make the scene easier to inspect. The old 1.4 m/s arithmetic references remain historical geometry comparisons, not measured human times.

### CITY-07 — asset-rich spatial verdict before keeper acceptance

After both CITY-04 PASS and H1-GATE PASS, CITY-07 realizes the seed as the accepted keeper game-space using admitted dependencies. Its separately accepted game-space authority remains intact: it may replace disposable proxy geometry and tune lawful local realization inside accepted CITY semantics rather than copying the greybox literally.

All thirteen CITY-03 §9 spatial proxy setups remain required on the **final asset-rich keeper candidate**; record an evidence mapping for `SCN-01..13` without claiming Living World runtime. Before CITY-07 can claim keeper acceptance, a human must also perform the nine traversal families formerly allocated to CITY-04 on that legible candidate: free crossing, follow route A, follow route B, bridge/bar/plaza travel, port seam, residential/rural seam, blocked route and alternate, quiet/busy contrast, and a service/private threshold. One observation can cover multiple setups if the evidence identifies each. The human need not be a developer or operate diagnostic-only controls; the Worker must present a usable demo and record observer, build SHA, speed settings, route conditions, times and notes.

Measure W04/W05/W06/W12/W13/X1 and available X5, plus the three representative concatenated trips specified by CITY-03. Record actual seconds and the movement-speed profile used; compare CITY-01 planning weights with explicit pace assumptions instead of treating 1.4 m/s arithmetic as a playtest. Inspect bridge/river separation, widths/grades/sightlines, route legibility, access thresholds, quiet/busy difference, expansion seams, and whether the seed feels too small or sparse even as a first district. Capture the scene, produce an owner-tagged deviation ledger and issue a **spatial PASS/REVISE** before the keeper-realization PASS. A failed CITY-03 topology/scale assumption routes to CITY-03; a realization or legibility defect owned by CITY-07 is repaired there. The CITY-07 keeper result cannot hide or waive the spatial verdict.

CITY-07's accepted keeper-change impact ledger remains binding. The human campaign above is the **single execution oracle** for the human/perceptual conclusion families it actually measures on the final keeper candidate, and each material keeper change must link to the scenario/timing/inspection that discharges its affected conclusions. Technical CITY-04 invariants or other conclusions outside that campaign still require the bounded technical recheck or invariant witness required by the CITY-07 game-space contract. Thus this amendment does not create a competing second validation system and does not allow material geometry changes after the human campaign to escape differential revalidation.

### CITY-08 — final demo regression during authoring proof

CITY-08 retains its fresh-agent public authoring/reuse claim. After the bounded correction and reuse/variant flow, provide the resulting demo for a further human play review of traversal, density, route choice and place readability. Record the human notes and route any regression to CITY-07 or the causal CITY owner. This does not duplicate all CITY-07 timed runs when the accepted movement geometry is unchanged; a material route/scale or movement change requires the affected measurement again.

## Exact supersession boundary

This amendment reallocates the **execution stage** of the CITY-01 §11 measurement/calibration handoff, CITY-03 §9 scenarios, CITY-03 §10 human route measurements/readability/sightlines, the current CITY-04 required human runs/timed deliverables/spatial PASS criterion, and the corresponding CITY-04/CITY-07 minimum-proof paragraphs in `CITY_REMAINING_COMPRESSION_AMENDMENT.md`. Those duties move to CITY-07 before keeper PASS, with a final demo regression in CITY-08. CITY-03 §10.2's exact boundary, water, dry-component, crossing and collision construction checks remain CITY-04 technical duties; CITY-07 judges their human readability after asset realization.

The accepted `CITY_07_GAME_SPACE_REALIZATION_AMENDMENT.md` remains authoritative for local keeper-geometry freedom and impact coverage. This amendment changes **when and where the first human spatial verdict is established**, not CITY-07's authority to produce a real third-person game-space inside accepted topology/site/access semantics. Where the two amendments overlap, the final CITY-07 demo campaign discharges the applicable human/perceptual impact-ledger obligations; technical invariants remain covered by bounded recheck or invariant witness.

The original CITY-01 and CITY-03 production specifications, `CITY_REMAINING_COMPRESSION_AMENDMENT.md`, and the accepted CITY-03 capsule remain historical proof sources; their CITY-04 measurement-executor wording is superseded by this reviewed amendment and must not be used alone to route current work. All other accepted CITY-00/01/02/03/05/06/09 semantics, CITY-03 polygons/masks/routes/sites/seams, route planning weights, and the dependency graph remain unchanged. Neither CITY-04 nor this amendment adds gameplay or keeper content ahead of H1-GATE. The H1-04 admitted Source slice is not broadened by this amendment; additional Quaternius imports must satisfy the applicable accepted asset boundary when CITY-07 executes.

Until this amendment is independently accepted, merged and DocSynced, the existing CITY-04 contract still requires the human runs and measured table. Owner preference or a preliminary impression alone cannot be reported as a current-contract CITY-04 PASS.
