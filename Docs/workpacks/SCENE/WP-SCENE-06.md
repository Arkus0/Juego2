# WP-SCENE-06 — LOCAL keeper-scene realization + Arkus authoring proof

Status: **PLANNED / DORMANT UNTIL PREREQUISITES**  
Class: PRODUCT / ENGINE VALIDATION (NON-FOUNDATIONAL)  
Mode: **LOCAL**

## Objective

Prove that the retained seed can actually be realized and iterated through the accepted Arkus/Unity authoring path with real representative assets, while preserving CITY topology and SCENE place semantics.

This is where the project tests the productivity claim. Earlier SCENE WPs only design the grammar.

## Required proof

1. Realize a bounded retained street/place slice with representative project assets.
2. Use public/discoverable Arkus authoring surfaces for at least one meaningful composition/change rather than direct hidden bridge scripting.
3. Prove the agent can discover relevant composition metadata without reading implementation source.
4. Realize at least one S3 interior/functional place and one S1/S2 neighbouring fabric example.
5. Validate entrances, bounds, collision/navigation assumptions, presentation bindings and missing-reference diagnostics.
6. Inspect realized result in Unity and record required corrections back into authored composition data rather than silently fixing only the scene.
7. Demonstrate reuse by instantiating/adapting an accepted composition a second time without rebuilding it module-by-module.

## Acceptance

- The first keeper slice is visibly part of the final town, not a test arena.
- Authoring truth and Unity realization can be reconciled without Unity becoming semantic authority.
- At least one composition is reused with materially less authoring work than its first construction.
- A missing/invalid asset/composition fails visibly.
- The result can be extended through CITY-03 expansion seams without moving keeper core geometry.
- No claim of 'automatic city generation' is made; the proof is constrained assisted authoring.
