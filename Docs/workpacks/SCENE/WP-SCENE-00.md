# WP-SCENE-00 — Place constitution + production-depth model

Status: **FROZEN PLAN / NOT_STARTED**  
Class: PRODUCT / SCENARIO PREPRODUCTION (NON-FOUNDATIONAL)  
Mode: **REMOTE**  
Depends on: `WP-CITY-00` PASS, `WP-ART-00` seed  
Blocks: amended `WP-CITY-01` start

## Objective

Freeze the vocabulary that later WPs use to discuss a playable place without confusing urban importance, visual importance, interior depth and systemic depth.

## Work

1. Define S0–S4 place-depth tiers and examples.
2. Define orthogonality between CITY location tiers and SCENE depth tiers.
3. Define place boundary vocabulary: street edge, parcel, shell, frontage, public entrance, service entrance, rear/patio, vertical connection, interior zone, threshold and expansion seam.
4. Define discovery-layer vocabulary: public, private, vertical/service, social/temporal, institutional/historical, extraordinary/cultural.
5. Define what a scene specification may require from ART, CITY, Living World and future Arkus catalogue discovery without claiming their authority.
6. Pre-register anti-scope rules for inaccessible fabric and interior promises.

## Deliverables

- `Docs/production/SCENE_PLACE_CONSTITUTION.md`
- place-depth matrix with ≥12 representative examples;
- ownership/input-output table;
- residual/deferred decisions ledger.

## Acceptance

- A reviewer can classify a bar, home, shop, warehouse, municipal office, alley, roof, courtyard and scenic building without ambiguity.
- S-depth does not imply CITY systemic tier or vice versa.
- The constitution allows a believable city where a substantial majority of fabric is S0/S1.
- At least one quiet residential S3 example exists to prove 'systemic' does not mean spectacle.
- Extraordinary/cultural depth is explicitly optional and non-universal.
- No Unity/catalogue authority/runtime implementation is introduced.

## Negative gates

FAIL if every important building is implicitly forced to be fully enterable, if visual hero status implies simulation depth, or if SCENE starts owning NPC behaviour or catalogue authority.
