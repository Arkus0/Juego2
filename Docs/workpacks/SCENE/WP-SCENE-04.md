# WP-SCENE-04 — Discovery, secrets + layered-city programme

Status: **FROZEN PLAN / NOT_STARTED**  
Class: PRODUCT / DISCOVERY PREPRODUCTION (NON-FOUNDATIONAL)  
Mode: **REMOTE**  
Depends on: `WP-SCENE-03` PASS, current Living World PA roadmap as non-binding input  
Blocks: amended `WP-CITY-03` start

## Objective

Make the city feel deeper than its acreage by planning physical, social, temporal, institutional and cultural discovery layers without pretending to author BG3/RDR2 quantities of bespoke content.

## Core distinction

- authored secret;
- systemic secret;
- hybrid secret.

Important secrets should prefer **multiple truthful discovery routes** when the underlying systems support them. A route is not valid merely because design wants redundancy; it must be owned by real access, knowledge, schedule, witness or spatial facts.

## Work

1. Produce district-by-layer discovery matrix.
2. Require every core district to have ≥1 credible non-surface discovery vector.
3. Require citywide representation of ≥4 discovery-layer families before CITY-03.
4. Define discovery-route patterns: follow, invitation, testimony, document, changed routine, service/rear access, observation, later consequence.
5. Define persistence/expiry expectations: some secrets are stable facts; some are opportunities that can move/disappear/change.
6. Define extraordinary/cultural strand rules. Martial arts, kung-fu lineage and Hong Kong/Chinese cinema may form a distinctive hidden layer, but cannot dominate every district or invalidate the ordinary Cantabrian setting.
7. Define anti-checklist and anti-collectible rules.
8. Define how player knowledge differs from world truth; future PA04/05/06 semantics remain authoritative.

## Deliverables

- `Docs/production/SCENE_DISCOVERY_PROGRAMME.md`
- district × discovery-layer matrix;
- authored/systemic/hybrid example bank;
- multi-route discovery pattern catalogue;
- extraordinary-cultural strand boundaries;
- discovery budget for the first retained seed.

## Acceptance

- At least one strong secret example requires no hidden room.
- At least one physical secret example requires no NPC exposition.
- At least one hybrid example can be discovered by ≥3 materially different truthful routes.
- The city remains coherent if the player misses the martial/cinema strand entirely.
- Important discoveries can affect access/knowledge/opportunity without becoming quest-marker omniscience.
- The plan explicitly protects ordinary quiet life from 'everything is a mystery' syndrome.

## Negative gates

FAIL for one-secret-per-building quotas, collectible-wallpaper design, universal kung-fu conspiracy, magic knowledge propagation, or secrets that require actors to behave irrationally merely to expose content.
