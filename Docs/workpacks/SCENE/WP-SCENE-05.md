# WP-SCENE-05 — Retained product-seed scenario specification

Status: **FROZEN PLAN / NOT_STARTED**

Mode: **REMOTE**  
Depends on: `WP-CITY-03` PASS, `WP-SCENE-04` PASS

## Objective

Turn CITY-03's selected retained area into an exact scenario-production specification: which exterior segments, buildings, interiors, access layers and discovery hooks must exist in the first keeper slice, and which are explicitly deferred.

## Work

1. Map every retained-seed parcel/location to CITY tier + SCENE S-depth.
2. Select first reusable street/building/interior compositions to prove.
3. Define retained geometry versus temporary presentation.
4. Define a bounded discovery pack containing ordinary, social/temporal and at least one deeper physical/institutional/cultural vector without overstuffing the seed.
5. Define authoring order and dependencies so later Arkus/Unity work can build incrementally.
6. Define acceptance scenarios for the local blockout and later Living World integration.

## Required seed proof shape

The seed should be capable of supporting, when later systems exist:

- ordinary morning with no drama;
- two distinct actor routes;
- one meaningful interior social location;
- one work/service flow;
- one private/semi-private threshold;
- one non-surface discovery vector;
- one player-caused local consequence visible on return;
- at least one clean expansion seam that remains physically believable.

## Deliverables

- `Docs/production/SCENE_PRODUCT_SEED_SPEC.md`
- parcel/location depth map;
- composition manifest proposal;
- interior/access list;
- discovery seed pack;
- local validation scenario pack.

## Acceptance

- The specification is buildable without inventing a new city or new art direction.
- Deferred interiors are explicit; doors do not become accidental promises.
- At least 60% of first-seed structural work is intended for reuse by later expansion; temporary presentation does not count against this rule.
- The seed demonstrates composition reuse rather than bespoke-building every frontage.
- Scope is small enough for a high-quality first local realization.
