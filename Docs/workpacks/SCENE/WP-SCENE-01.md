# WP-SCENE-01 — Streets, parcels + exterior grammar

Status: **FROZEN PLAN / NOT_STARTED**  
Class: PRODUCT / SCENARIO PREPRODUCTION (NON-FOUNDATIONAL)  
Mode: **REMOTE**  
Depends on: `WP-SCENE-00` PASS, `WP-CITY-01` PASS  
May run in parallel with: `WP-SCENE-02` after its own prerequisites

## Objective

Turn CITY mobility into reusable micro-spatial grammar for streets and exterior places so later authoring is constrained by believable frontage, access, slope and route structure rather than an empty plane.

## Required grammar

Define, where relevant:

- street/path width and slope class;
- junction/corner identity;
- frontage alignment and setback bands;
- parcel/buildable region;
- public/service/rear access opportunities;
- stairs/ramp/vertical connectors;
- pedestrian/bicycle/service affordances;
- furniture/vegetation bands;
- river/wharf edge and retaining conditions;
- sightline/landmark/no-build constraints;
- quiet-route and service-route capability.

## Deliverables

- `Docs/production/SCENE_EXTERIOR_GRAMMAR.md`
- reusable street/exterior archetype catalogue proposal;
- parcel/access schema requirements for future Arkus discovery;
- at least one old-quarter, commercial, residential, waterfront/work and rural-edge exterior example.

## Acceptance

- Grammar can express irregular old-quarter fabric without bespoke coordinates for every façade.
- Public and service/rear movement can coexist where product value requires it.
- Waterfront/port grammar does not silently assume unaccepted hydrology/navigation semantics.
- At least two archetypes support non-obvious traversal/discovery without requiring secret tunnels.
- Exterior rules remain compatible with CITY route graph rather than inventing new district links.
