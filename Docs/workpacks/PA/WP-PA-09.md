## Objective

Execute the frozen Juego2 player-causal-agency plan and prove at research level that the protagonist can perturb the same causal world as NPCs instead of using privileged quest-only state paths.

## Required inputs

- accepted PA-01..08 findings;
- `PA-09_PLAYER_CAUSAL_AGENCY.md` and `PA-09_AMENDMENT_01_EMBODIED_ACTIONS.md`;
- both cross-cutting Living World amendments.

## Research focus

- meaningful player actions as first-class causes;
- semantic equivalence between player and NPC world actions where appropriate;
- embodied/non-dialogue actions alongside conversation;
- consequences routed to material, belief, relationship, memory, schedule, activity and institutional owners rather than mutated ad hoc;
- actions whose causal chains continue while the player is absent;
- recovery/repair as gameplay, not reset magic;
- legibility of player-caused aftermath;
- combat as a future action family that must rejoin the same consequence universe, without researching combat mechanics here.

## Deliverables

All frozen-plan outputs plus:

- bounded player→NPC→NPC causal chain;
- non-dialogue intervention scenario;
- player-vs-NPC semantic-equivalence counterfactual;
- quest-magic negative fixture;
- H2/H4/H6 consumer boundary notes.

## Acceptance

A player can change shared state through an ordinary semantic action and later encounter downstream NPC consequences that continue without a bespoke script carrying the chain.

Negative gate: reject any model where the semantically equivalent NPC action uses Living World owners but the player action bypasses them through private quest flags.

Accepted by the PA-B1 batch evidence and independent PASS review above, including the bounded A11B-10 story/systemic collision oracle.

## Deferred proof

Controls, interaction UX, animation, physics, concrete action inventory and runtime authority are H2+ implementation concerns.
