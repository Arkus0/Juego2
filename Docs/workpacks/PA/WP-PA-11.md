## Objective

Determine how the player can understand important simulation consequences that occurred elsewhere or earlier without omniscient quest markers or mandatory exposition.

## Required inputs

- accepted PA-01..10 findings;
- donor `PA-09_PLAN_INVESTIGATION_LEGIBILITY.md` remapped to canonical PA-11;

## Research questions

- witnessed versus missed events;
- testimony whose content reflects actual actor knowledge;
- physical/digital/documentary traces and records;
- routine inference and anomalies;
- causal provenance sufficient for reconstruction without exposing engine-only truth;
- notebook/knowledge presentation as a possible consumer, not assumed architecture;
- how false testimony and stale belief remain playable rather than UI bugs;
- how much ambiguity is desirable before the system becomes unreadable.

## Deliverables

- canonical PA-11 dossier;
- missed-event reconstruction scenario using at least two independent channels;
- false/stale testimony case;
- omniscient-marker negative control;
- trace-retention ownership/budget questions for PA-13;
- H4/H5 future-consumer notes.

## Acceptance

A player can reconstruct a meaningful missed causal event from at least two legitimate, independently useful channels while the system preserves uncertainty and actor-specific knowledge.

Negative gate: the investigation layer cannot read privileged engine lineage and simply present it as knowledge the player has not earned.
