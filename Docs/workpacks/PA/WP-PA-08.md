# WP-PA-08 — Leisure, Social Activities & Minigames research

Status: **FROZEN PLAN / NOT_STARTED**  
Class: **RESEARCH / NON-FOUNDATIONAL**  
Execution: **REMOTE_RESEARCH**  
Depends on: `WP-PA-07` PASS + merge + DocSync  
Blocks: `WP-PA-09` only

## Objective

Execute the already frozen Juego2 plan `PA-08_LEISURE_SOCIAL_ACTIVITIES_AND_MINIGAMES.md` and decide how repeatable activities belong to ordinary town life rather than forming isolated protagonist-only minigame islands.

## Required inputs

- accepted PA-01..07 findings;
- `Docs/research/living-world/PA-08_LEISURE_SOCIAL_ACTIVITIES_AND_MINIGAMES.md` exactly as the frozen research plan;
- playable-causal-city and intentional-transformation amendments.

## Research focus

- NPC participation with or without the player;
- real place/time/capacity/opportunity costs;
- join/refuse/watch/interrupt/leave/resume boundaries;
- activity-local game rules versus shared Living World outcomes;
- bounded social/material outcomes after resolution;
- schedule/relationship/memory/work interactions through their owners;
- activities that are genuinely fun even before systemic consequences are considered;
- individual/low-social activities as well as explicitly social ones.

## Deliverables

All outputs required by the frozen PA-08 plan, plus:

- at least one social and one individual/low-social scenario;
- explicit activity→structured-outcome seam;
- player-absent activity case;
- minigame-island negative control;
- future H2/H3/H4 implementation questions kept separate from research findings.

## Acceptance

An accepted activity model can exist in normal town life without the player, can admit player participation/interference where appropriate, consumes real world opportunity, and emits only bounded outcomes owned by normal downstream systems.

Negative gate: the player launching a private score screen must not be the only way an activity exists or matters.

## Deferred proof

Input feel, UI, animation, networking/timing and individual game rules require later playable work.

## Definition of Done

PA-08 resolves the shared-activity boundary without designing every minigame. Reviewer PASS names `WP-PA-09` next.
