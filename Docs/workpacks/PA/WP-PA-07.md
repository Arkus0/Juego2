## Objective

Define the smallest material/work model that makes jobs, services and businesses create human opportunities and consequences instead of decorative schedules or a hidden economy game.

## Required inputs

- accepted PA-01..06 findings;
- accepted CITY location/depth planning as scenario context only;
- donor frozen `PA-07_PLAN_ECONOMY_WORK.md`, rebased to Juego2;
- current player-causal and governance amendments.

## Research questions

- jobs, shifts, opening/service windows and worker absence;
- service/resource capacity and contention;
- household/work conflicts;
- material dependencies only where they create visible decisions/opportunities;
- demand/supply abstraction appropriate to a compact town;
- how player action can help, disrupt, steal, supply, block or reroute a dependency through shared state;
- how later governance can alter rules/resources/opportunities without directly choosing actor responses;
- recovery and substitution when a worker/resource is unavailable.

## Deliverables

- canonical PA-07 dossier;
- minimum material vocabulary and ownership map;
- one multi-actor work/service causal scenario;
- one player intervention scenario;
- hidden-macroeconomy negative control;
- H3/H4 future-consumer notes and empirical unknowns.

## Acceptance

A bounded material/work change must be able to alter at least two actors' later choices through shared causal state, not a bespoke quest branch.

Negative gate: reject any proposed economy complexity that cannot be tied to observable player/NPC behaviour in the intended town scale.

Accepted by the PA-B1 batch evidence and independent PASS review above.
