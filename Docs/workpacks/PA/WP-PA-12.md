# WP-PA-12 — Governance as Intervention in Simulation research

Status: **FROZEN PLAN / NOT_STARTED**  
Class: **RESEARCH / NON-FOUNDATIONAL**  
Execution: **REMOTE_RESEARCH**  
Depends on: `WP-PA-11` PASS + merge + DocSync  
Blocks: `WP-PA-13` only

## Objective

Research governance as high-leverage intervention into the same causal town: policy changes rules/resources/opportunities, then actors respond through their own systems.

## Required inputs

- accepted PA-01..11 findings;
- donor `PA-10_PLAN_GOVERNANCE.md` as historical preregistration input;
- `PA-12_AMENDMENT_01_EMERGENT_GOVERNANCE.md` as current Juego2 direction;
- intentional-transformation amendment.

## Research focus

- reusable institutional levers such as access, permission, schedule, capacity, allocation, service, cost/support, obligation and public commitment;
- macro decision → rule/resource/opportunity change → independent actor response;
- third-person consequences the player can encounter after leaving the office/meeting;
- later local player action that can help, resist, mediate or exploit policy implementation;
- constructive as well as destructive transformation;
- repeal/rollback without pretending prior memories, debts, moved resources or relationships never happened;
- presentation default through people/meetings/documents/compact UI rather than a separate strategy game unless evidence later requires one.

## Deliverables

- canonical PA-12 dossier;
- macro→micro and micro→aftermath scenario;
- two divergent trajectories from equivalent starting state without global ideology-mode flags;
- repeal-is-not-time-machine fixture;
- mind-control and dashboard-only negative controls;
- future H4+/governance consumer notes.

## Acceptance

A governance decision can change shared opportunities for multiple actors, actors respond for their own reasons, and later local action can alter the aftermath without bypassing subsystem ownership.

Negative gate: `CommunistTown`, `AuthoritarianTown`, `CultTown` or equivalent global labels cannot substitute for concrete rules plus actor decisions.

## Deferred proof

Final mayor UX, policy tuning, institutional breadth, save/runtime scaling and fun require later implementation/playtesting.

## Definition of Done

PA-12 defines governance as systemic gameplay rather than buffs or mind control. Reviewer PASS names `WP-PA-13` next.
