# WP-H2-02 — Keeper playable shell

Status: **DORMANT / NOT_STARTED**  
Class: PRODUCT / `PRODUCT_CHECKPOINT`  
Depends on: accepted H2 plan + `WP-H1-GATE` PASS + `WP-CITY-07` PASS  
Blocks: `WP-H2-03`

## Claim

The retained CITY-07 slice can be played as a third-person game space with the minimum player shell needed to judge H2 visually and spatially.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

- **Positive claim:** the accepted CITY-07 keeper slice functions as one real third-person playable space across the representative Puente -> Casco -> Bar chain, including the public Bar interior.
- **Mandatory positive evidence:** (1) a continuous third-person play capture/traversal of the representative chain on the accepted keeper fixture; (2) recorded evidence that collision/traversal and the exterior -> threshold -> public-interior transition are usable; (3) evidence of the bounded interaction surface reserved for H2-03; (4) confirmation that the player/camera is exercising the retained keeper scene rather than a parallel test map; (5) a short residual ledger for any non-blocking movement/camera issues.
- **Negative gates:** FAIL if traversal uses a separate proxy/test map; if collision, camera or threshold handling prevents representative traversal; if the Bar public interior is not actually reachable in the playable flow; if the shell hides/replaces keeper geometry to make traversal pass; or if Living World/story/schedule systems are introduced as substitutes for the bounded player-shell claim.
- **Non-claims:** no final NPC behaviour, save game, combat, quest flow or town-wide gameplay loop.
- **Allowed residuals:** non-blocking camera tuning, animation polish, controller/UX breadth and traversal polish outside the representative fixture. Residuals may not include a broken representative route, inaccessible required interior, or a separate non-keeper play space.
- **Evaluation method:** human third-person play inspection is mandatory for camera/scale/traversal readability, combined with the smallest machine-verifiable collision/scene/fixture checks available where useful. A screenshot or editor-only inspection cannot substitute for the play traversal.
- **Consumed predecessors:** `WP-CITY-07` supplies the accepted keeper geometry/access/elevation/spatial conclusions; `WP-H1-GATE` supplies accepted Unity materialization/authority boundaries. H2-02 consumes rather than redefines those guarantees.

## Required benchmark

Walk the accepted representative chain from the Puente/bridge approach through W12/Casco to Bar F01 and into its public interior.

## PASS only if all are true

- stable third-person movement and camera work across the representative chain;
- collision/traversal/navigation assumptions do not produce obvious keeper-blocking failures;
- exterior -> Bar threshold -> public interior transition is usable;
- one bounded interaction surface is available for H2-03 dialogue/bark integration;
- the player/camera scale exposes the real keeper environment rather than a separate test map;
- no Living World, schedule or story system is introduced merely to satisfy the shell.

## Negative gates

The acceptance-contract negative gates above are binding. A technically controllable character is insufficient if the required keeper route/interior cannot be played continuously in third person.

## Non-claims

No final NPC behaviour, save game, combat, quest flow or town-wide gameplay loop.
