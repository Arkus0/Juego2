## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

- **Positive claim:** the accepted CITY-07 keeper slice functions as one real third-person playable space across the representative Puente -> Casco -> Bar chain, including the public Bar interior.
- **Mandatory positive evidence:** (1) a continuous third-person play capture/traversal of the representative chain on the accepted keeper fixture; (2) recorded evidence that collision/traversal and the exterior -> threshold -> public-interior transition are usable; (3) one player hotspot, one NPC interaction anchor and one object on the same keeper street, using admitted Core surfaces where selected; (4) confirmation that the player/camera is exercising the retained keeper scene rather than a parallel test map, with named 01A/H2F controller disposition; (5) a short residual ledger for any non-blocking movement/camera issues; (6) a concise street/interior pattern reuse note.
- **Negative gates:** FAIL if traversal uses a separate proxy/test map; if collision, camera or threshold handling prevents representative traversal; if the Bar public interior is not actually reachable in the playable flow; if the hotspot/NPC/object exists only in a parallel fixture; if the shell hides/replaces keeper geometry to make traversal pass; if the shell silently swaps the frozen H2F player/camera/input/navigation baseline without an accepted amendment; or if Living World/story/schedule systems are introduced as substitutes for the bounded player-shell claim.
- **Non-claims:** no final NPC behaviour, save game, combat, quest flow or town-wide gameplay loop.
- **Allowed residuals:** non-blocking camera tuning, animation polish, controller/UX breadth and traversal polish outside the representative fixture. Residuals may not include a broken representative route, inaccessible required interior, a separate non-keeper play space, or an unresolved foundational player/navigation-tool choice.
- **Evaluation method:** human third-person play inspection is mandatory for camera/scale/traversal readability, combined with the smallest machine-verifiable collision/scene/fixture checks available where useful. A screenshot or editor-only inspection cannot substitute for the play traversal.
- **Consumed predecessors:** `WP-CITY-07` supplies the accepted keeper geometry/access/elevation/spatial conclusions; `WP-H1-GATE` supplies accepted Unity materialization/authority boundaries; `WP-H2F-GATE` supplies the frozen player/camera/input/navigation and relevant render/toolchain baseline. H2-02 consumes rather than redefines those guarantees.
