## Claim

Arkus can express and edit the representative keeper world through typed game-space/assembly concepts rather than anonymous transform placement, while preserving accepted CITY/ART/H1 authority.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

- **Positive claim:** the representative keeper fixture is discoverable, authorable and locally editable through typed accepted public Arkus surfaces without making Unity or anonymous transforms the semantic authority.
- **Mandatory positive evidence:** (1) a recorded fresh-author transcript from public discovery through intent/plan/materialize/inspect; (2) one localized semantic edit with before/after evidence showing unrelated accepted content remained unchanged; (3) public evidence of the ART composition/provenance choices used; (4) one visible unsupported/missing-required-coverage failure; (5) final rerun of the complete representative proof against the accepted `WP-CITY-07` keeper fixture, including reconciliation/rematerialization evidence where the accepted H1 flow requires it.
- **Negative gates:** FAIL if the proof requires implementation-source reading, a hidden Unity-only registry/authority, anonymous-transform editing presented as the semantic authoring contract, silent final-looking fallback for unsupported required coverage, regeneration/damage of unrelated accepted content, or only a synthetic/pre-keeper fixture for final acceptance.
- **Non-claims:** no visual-polish, NPC-routine, Living World or whole-town-generator claim.
- **Allowed residuals:** vocabulary/content not required by the representative Puente -> Casco -> Bar benchmark; broader town generation; visual polish owned by ART-02; richer actor/activity semantics owned after H2. Residuals may not include missing typed concepts or coverage required by the benchmark itself.
- **Evaluation method:** named combination of fresh-author transcript/public-surface inspection, deterministic or structured checks for preservation/provenance/parity where machine-verifiable, and human inspection of the realized keeper fixture. Human judgement does not replace machine-verifiable authority/preservation evidence.
- **Consumed predecessors:** `WP-H1-GATE` supplies accepted canonical→Unity/public-authoring and reconciliation boundaries; `WP-ART-01` supplies accepted assembly/provenance/coverage truth; `WP-CITY-07` supplies the keeper spatial/structural fixture for final acceptance. H2-01 integrates these guarantees and does not re-prove their generic mechanics.

## Work

Provide the minimum typed authoring vocabulary needed by the retained benchmark, including route/street/site, building/shell, facade/host/opening, threshold/portal/interior, shared metric/elevation relationships, approved composition identity and only those landmark/activity/wait concepts actually consumed by the benchmark.

Implementation/research may begin before CITY-07 closes if it uses bounded predecessor-valid fixtures and does not pretend keeper acceptance already exists. Final H2-01 acceptance must rerun the representative proof against the accepted CITY-07 keeper constraints/slice it claims to support.

Prove one bounded intent -> plan -> materialize -> inspect -> localized semantic edit -> revalidate/reconcile loop through accepted public surfaces.

## PASS only if all are true

- `WP-CITY-07` has passed before final H2-01 acceptance;
- a fresh capable author can discover the required vocabulary/composition constraints without reading implementation source;
- the author can make one legal local semantic edit without regenerating unrelated world content;
- asset/assembly selection preserves accepted ART provenance/connection requirements;
- Unity materialization uses accepted H1 authority and reconciliation rather than becoming canonical truth;
- missing/unsupported required coverage fails visibly;
- unaffected accepted content is demonstrably preserved;
- the final evidence uses the accepted keeper-relevant fixture, not only synthetic cubes or a pre-keeper approximation.

## Negative gates

The acceptance-contract negative gates above are binding. In particular, a visually plausible result is still FAIL if it was produced through hidden authority, silent fallback or unbounded regeneration.

## Non-claims

No visual polish, NPC routine, Living World or whole-town generator claim.
