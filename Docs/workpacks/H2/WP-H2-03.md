# WP-H2-03 — Character presentation + population proxy

Status: **DORMANT / NOT_STARTED**
Class: PRODUCT / `PRODUCT_CHECKPOINT`
Depends on: `WP-H2-02` PASS + `WP-ART-CHAR-02` PASS + `WP-ART-ANIM-02` PASS
Blocks: `WP-ART-02` and `WP-ART-03`
Binding decision: `Docs/product/VISUAL_PRODUCTION_FACTORY_AMENDMENT.md`

## Expanded role

The original six-character benchmark remains useful, but it must now **consume** the industrialized character and animation production lanes rather than hand-authoring six isolated examples.

The six required presentation characters remain an A/B/C design-role sample, not a population cap or a claim of systemic NPC depth. At least one short local/GC2 dialogue or equivalent admitted interaction and quiet/busy occupancy are still required.

## Claim

The keeper H2 environment can be judged with representative human occupancy, and the character/animation factories produce credible Juego2 inhabitants that can populate scenes repeatedly without bespoke per-character runtime plumbing.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

**Mandatory positive evidence:**

1. third-person capture showing at least six human-scale character presentations together/in representative use of the keeper slice;
2. evidence that those characters were produced through `ART-CHAR-02`, not six bespoke parallel pipelines;
3. clothing, scale, silhouette and wardrobe variation sufficient to distinguish the presentations visually;
4. idle/walk presentation plus a representative subset of `ART-ANIM-02` conversational/ambient/object vocabulary;
5. bounded point-to-point/nav movement;
6. one player-triggered minimal dialogue/bark interaction using an admitted local surface;
7. quieter and busier occupancy states;
8. provenance/input record and A/B/C intended-role tags;
9. no hidden persistent identity/jobs/memory/goals introduced to make the benchmark look deeper.

**FAIL if:** six visually identical/raw humanoids satisfy the count only numerically; characters bypass the accepted factory; source characters are accepted because rigs are technically valid while clothing/presentation is poor; proxy movement is presented as schedules/routines; or the animation proof requires bespoke per-NPC controllers that contradict `ART-ANIM-02` reuse.

**Allowed residuals:** hero-specific facial/detail polish, more wardrobe breadth, story-specific acting, schedule/routine depth, persistent identity and population scale beyond the benchmark.

## PASS only if all are true

- six or more factory-produced human-scale presentations are visible in the retained benchmark;
- all player-facing characters are appropriately clothed/presentable for setting and role;
- the group shows useful visual variation;
- admitted idle/walk and reusable acting/ambient animation vocabulary works on representative characters;
- bounded nav/occupancy movement works;
- one minimal interaction produces a dialogue line/bark response;
- quiet/busy occupancy can be inspected;
- no systemic NPC depth is falsely claimed.

## Non-claims

No persistent NPC identity, clock, schedule, needs, social graph, memory, beliefs, rumours or autonomous events.
