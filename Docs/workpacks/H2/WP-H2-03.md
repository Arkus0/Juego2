# WP-H2-03 — Character presentation + population proxy

Status: **DORMANT / NOT_STARTED**  
Class: PRODUCT / `PRODUCT_CHECKPOINT`  
Depends on: `WP-H2-02` PASS + accepted relevant ART/H1 character/animation inputs  
Blocks: `WP-ART-02` and `WP-H2-GATE`

## Claim

The keeper H2 environment can be judged with representative human occupancy, and Juego2 has a credible first character presentation language without prematurely implementing persistent Living World NPCs.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

- **Positive claim:** the retained H2 keeper slice contains enough visually representative, human-scale character occupancy and bounded movement/interaction to judge the environment-character relationship without claiming persistent NPC simulation.
- **Mandatory positive evidence:** (1) third-person capture showing at least six human-scale character presentations together/in representative use of the retained slice; (2) evidence of clothing, scale, silhouette and wardrobe variation sufficient to distinguish the presentations visually; (3) idle and walk presentation plus bounded point-to-point/nav movement; (4) one player-triggered minimal dialogue/bark interaction; (5) inspection of at least two occupancy conditions, including a quieter and a busier representative state; (6) a short provenance/input record tying character/animation use to accepted ART/H1 inputs.
- **Negative gates:** FAIL if six visually identical/raw humanoids satisfy the count only numerically; if unpresentable source characters are accepted because their rig is technically valid; if proxy movement is presented as schedules/routines; if hidden persistent identity/jobs/memory/goals are introduced to make the benchmark look deeper; or if occupancy is demonstrated only outside the retained keeper/playable fixture.
- **Non-claims:** no persistent NPC identity, clock, schedule, needs, social graph, memory, beliefs, rumours or autonomous events.
- **Allowed residuals:** final facial detail, broad wardrobe catalogue, bespoke behaviour variety, schedule/routine depth, persistent identity and population scale beyond the six-character H2 benchmark. Residuals may not leave the representative characters raw, unclothed, wrongly scaled or visually unusable for ART-02 judgement.
- **Evaluation method:** mandatory human third-person visual/play inspection for presentation, occupancy and scale, combined with bounded machine-verifiable checks for actor count/input identity/navigation/interactions where practical. Count alone cannot satisfy the visual claim.
- **Consumed predecessors:** `WP-H2-02` supplies the real retained playable fixture and interaction surface; accepted ART inputs supply character/wardrobe presentation direction; accepted H1 inputs supply the relevant import/materialization/animation bridge mechanics. H2-03 does not re-prove generic bridge behaviour.

## PASS only if all are true

- at least six human-scale character presentations are visible in the retained benchmark;
- all player-facing characters are appropriately clothed/presentable for the setting and role;
- scale, silhouette and wardrobe variation are sufficient to judge the environment/character relationship;
- idle and walk animation presentation is functional;
- characters can perform bounded point-to-point/nav movement used for occupancy testing;
- one minimal player interaction produces a bounded dialogue line/bark response;
- at least two representative occupancy conditions are inspected so density/camera/traversal are not judged only in an empty scene;
- characters do not silently acquire schedules, jobs, memory, relationships, goals or narrative authority.

## Negative gates

FAIL if six visually identical/raw humanoids satisfy the count only numerically; if proxy movement is presented as a daily routine system; or if an unpresentable source character is accepted because its rig is technically valid. The additional acceptance-contract negative gates above are also binding.

## Non-claims

No persistent NPC identity, clock, schedule, needs, social graph, memory, beliefs, rumours or autonomous events.
