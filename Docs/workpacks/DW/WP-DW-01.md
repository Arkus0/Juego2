## Objective and central claim

Prove that accepted CITY programme/binding facts can be projected through the generic DW contract and that at least two real mechanically decidable CITY guarantees produce causal REDs when violated, without adding CITY semantics to H0.

## WHY_THIS_BOUNDARY

DW-00 can be perfectly generic yet fail on the first real design domain. This WP owns the smallest non-toy CITY proof. It does not expand into production reporting/budgeting, which can fail independently even if invariant validation works.

## Inherited guarantees

Consumes accepted CITY authority as source truth, DW-00 projection/provenance/rebuild semantics and existing H0 public surfaces. It does not reopen accepted CITY design decisions merely by encoding them.

## New guarantees owned

- a bounded CITY provider/schema that maps accepted design facts without authority inversion;
- independent/effective enumeration for every CITY universe claimed complete by this WP;
- executable preservation of programme-required access roles by later binding;
- one second relation/cardinality invariant from accepted CITY design whose oracle is explicit and mechanically decidable;
- actionable diagnostics naming projected subject, violated rule, missing/invalid relation and source provenance;
- causal mutation fixtures that prove the validator, not merely the mutation, turns RED.

## Explicitly not re-proved

Whether CITY's design rules are good, complete CITY programme correctness, retained-seed geometry, Unity greybox, art feasibility, H0 internals or PA semantics.

## Allowed scope

CITY DW provider/schema, selected accepted CITY facts, deterministic validators/queries, focused tests/fixtures and evidence.

## Forbidden scope

Changing accepted CITY rules to fit the projection, full CITY conversion, Unity/scene work, generic H0 semantic changes, subjective design scoring or treating generated DW state as CITY authority.

## Architecture / authority boundary

The source universe is enumerated from accepted CITY authority independently of the DW rows under test. The primary access invariant is evaluated as a true subset relation over source-derived programme requirements versus source-derived accepted binding facts. A missing projected row/role must not shrink the universe and make validation green.

## Acceptance criteria

- the complete accepted universe relevant to each chosen invariant is enumerated independently from the projection registry;
- for every accepted CITY-02 A/B functional POI in the access-role universe, `programme_required_access_roles` is a subset of `bound_required_access_roles` under the accepted binding rule;
- the second invariant exercises a different structural shape (for example required anchor/cardinality for an accepted interior/spatial class) and has an explicit source-owned oracle;
- every projected fact used by either invariant links back to accepted authority provenance;
- valid accepted data is GREEN;
- a deliberate removal of one inherited required access role causes the validation pipeline itself to RED and reports the exact missing role/subject;
- a deliberate violation of the second invariant likewise REDs through its semantic oracle;
- reversing input enumeration or rebuilding from scratch cannot change verdict/digest for semantically equal source truth;
- no H0 kernel/public generic contract gains a CITY-specific type/name/rule.

## Deterministic proof / evidence

Build the CITY slice from accepted sources, validate both invariants, then run causal synthetic regressions. The access-role negative control must mutate effective bound roles while leaving the source universe/oracle intact and assert a validator/pipeline failure, not simply assert that the mutation happened.

## Causal negative-conformance classes

- inherited required access role removed from one effective binding;
- entire required-role surface omitted for a subject rather than one row removed;
- one A/B POI omitted from the projected set while the independent source universe still contains it;
- second-invariant required anchor/relation removed;
- provenance points to a different/stale CITY source while values happen to match;
- a validator enumerates only declared/projected rules and therefore fails to notice a removed rule execution.

## Content-shape probe

Required. Use the real accepted A/B access-role universe and the smallest real CITY universe needed by the second invariant. Do not substitute toy POIs for the positive proof; synthetic mutations are allowed only as causal negative controls over the real-shaped data.
