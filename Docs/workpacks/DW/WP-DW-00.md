## Objective and central claim

Implement the smallest generic Design World provider/projection boundary needed to represent selected accepted design facts as typed, provenance-bearing, rebuildable Arkus-consumable state without transferring semantic authority away from the accepted source documents or changing H0 meaning.

## WHY_THIS_BOUNDARY

A CITY implementation without an explicit authority/provenance contract could accidentally make a generated index the new truth or bake CITY vocabulary into the kernel. A contract with no executable reference would be speculative. This WP therefore owns the generic projection envelope, a tiny domain-neutral reference fixture for mechanics/completeness proof, and one bounded approved-Juego2 content-shape probe through the same public surface. The product probe tests representability and granularity only; the first real CITY semantic/invariant consumer remains DW-01.

## Inherited guarantees

Consumes accepted H0 identity/reference/validation/mutation/journal/snapshot/query/transport boundaries, the DW planning architecture and `Docs/engineering/FOUNDATIONAL_PROOF_STANDARD.md` v1.5 or later accepted successor. H0 remains canonical for its own authored state and public semantics.

## New guarantees owned

- versioned generic projected-fact/relationship envelope sufficient for DW consumers;
- stable provenance from every proof-relevant projected fact to an accepted source authority anchor;
- explicit projection/schema version and deterministic normalized representation;
- rebuild equality from the same authority inputs and projection version;
- fail-closed stale/missing/ambiguous provenance handling;
- a reference source adapter/fixture demonstrating the contract without CITY/PA vocabulary;
- one bounded approved-Juego2 representative content-shape probe proving the generic surface can express real target content at the identity/granularity/provenance boundary it owns without importing domain semantics into H0; and
- derived DW state is disposable and cannot become source authority merely because it is queryable.

## Explicitly not re-proved

Full H0 correctness, transport parity, H1 projection semantics, CITY design correctness or semantic invariants, PA research correctness, CTX policy or Unity behavior.

## Allowed scope

Generic DW provider/schema/adapter code, domain-neutral reference fixtures, one bounded approved-Juego2 product-source probe limited to representability/identity/granularity/provenance through the owned public surface, focused tests, architecture reconciliation and evidence. Existing public Arkus contracts may be consumed as-is.

## Forbidden scope

CITY/PA-specific semantics in kernel/public generic contracts, ownership of CITY semantic invariants before DW-01, Unity integration, workpack/reviewer process modeling, LLM extraction as authority, H0 oracle weakening, gameplay, art tooling or external product packaging.

## Architecture / authority boundary

Accepted source documents remain authority. DW state is a derived projection whose records retain source lineage. Rebuilding the projection cannot mutate authority inputs. The representative Juego2 probe may use accepted product/design content as an input shape, but it does not make DW-00 authoritative for that content's domain semantics. If accepted public Arkus contracts cannot express the required generic envelope without semantic change, this WP stops and produces causal reopen evidence rather than patching H0 privately.

## Acceptance criteria

- projected records carry stable identity, schema/projection version and source provenance sufficient to detect stale/missing/ambiguous authority anchors;
- identical authority inputs + projection version rebuild to equal normalized projected facts/relations and equal relevant digests/results;
- changing a material authority fact or projection rule changes the relevant normalized projection/result;
- deleting derived DW state and rebuilding does not require hidden generated truth;
- authority source bytes/hash are unchanged by projection/query/validation operations;
- no CITY/PA/process vocabulary or type enters H0 kernel dependencies/public semantics;
- reference fixture is usable through the same public surface later consumers will use;
- one bounded slice of the currently approved Juego2 target is represented through that same surface and the probe records representability, identity/granularity, provenance and every affected inspect/query/validate/diff/rebuild boundary actually owned by DW-00;
- every representative-probe finding is classified as an in-scope blocker, exact predecessor reopen condition, named future/residual decision or out-of-boundary observation rather than silently normalized away; and
- provenance failure cannot be reported as a valid fact merely because an index row remains.

## Deterministic proof / evidence

Two complementary fixtures are required before freeze:

1. **Neutral proof fixture.** A tiny domain-neutral fixture defines an independently enumerated source universe with typed objects, relations and one mechanical invariant. The proof builds the projection, queries/validates it, rebuilds from scratch and compares normalized output. Authority source hash is captured before/after. This fixture owns the mechanics/completeness and causal negative controls below.
2. **Representative Juego2 content-shape probe.** Use one bounded slice from a currently approved Juego2 product/design source, with only enough concrete content to exercise the generic envelope and the public boundaries DW-00 actually defines. An accepted CITY record/relationship shape is permitted as source content, but the probe asks only whether the shape is representable with correct identity/granularity/provenance and owned inspect/query/validate/diff/rebuild behavior. It must not assert CITY semantic correctness, access-role invariants, programme completeness or other DW-01 claims.

The representative probe supplements rather than replaces the neutral independent-universe proof and negative controls.

## Causal negative-conformance classes

- one material source fact is omitted while the independent neutral source universe remains complete: projection completeness/validation must RED;
- a projected record retains an old source anchor after the source changes: stale provenance must RED;
- a projected fact points ambiguously to two authority locations: it cannot validate as trustworthy;
- projection rule/version changes while cached derived output is reused: staleness/rebuild check must RED;
- derived/index state is deleted and a hidden dependency prevents equal rebuild;
- a domain-specific type/reference is introduced into the generic H0 dependency graph.

## Content-shape probe

Required under the foundational proof standard and **not** satisfied by the neutral fixture alone.

Keep the domain-neutral fixture above for independently enumerable mechanics proof. Separately, pass one bounded slice of the currently approved Juego2 target through the candidate surface. Prefer an already accepted source shape that stresses real identity/relation/provenance granularity without requiring new domain design—for example one accepted CITY programme/entity relationship slice with its source anchor and typed relation shape.

The probe must:

- exercise only the representability and public-contract boundaries DW-00 owns;
- record the approved source/anchor used and the assumptions needed to project it;
- demonstrate that no CITY/PA-specific concept had to enter H0/public generic semantics merely to represent the real shape;
- inspect the owned identity/granularity/provenance plus applicable inspect/query/validate/diff/rebuild behavior;
- classify every finding using the foundational standard's blocker/reopen/residual/out-of-boundary categories; and
- leave semantic CITY rules, invariant truth and full CITY universe/completeness to DW-01.

A synthetic/domain-neutral fixture remains valid proof infrastructure and negative-control material, but it is not the representative Juego2 probe.
