# WP-H1-06 — Asset/prefab realization + managed derivatives

Status: PLANNED / NOT_STARTED
Class: FOUNDATIONAL
EXECUTION_REQUIREMENT: LOCAL_UNITY_REQUIRED
Depends on: `WP-H1-05` PASS
Blocks: `WP-H1-07`

## Objective and central claim

Resolve logical catalogue bindings into truthful Unity asset/prefab relationships, instantiate accepted source/derived prefabs without overwriting them, and create only explicitly bridge-managed Unity prefab derivatives inside the accepted generation boundary.

## WHY_THIS_BOUNDARY

Scene graph publication is already accepted by H1-05; this WP adds a distinct asset/prefab authority and reference-fidelity claim. Component property semantics are kept out because their adapter universe and error cases differ. Asset and prefab handling stay together because Unity prefab identity is an asset relationship, not a separate authority.

A critical distinction is frozen here:

- **Juego2 Derived art** is authored by ART from Quaternius Source (mesh/material/outfit/animation/content changes);
- **bridge-managed derivatives** are Unity projection artifacts/variants created for technical realization without becoming new art-source authority.

H1-06 owns only the second category.

## Inherited guarantees

H1-04 catalogue identity over accepted ART-01 Quaternius Source + Juego2 Derived content, H1-05 generation/publication, H1-01 logical asset dependencies and H0 canonical authority.

## New guarantees owned

- source and Juego2 Derived assets/prefabs are read-only catalogue inputs to bridge materialization;
- prefab instance/source/nested relationship is retained in normalized observation;
- bridge-managed prefab derivative identity, lineage and generation receipt;
- missing/wrong-type/rebound logical asset diagnostics;
- asset changes cannot silently change the canonical selection.

## Explicitly not re-proved

Catalogue completeness, scene publication mechanics, arbitrary components, ART source/derivative creation/provenance, licensing beyond accepted ART inputs or Unity YAML byte stability.

## Allowed scope

PrefabUtility/AssetDatabase use inside managed roots, accepted upstream/derived prefab inspection/instantiation, bridge-managed variants/derivatives, mesh/material/clip reference wiring needed by fixtures.

Synthetic repository-owned prefabs/materials remain allowed only for harness-specific causal negative controls or bridge-marker mechanics that are not production-art stand-ins.

## Forbidden scope

Overwriting Quaternius upstream files or ART-owned Juego2 Derived source assets, editing meshes/outfits/textures/animations as content production, arbitrary asset transformation factory, Addressables/bundles, asset-store automation, gameplay, treating prefab contents as canonical state, adopting a competing substitute production-art source, or fabricating production assets when the accepted ART pipeline should own that work.

## Architecture / authority boundary

The canonical binding selects a logical catalogue ID. The catalogue resolves it; the bridge realizes it. Upstream/derived asset bytes/native IDs remain external inputs recorded by catalogue fingerprint and receipt.

Bridge-managed Unity prefab derivatives are disposable projection artifacts with explicit lineage to one accepted catalogue input; they must never be confused with ART-authored Juego2 Derived assets. Deleting/rebuilding bridge outputs cannot delete or rewrite ART source truth.

## Acceptance criteria

- upstream Quaternius and Juego2 Derived source prefab relationships are inspectable and preserved after save/reload;
- accepted ART source/derived files remain byte/semantic unchanged by materialization;
- bridge-managed derivatives exist only under managed roots with explicit source catalogue lineage;
- normalized observation distinguishes ART-owned input identity from bridge-generated derivative identity;
- missing/incompatible/rebound logical IDs block truthful publication with stable diagnostics;
- replacing a catalogue mapping changes catalogue/plan evidence but not canonical world hash;
- repeated materialization is semantically idempotent;
- deleting generated bridge derivatives and rebuilding reproduces normalized relationships;
- production-intent prefab/material relationships exercised by the content shape use accepted ART-01 assets rather than locally authored substitutes.

## Deterministic proof / evidence

Use accepted ART-01 upstream and Juego2 Derived prefab/material relationships, plus one distinct bridge-managed Unity derivative. Observe effective prefab/source/nested links before/after reload, protect accepted ART input hashes and compare generation observations. Harness-only synthetic fixtures may supplement causal controls but do not satisfy the production-content portion of the claim.

## Causal negative-conformance classes

- accepted ART source/derived prefab overwritten;
- prefab instance flattened while parity still reports success;
- nested/source lineage omitted;
- bridge derivative falsely presented as ART/Juego2 source truth;
- wrong asset type accepted;
- missing logical asset silently replaced by a default;
- generated derivative outside managed root;
- input-source mutation changes canonical hash or is journaled as canonical authoring;
- accepted production asset is silently replaced by fabricated substitute content while relationship tests remain green.

## Content-shape probe

Required. Use wall/roof/door/window/prop and/or character relationships from accepted ART-01 upstream/derived content where available; a lone primitive or locally fabricated substitute production-art prefab is insufficient. Synthetic harness-only fixtures are permitted only for proof mechanics/negative controls.

## Dependency / IP implications

No new art source/adoption is expected here. This WP consumes ART-01 content through the H1-04 catalogue and preserves its provenance/distribution boundary. Any missing content shape routes to ART for adaptation/creation before H1 consumes it.

## Residual risks

Complex broad art transformation, wardrobe breadth, missing-object production, animation authoring, vehicles and shipping asset delivery remain ART/H2 scope; H1-11 later proves representative bridge conformance over accepted real upstream + derived assets.

## Exact predecessor reopen condition

Reopen H1-04 only if effective Unity identity proves catalogue mapping false/inapplicable. Prefab realization defects remain H1-06. Content-authoring defects route to ART rather than being patched inside the bridge.
