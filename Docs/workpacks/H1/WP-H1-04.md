# WP-H1-04 — Unity catalogue + identity resolution

Status: PLANNED / NOT_STARTED
Class: FOUNDATIONAL
EXECUTION_REQUIREMENT: LOCAL_UNITY_REQUIRED
Depends on: `WP-H1-03A` PASS
Blocks: `WP-H1-05`

## Objective and central claim

Expose a deterministic, complete-for-declared-scope Unity catalogue of scenes, assets, prefabs, materials, clips and component schemas, with stable Arkus logical IDs mapped to effective Unity locators without making the catalogue canonical `WorldState`.

This is also the **first H1 point at which game-representative source art is needed**. Before such content enters the Unity project, the Worker must adopt a minimal exact human-approved Quaternius Source distribution/slice under `DEPENDENCY_IP_POLICY.md`. H1-04 therefore establishes the source identity/license/import baseline needed to catalogue real production-intent assets; it does not yet prove the broad end-to-end representative slice owned by H1-11.

## WHY_THIS_BOUNDARY

Inventory/identity is read-only and has a different completeness oracle from materialization. It can be correct while writes are unsafe, or false while a hand-picked scene appears to materialize. Asset classes stay together because they share one catalogue authority and snapshot/fingerprint contract.

The minimal external-source adoption belongs here rather than in a separate administrative WP because H1-04 is the first claim that needs effective game-representative assets to exist. Creating throwaway substitute art and replacing it later would create a temporary contract and invalidate useful catalogue/import evidence. The adoption record qualifies the input; catalogue identity/completeness remains the product claim.

## Inherited guarantees

HK01/HK03 discoverability and bounded reads, HK07 projections, HK08 pagination/cost semantics, H1-01 catalogue-reference vocabulary, H1-02 project identity, H1-03 authority and H1-03A public Editor execution/lifecycle, plus HK00A `DEPENDENCY_IP_POLICY.md`.

## New guarantees owned

- versioned catalogue entry/snapshot/fingerprint contracts;
- stable logical IDs separate from path/GUID/file-ID/`GlobalObjectId`;
- bounded query/get for admitted assets, prefabs, scenes, materials, clips and component schemas;
- deterministic collision/missing/incompatible diagnostics;
- independently/effectively bounded completeness universe for reviewed roots/types;
- exact provenance/adoption record for the minimal Quaternius Source slice used from H1-04 onward: source/distribution identity, version/hash where applicable, license/commercial terms, distribution mode, notices, local import root and replacement boundary.

## Explicitly not re-proved

Canonical object identity, generic H0 query correctness, materialization, source-asset mutability, arbitrary Unity-project completeness, full-pack Quaternius coverage, final art quality or H1-11's broad real-asset conformance claim.

## Allowed scope

AssetDatabase/editor inspection, mapping manifest, catalogue scoped capabilities, pagination, snapshot fingerprint, focused local evidence, and the minimum approved Quaternius Source import/adoption slice needed to exercise facade/prefab/material/clip identity.

Repository-owned synthetic content remains allowed only for harness-only proof objects that are not substitutes for game art (for example bridge markers or deliberately malformed negative-test fixtures).

## Forbidden scope

Serializing the asset database into `WorldState`, using paths/native IDs as canonical identity, writing source assets, scanning outside approved project roots, runtime asset delivery/addressables, gameplay, full-pack ingestion, keeper/CITY production, or creating substitute production-intent facade/prefab/material/prop/character art when the approved Quaternius Source provides that content shape.

## Architecture / authority boundary

Catalogue state is project integration state. Canonical bindings reference logical IDs; effective locators remain bridge evidence. The accepted universe is all matching effective assets under explicit reviewed roots, not whatever rows happen to be registered in the mapping manifest.

Quaternius Source is an external read-only input, never canonical authority. Its native paths, hierarchy and metadata may inform catalogue observations but do not define Arkus identity or schemas. Unknown/ambiguous/incompatible license or distribution terms block adoption fail-closed under `DEPENDENCY_IP_POLICY.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- before any game-representative third-party asset is catalogued, the exact human-approved Quaternius Source distribution/slice has a complete adoption record satisfying `DEPENDENCY_IP_POLICY.md`;
- public bounded discovery describes each admitted unit with stable logical identity, type, relevant dimensions/dependencies and adoption status;
- moving an asset while preserving Unity identity preserves logical identity;
- copying creates distinct native identity and cannot alias the source logical ID;
- missing/duplicate/stale mappings fail visibly;
- unchanged effective inventory produces an identical normalized catalogue snapshot/fingerprint;
- catalogue changes do not change canonical world hash;
- a referenced missing/incompatible entry is detectable before truthful materialization;
- capability inventory comes from canonical composition, not a Unity-only registry;
- production-intent content in the H1-04 content-shape probe uses the accepted Quaternius Source slice rather than locally fabricated replacement art.

## Deterministic proof / evidence

Build a small effective Unity inventory containing an accepted Quaternius Source prefab/material/clip/facade slice plus only harness-specific repository-owned proof objects where needed. Compare AssetDatabase/type enumeration against the normalized catalogue and mapping, then exercise move/copy/delete/reimport cases in a disposable project copy. Preserve the exact source/adoption fingerprint used by the candidate.

## Causal negative-conformance classes

- manifest row removed so asset and obligation disappear together;
- move changes logical identity;
- copy aliases source;
- path or `GlobalObjectId` presented as canonical identity;
- stale/missing asset remains silently usable;
- catalogue mutation changes canonical state/hash;
- one public catalogue entry disappears from JSONL or MCP;
- Quaternius source/version/license/adoption identity is missing or ambiguous while the catalogue presents the asset as approved;
- substitute game-art fixture is silently used in place of the accepted Quaternius source slice.

## Content-shape probe

Required. Use the accepted Quaternius Source for the game-representative facade module, source prefab, material and civilian animation-clip shape. Repository-owned synthetic fixtures are acceptable only for harness-specific mechanics or causal negative controls that Quaternius is not meant to provide; they must not stand in for production-intent art.

## Dependency / IP implications

**This is the first external game-asset adoption point in H1.** The Worker must record and verify the exact human-approved Quaternius Source distribution/slice before import according to `DEPENDENCY_IP_POLICY.md`. No source archive may be committed or redistributed contrary to its exact license/distribution terms. Discovery is not legal approval: anything outside the accepted source/adoption record fails closed for retained/materialized use until separately admitted.

H1-05 through H1-10 consume this same accepted source baseline when they need game-representative assets. They may add harness-only test fixtures, but must not introduce alternative substitute production art by default.

## Residual risks

The catalogue is complete only for declared roots/types. Full Quaternius pack coverage, broad representative hierarchy/rig/animation conformance, production asset breadth, Addressables delivery, external repositories and cross-project identity remain outside scope; H1-11 owns expansion to the representative real-asset conformance slice.
