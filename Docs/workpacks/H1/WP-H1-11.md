## Objective and central claim

Adopt one exact commercially admissible real asset slice and prove that the accepted catalogue, binding, materialization, component, validation, reconciliation and reconstruction contracts handle the real hierarchy/material/pivot/rig/animation shapes required for the H1 parity scenario.

## WHY_THIS_BOUNDARY

Generic bridge correctness and third-party content reality can fail independently. This WP has product value only as one end-to-end adoption/conformance slice, so licensing, import recipe and effective bridge proof stay together. A WP per asset would be absurd fragmentation.

## Inherited guarantees

H1-04 through H1-10 complete generic bridge guarantees, HK00A dependency/IP policy, approved `SETTING`/`VISUAL_BIBLE` and accepted CITY-00 spatial-constitution shape.

## New guarantees owned

- exact asset source/version/license/linkage/adaptation record;
- reproducible import/adaptation recipe and logical catalogue mapping;
- effective support for nested hierarchy, shared materials, pivots/scales, humanoid rig and idle/walk/sit clip references in the selected slice;
- real asset package remains replaceable and non-authoritative.

## Explicitly not re-proved

All generic bridge invariants, every asset in the source pack, final art quality, gameplay animation behavior or H2 town production.

## Allowed scope

Approximately 10-15 source pieces: wall modules, roof, door, window/shutter, small props, lamp, tree, rock, one humanoid and three civilian clips; exact adoption record; minimal deterministic import/adaptation outputs; bridge evidence.

## Forbidden scope

Full pack ingestion, whole street/town production, wardrobe/vehicles, bar interior, broad transformation factory, gameplay/controller logic, unverified content or hidden source archives committed contrary to license policy.

## Architecture / authority boundary

Preferred source is an exact human-approved Quaternius distribution if its current terms and files satisfy policy. The Worker must name and validate the exact source in the adoption record; a shape-equivalent commercially admissible source is allowed without changing the claim. If no candidate satisfies the fixed shape and policy, the WP stops blocked rather than weakening or expanding the contract. Assets populate the bridge catalogue and generated projection only. They do not define Arkus schemas or identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- exact source, version/hash, license, distribution mode, notices and replacement boundary are recorded before import;
- every selected item has a stable logical catalogue ID and truthful native mapping;
- one small street-corner/humanoid slice materializes with hierarchy, prefab, material and component relationships intact;
- pivot/scale/import assumptions are explicit and validated;
- humanoid plus idle/walk/sit bindings inspect and reload without missing references;
- clean reconstruction reproduces normalized semantic parity;
- removing/replacing one asset yields actionable missing/incompatible diagnostics, not silent substitution;
- no H2 gameplay, exact CITY-03 seed or keeper-town geometry is introduced.

## Deterministic proof / evidence

Run the accepted clean-checkout -> import/resolve -> canonical bind -> materialize -> validate -> save/reload -> inspect -> delete generated -> rebuild flow. Preserve normalized evidence and one supplementary rendered capture; screenshots are not the parity oracle.

## Causal negative-conformance classes

- unverified/incorrect license passes adoption;
- asset omitted from import manifest/proof universe;
- pivot/scale or nested hierarchy lost;
- material or animation reference silently missing;
- logical ID replaced by asset path;
- source asset modified by projection;
- rendered-looking success hides normalized parity failure.

This WP is the representative content-shape proof. It uses approved Juego2 material and constitution-shaped relationships but remains disposable bridge evidence, deliberately smaller than H2/CITY-07 keeper realization.

## Dependency / IP implications

## Residual risks

Unselected pack content, full character wardrobe, production LOD/performance, visual polish and final animation behavior remain H2+.

## Exact predecessor reopen condition

Reopen the relevant H1 owner only when the real asset shape provides concrete evidence that its accepted generic guarantee is false/inapplicable. A missing optional feature becomes future scope, not automatic predecessor failure.
