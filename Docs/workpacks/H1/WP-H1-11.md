# WP-H1-11 — Representative real-asset Juego2 bridge slice

Status: PLANNED / NOT_STARTED

EXECUTION_REQUIREMENT: LOCAL_UNITY_REQUIRED
Depends on: `WP-H1-10` PASS
Blocks: `WP-H1-GATE`

## Objective and central claim

Expand the exact Quaternius Source baseline already adopted at H1-04 into one broader representative real-asset slice and prove that the accepted catalogue, binding, materialization, component, validation, reconciliation and reconstruction contracts handle the real hierarchy/material/pivot/rig/animation shapes required for the H1 parity scenario.

## WHY_THIS_BOUNDARY

H1-04 deliberately introduces the real source baseline early so H1-04..10 do not build against throwaway production-art substitutes. H1-11 remains necessary because a minimal early source slice cannot by itself prove representative breadth across hierarchy, materials, pivots/scales, humanoid rigging and multiple animation references after all generic bridge owners exist.

This WP therefore owns **representative expansion + end-to-end conformance**, not first adoption. Keeping that closure separate means generic bridge defects discovered by broader real shapes can route to their causal owners without making every earlier WP prove the whole source distribution.

## Inherited guarantees

H1-04 accepted exact Quaternius Source identity/license/distribution baseline and logical catalogue mapping; H1-05 through H1-10 complete generic bridge guarantees over that real-source-backed slice; HK00A dependency/IP policy, approved `SETTING`/`VISUAL_BIBLE` and accepted CITY-00 spatial-constitution shape.

## New guarantees owned

- representative expansion manifest within the already-approved Quaternius Source distribution, including exact selected source items and any additional version/hash/import fingerprints required by those items;
- reproducible import/adaptation recipe for the expanded representative slice and logical catalogue mapping;
- effective support for nested hierarchy, shared materials, pivots/scales, humanoid rig and idle/walk/sit clip references across the selected representative slice;
- end-to-end conformance of the complete accepted H1 bridge chain against those broader real source shapes;
- real asset package remains replaceable and non-authoritative.

## Explicitly not re-proved

The H1-04 legal/commercial adoption decision unless the expanded item set changes the applicable terms/distribution boundary, all generic bridge invariants as abstract claims, every asset in the source distribution, final art quality, gameplay animation behavior or H2 town production.

## Allowed scope

Approximately 10-15 source pieces from the accepted Quaternius Source distribution: wall modules, roof, door, window/shutter, small props, lamp, tree, rock, one humanoid and three civilian clips; expanded exact item/import record; minimal deterministic adaptation outputs; end-to-end bridge evidence.

Harness-only synthetic fixtures may still be used for causal negative controls or bridge-specific markers, but they do not substitute for any production-intent source shape that this WP claims to exercise.

## Forbidden scope

Full-pack ingestion, whole street/town production, wardrobe/vehicles, bar interior, broad transformation factory, gameplay/controller logic, unverified content, hidden source archives committed contrary to license policy, or reverting the already-established H1-04+ source baseline back to locally fabricated production art.

## Architecture / authority boundary

The source distribution is the exact human-approved Quaternius Source baseline accepted at H1-04. This WP may expand the selected item set only within that reviewed distribution/terms boundary, recording exact items/import fingerprints and re-running `DEPENDENCY_IP_POLICY.md` if the applicable source/version/license/distribution terms differ from H1-04.

Assets populate the bridge catalogue and generated projection only. They do not define Arkus schemas or identity. Native source paths/hierarchy/rig metadata remain replaceable external inputs behind logical catalogue identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- the H1-04 source/adoption record is still valid for the exact Quaternius Source distribution used by this candidate, or is explicitly revalidated where terms/version/distribution changed;
- the expanded representative item manifest is exact and reproducible before materialization;
- every selected item has a stable logical catalogue ID and truthful native mapping;
- one small street-corner/humanoid slice materializes with hierarchy, prefab, material and component relationships intact;
- pivot/scale/import assumptions are explicit and validated;
- humanoid plus idle/walk/sit bindings inspect and reload without missing references;
- clean reconstruction reproduces normalized semantic parity;
- removing/replacing one selected source asset yields actionable missing/incompatible diagnostics, not silent substitution;
- no H2 gameplay, exact CITY-03 seed or keeper-town geometry is introduced;
- no production-intent item in the representative claim is satisfied solely by a fabricated substitute asset.

## Deterministic proof / evidence

Run the accepted clean-checkout -> source verify/import/resolve -> canonical bind -> materialize -> validate -> save/reload -> inspect -> delete generated -> rebuild flow over the expanded Quaternius Source slice. Preserve normalized evidence, exact source/item/import fingerprints and one supplementary rendered capture; screenshots are not the parity oracle.

## Causal negative-conformance classes

- expanded source item/version/license/import identity is missing while evidence claims approved real-source coverage;
- selected asset omitted from import manifest/proof universe;
- pivot/scale or nested hierarchy lost;
- material or animation reference silently missing;
- logical ID replaced by asset path;
- source asset modified by projection;
- missing source item silently replaced by fabricated/default production art;
- rendered-looking success hides normalized parity failure.
