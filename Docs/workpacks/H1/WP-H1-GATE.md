Answer: can Arkus, from public discovered contracts and without making Unity canonical truth, create, inspect, modify, validate, materialize and reconstruct a representative Juego2 slice in Unity with identity, dependencies, diagnostics, transactionality/provenance and parity sufficient to begin H2?

## WHY_THIS_BOUNDARY

This is a composition/readiness claim after all semantic owners exist. It must not share implementation ownership with the preceding WPs or invent behavior to close an omission. The fresh AI-agent trial belongs here because public Unity-bridge use by a new agent is itself newly claimed only at this boundary.

## Inherited guarantees

All accepted H0 work and H1-00 through H1-11. The gate consumes their evidence and re-executes only integration seams needed by the reference scenario.

## New guarantees owned

- complete deterministic H1 reference scenario and parity verdict;
- exhaustive gate-stage/residual/dependency reconciliation;
- one fresh independent public-client AI-agent trial of the new Unity capability;
- explicit H2 authorization decision.

## Explicitly not re-proved

Every H0 suite, all per-WP negative matrices, every Unity asset/component, shipping performance, gameplay or model-vendor quality.

## Allowed scope

Gate orchestration, deterministic scenario/evidence, causal gate-owned omission controls, dependency/residual reconciliation, packaged exact-SHA trial instructions/artifacts and documentation.

## Forbidden scope

New product capabilities/schemas, bridge fixes, gameplay, broader asset content, changing predecessor oracles to fit the gate, or using the AI trial as the deterministic oracle.

## Architecture / authority boundary

Gate evidence begins with canonical discovery/authoring and ends with normalized Unity observation/rebuild. Unity files never become the source used to prove canonical state. Any semantic gap reopens its causal owner.
