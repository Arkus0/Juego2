## Objective

Turn the exact adopted Quaternius Source baseline plus the accepted Juego2 visual direction into a **bounded production-ready environment kit** that is sufficient to realize the first retained CITY-07 chain without the visible failure mode observed in the disposable Astra test: primitive boxes/planes carrying facade, roof or prop assets as decoration while being presented as if they were finished architecture.

This WP exists because the trigger already named by the ART track has occurred: CITY-04 produced the measured/walked retained greybox. H1-04 proved a minimal real-source catalogue slice and intentionally did **not** prove full-pack breadth, Cantabrian adaptation or production-art completeness. ART-01 now owns the missing product/content boundary.

The goal is not to replace useful Quaternius content. The goal is to make a small, traceable, reusable Juego2 kit that can build the retained slice honestly and recognizably as the accepted fictional Potes/Liébana setting.

## Central claim

A fresh environment author — human or AI-assisted — can select from the reviewed ART-01 kit and construct a bounded third-person street/building composition for the retained seed **without needing visible primitive fallback to stand in for missing production architecture**, while every retained asset remains traceable to an approved upstream source or a reviewed Juego2 derivative.

## Required work

### 1. Demand-led kit coverage

Build a coverage matrix from the actual retained CITY-07 representative chain rather than ingesting the whole Quaternius distribution indiscriminately.

The initial planning expectation from the CITY-07 concept work is approximately **40–60 useful pieces**, but this is **not** an acceptance quota. The acceptance oracle is functional coverage. A smaller set may pass if it truly covers the chain; a larger set may be required if composition still falls back to visible proxies.

At minimum disposition the following roles where the retained chain needs them:

- wall/facade modules and end/corner conditions;
- roof modules, eaves, ridge/termination and roof-to-wall meeting conditions;
- doors, windows, shutters and opening frames with believable depth/threshold treatment;
- base/plinth/foundation and building-to-ground transitions;
- columns/posts, beams, porches/arcades and bounded secondary volumes where required;
- retaining walls, garden/edge walls, steps/landings, kerb/road/ground transitions and drainage/readable edge treatment;
- street and small-square surface families;
- signs, lamps, benches, crates/barrels/firewood, laundry/planters and other bounded facade/street props actually required by the slice;
- accepted wet-valley vegetation/rocks/nature sufficient to avoid sphere-tree / dome-hill placeholder presentation;
- any player-facing humanoid/outfit presentation needed for scale inspection, using the accepted character rules.

For each demand row classify the solution as one of:

- `KEEP_AS_IS` — source asset already fits the accepted visual target;
- `ADAPT` — source asset remains the basis but needs bounded material/texture/mesh treatment;
- `CREATE_DERIVED` — a Juego2 derivative or new compatible piece is needed because forcing the source would produce the wrong silhouette/structure;
- `REJECT_FOR_KEEPER` — source shape is usable only as disposable reference/proxy or is visually incompatible;
- `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` — a genuinely new external source is needed and must pass `DEPENDENCY_IP_POLICY` before use.

### 2. Composition metadata, not asset-name inventory only

The kit is not ready merely because assets have catalogue IDs. For retained environment pieces record enough reviewed composition metadata for a human or AI author to know how they can be assembled.

Where relevant, record:

- effective dimensions and scale assumptions;
- architectural role (`wall`, `corner`, `opening`, `roof`, `eave`, `base`, `porch`, `retaining`, `ground`, `prop`, `nature`, etc.);
- compatible family/material variants;
- connection/meeting expectations and required support;
- whether repetition is allowed and along which axis;
- frontage/interior/exterior orientation where meaningful;
- whether the piece is structural, dressing-only or collision/support-only;
- known forbidden combinations that create floating roofs, mirrored signs, impossible openings, duplicate surfaces or obvious visual seams;
- provenance: exact Quaternius source identity or Juego2 derivative lineage.

This metadata may live in the existing accepted catalogue/projection surfaces where they already support the field truthfully, or in an ART-owned reviewed companion manifest if H1 deliberately does not own that semantic. ART-01 must not silently mutate H1 catalogue semantics.

### 3. Cantabrian/Juego2 adaptation pass

Apply the accepted visual bible where the source pack conflicts with the product target. The retained kit must not default to:

- alpine half-timbering or chalet silhouette;
- fantasy/medieval castle language;
- bright terracotta warmth as the town-wide default;
- flat blue-sky/sunny-Mediterranean presentation;
- decorative facade stickers on unarticulated primitive shells.

The retained target is the accepted low-poly Juego2 language: wet/dark tile, stone and restrained render, dark timber where appropriate, damp greens, overcast default, readable late-PS2/early-PS3 shape language and human-scale irregularity.

ART-01 may create bounded derivative meshes/materials/textures where needed. Every derivative must retain traceable upstream/source identity and must receive its own stable catalogue identity rather than silently replacing the purchased upstream item.

### 4. Visible-proxy / fallback policy

Define and enforce three presentation states for environment realization:

- `KEEPER_READY` — composed only from accepted keeper-capable pieces plus ordinary authored Unity geometry that itself meets the accepted visual criteria;
- `PROXY_VISUAL` — deliberate blockout/coverage placeholder, visibly and evidentially marked as non-keeper;
- `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` — the requested composition cannot truthfully be produced from the admitted kit yet.

An authoring agent must **not** convert `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` into apparent success by creating a large box/plane and attaching windows, roofs, signs or textures to it while reporting the result as keeper-ready.

`PROXY_VISUAL` and `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` are valid **truthful diagnostic/iteration states**, but they are not ART-01 readiness success states for any required benchmark element. If a required benchmark need ends in either state, ART-01 remains blocked/not-ready until that need is resolved or the proper causal owner changes the requirement.

Unity-native geometry is not banned. It is acceptable when intentionally authored as retained geometry and it satisfies the same silhouette, depth, junction, ground-contact and material criteria as the rest of the kit. What is forbidden is silent substitution of crude proxy geometry for missing production structure.

### 6. Agent-composition smoke test

Because the current defect was exposed by an AI-assisted Astra build, ART-01 must prove that the kit and its metadata are usable by an author who was not the asset creator.

Use a fresh human/agent session to assemble or materially revise the benchmark from the admitted kit and composition metadata. Astra may be used as evidence, but the contract is tool-agnostic and no hosted author becomes semantic authority.

The effective smoke test is downstream of H2F-GATE and must use the accepted H2F baseline rather than a private or provisional render/toolchain configuration.

The smoke test must distinguish:

- a genuine keeper-ready composition;
- a deliberate proxy result;
- a truthful coverage failure.

Choosing `PROXY_VISUAL` or `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` is acceptable as **honest negative evidence during execution** and is preferable to faking success. It does **not** satisfy the required benchmark. ART-01 PASS requires the frozen representative benchmark, including every required retained need in that benchmark, to end `KEEPER_READY`.

A tool silently returning a proxy-looking scene as keeper-ready is a FAIL.

## Visual acceptance oracle

The effective Unity benchmark receives a human third-person inspection plus retained captures. The inspection is product evidence, not a pixel-perfect image oracle.

At minimum the reviewer checks:

1. **Silhouette / massing** — important buildings do not read as one unarticulated rectangular prism with a roof asset placed on top when the intended grammar requires richer structure.
2. **Facade depth** — openings have believable wall/threshold depth or an explicitly accepted stylized equivalent; windows/doors/signs do not read as stickers on a flat plane.
3. **Roof integration** — roof, eaves and wall mass meet coherently; no obvious floating/disconnected cap.
4. **Ground contact** — buildings and streets have a deliberate base/threshold/edge transition rather than emerging from one undifferentiated tiled plane.
5. **Composition** — corners, setbacks, local volumes, retaining/steps and prop placement create a readable authored place rather than an asset showroom.
6. **Visual-bible fit** — the retained result does not default to the vetoed alpine/fantasy/sunny-Mediterranean read.
7. **Human scale** — door, street, facade and public-space proportions still read correctly with a human-sized proxy in third person.
8. **No silent proxy** — any primitive-looking placeholder observed during iteration is explicitly marked `PROXY_VISUAL` and excluded from the keeper claim; the frozen required benchmark cannot PASS while a required element remains in that state.

A supplementary untextured/neutral-material inspection may be used to judge massing and facade depth. It is diagnostic, not a requirement to ship an alternate material setup.

## Negative gates

- ART-01 freezes or passes its effective Unity benchmark before `WP-H2F-GATE` PASS, or relies on a private/provisional render/toolchain baseline instead of the accepted H2F baseline;
- a mandatory benchmark need remains `PROXY_VISUAL` or `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` at the frozen candidate, even when that state is reported honestly;
- a visible keeper building is fundamentally a primitive cuboid/plane with facade/roof/opening assets attached as decoration because the kit lacked the necessary structural pieces;
- a missing corner, opening, roof junction, threshold or ground-contact solution is hidden by texture/material polish instead of being covered or declared blocked;
- alpine half-timber/chalet or fantasy language survives as the default keeper read merely because it came from Quaternius;
- an AI/human author silently substitutes a proxy but reports `KEEPER_READY`;
- source/derivative provenance becomes ambiguous;
- a new third-party pack is used without exact adoption review;
- ART-01 changes CITY topology/programme/access semantics;
- the binding paintover/assembly/dimensional contracts are skipped;
- or the benchmark is judged only from a top-down/editor view without human-scale inspection.

## Definition of Done

If the author can only return `PROXY_VISUAL` or `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` for a mandatory benchmark need, that is valuable truthful evidence but the WP is **not done** and CITY-07 remains blocked.
