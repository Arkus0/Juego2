## Post-H2F visual production

The accepted visual-production expansion adds reusable environment, character/wardrobe, animation and dialogue/UI production lanes before the strengthened H2-GATE. `WP-ART-ENV-02`, `WP-ART-CHAR-01/02`, `WP-ART-ANIM-01/02`, `WP-ART-URBAN-01` and `WP-ART-03` consume the current product direction rather than the historical inland setting as final authority.

ART may iterate rapidly inside accepted authority. Independent review is not required for every local art edit. Use meaningful target/structural/final checkpoints and revalidate only affected structural/semantic conclusions.

ART cannot hide missing required coverage behind props/materials, weaken CITY access/topology, invent bridge semantics, or justify new paid/external acquisition solely because admitted source content was filtered by an obsolete per-asset realism interpretation.

The strengthened H2-GATE consumes the post-H2F visual production chain and final port-town keeper proof.
