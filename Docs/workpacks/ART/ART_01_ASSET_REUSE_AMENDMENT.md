## Why this exists

`WP-ART-01` began under an older final-setting interpretation centered on fictional Potes/Liébana and contains rejection language that can be read too strictly at the individual-asset level. Current product authority is a large fictional northern-Spain port town, and the owner has explicitly decided to prefer broad source reuse over literal geographic realism per mesh.

This amendment prevents already-owned useful material — especially timber-heavy or medieval/fantasy-pack source — from being discarded merely because its untouched prefab is not directly region-authentic.

## Supersession inside WP-ART-01

Where `WP-ART-01` or its existing evidence says or implies:

- the final result must read specifically as fictional Potes/Liébana;
- alpine/half-timber/medieval source must be rejected at source-asset level;
- source selection should prefer direct geographic authenticity over reusable/adaptable utility;

interpret it instead through the current `VISUAL_BIBLE.md`, `SETTING.md` and `ASSET_REUSE_VISUAL_DIRECTION_AMENDMENT.md`.

The historical inland benchmark route and its structural evidence remain valid test content; they no longer define the final-product setting.

## Triage extension

Keep the existing dispositions and add one:

- `KEEP_AS_IS` → `DIRECT / A`;
- `ADAPT` → `ADAPTABLE / B`;
- **`DONOR_COMPONENTS` → `DONOR / C`**;
- `REJECT_FOR_KEEPER` → `REJECT / D`;
- `CREATE_DERIVED` remains valid;
- `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` remains valid.

`DONOR_COMPONENTS` means the source prefab as a whole is not keeper-ready but named subparts are useful in a Juego2 derivative/assembly. Record source lineage and the retained components.

## Required recovery pass before final candidate

ART-01 Phase A work and the existing `PREFOUNDATION_INPUT` are retained. Do **not** restart the workpack.

After H2F-GATE, before freezing the effective ART-01 candidate:

1. collect a representative sample of previous rejects/deprioritizations caused mainly by visible timber, medieval/fantasy pack origin, weak direct Cantabrian specificity or correctable material/palette mismatch;
2. record the old reason;
3. re-evaluate each as `KEEP_AS_IS`, `ADAPT`, `DONOR_COMPONENTS`, `CREATE_DERIVED` or `REJECT_FOR_KEEPER`;
4. demonstrate at least one recovered environment asset/family from source through bounded adaptation/component reuse into a third-person composition;
5. demonstrate explicitly that timber percentage itself is not an automatic rejection rule;
6. route useful wardrobe recovery findings to `WP-ART-CHAR-01` where they belong;
7. only retain `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` for roles still materially uncovered after this pass.

No fixed recovery ratio is required. The evidence must be sufficient to show that the previous filtering interpretation no longer destroys useful source breadth.

## Wood rule

Timber is an admitted material. It may be used in mixed or timber-forward assemblies when the final composed result belongs to Juego2.

ART-01 must reject or adapt **genre signals**, not wood as such. For example, a source building may lose decorative half-timber motifs, steep chalet proportions or fantasy signage while keeping beams, balconies, windows, roof elements or other useful geometry.

## Adaptation-cost oracle

Do not preserve a bad asset merely to maximize reuse.

A source item may still be `REJECT_FOR_KEEPER` when:

- its core silhouette/function remains incompatible;
- removing genre-specific signals would amount to rebuilding most of it;
- its required modification cost is clearly worse than a cleaner admitted alternative or reusable derivative;
- it causes an incoherent final scene despite bounded adaptation.

The rejection reason must be causal and specific. “Too much wood” or “comes from Medieval Village/Fantasy” is not sufficient by itself.

## Final ART-01 acceptance addition

In addition to all existing structural/provenance/H2F/proxy requirements, ART-01 cannot PASS unless:

- the recovery pass above is present;
- `DONOR_COMPONENTS` is available and used where materially useful;
- the effective third-person result is judged against the **current northern-Spain port-town visual direction**, not the retired Potes final-setting anchor;
- no required external purchase/gap is justified solely by an asset category that the recovery pass shows can be reused/adapted;
- final scene coherence remains strong enough that the result does not read as fantasy/alpine/medieval kitbash or dressed greybox.

## Non-changes

This amendment does not weaken assembly grammar, dimensional fit, facade depth, roof/base/ground joins, provenance, `KEEPER_READY` truthfulness, H2F sequencing or fresh-author evidence. It widens lawful source reuse while preserving the quality bar of the composed result.
