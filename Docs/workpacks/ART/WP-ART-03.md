## Claim

The final Juego2 visual language is approved on the **actual first keeper block of the large port town**, and the environment, character, animation and UI production lanes are sufficiently industrialized that future content creation is primarily content authoring rather than repeated pipeline/tooling invention.

## Integrated benchmark

Run the final benchmark on `WP-CITY-URBAN-01`, not only on the historical inland pilot.

The scene must include enough real third-person use to judge together:

- port-town architecture/street identity;
- a public threshold/interior;
- keeper-ready ground/building/street joins;
- props/signage/vegetation/material/lighting language;
- multiple factory-produced characters with visibly distinct wardrobe reads;
- locomotion plus representative conversational/ambient/object animation vocabulary;
- the approved no-voice dialogue/UI presentation pattern from `WP-ART-UI-01`, regardless of whether its accepted runtime surface is Dialogue 2 or Core/local;
- the H2F-admitted player/camera at real play scale.

Temporary dialogue content is allowed. Deep systemic gameplay is not required.

## Production challenge

After the integrated scene is visually approved, a **fresh capable author/agent** receives a bounded new production brief that is not a direct copy of the accepted benchmark.

The challenge must require:

- one additional micro-environment composition or meaningful variant using the environment factory;
- a small group of new presentable ordinary inhabitants using the character factory;
- reuse of admitted animation vocabulary;
- the approved UI/dialogue presentation pattern if the brief includes conversation;
- no new bespoke framework/tool architecture merely to complete ordinary content production.

Record first-use versus repeated-use operations for the production lanes used by the challenge. The challenge need not produce a shipping district, but it must be large enough to expose whether the supposed factories actually reduce repeated setup work.

## Look lock

Owner visual approval here locks the **fundamental** production direction:

- silhouette/massing language;
- material/palette/atmosphere family;
- human/environment scale relationship;
- character wardrobe/population read;
- ordinary animation/acting language;
- dialogue/UI presentation language;
- scenario assembly grammar.

Later work may add breadth, hero polish, new assets and special-case animation, but it should not casually reopen these foundations. A material change requires an explicit reviewed visual-direction amendment.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

**Mandatory positive evidence:**

1. continuous third-person capture on the actual port-town keeper block;
2. owner visual approval of the integrated environment + population + animation + UI presentation;
3. proof that required environment roles are `KEEPER_READY`;
4. character-factory group and animation-vocabulary evidence in the actual block;
5. approved no-voice dialogue/UI presentation in context;
6. fresh-author production challenge and outputs;
7. separately observable first-use versus repeated-use effort/operation evidence with counting assumptions and a real reuse reduction;
8. residual ledger separating future breadth/polish from foundation-blocking gaps.

**FAIL if:** the final port-town scene still reads as dressed greybox; the inland pilot is used as a substitute for final-setting approval; characters are mostly raw/clone source models; ordinary acting requires new bespoke animation plumbing; dialogue/UI uses an untouched stock/default presentation as the final visual result; a fresh author cannot produce another bounded scene/group without implementation archaeology or new framework code; or a fundamental visual/pipeline uncertainty is mislabeled as future polish.

## Allowed residuals

- additional neighbourhood-specific architecture;
- hero/protagonist bespoke clothing/facial detail;
- rare occupations and special animation beats;
- combat/chase animation sets;
- Inventory/Quest/combat UI;
- final town-wide content breadth;
- later performance polish that does not change the visual identity.

## Non-claims

No persistent Living World, finished story, combat, chase, Inventory, Behavior, Perception or full 20–30 minute slice claim. This WP locks **how Juego2 is made and looks**, not all of what the player can do.
