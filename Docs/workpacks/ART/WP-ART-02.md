## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

- **Positive claim:** the retained Puente -> Casco -> Bar keeper slice, with representative H2 occupancy, visibly expresses the approved Juego2 visual language at human third-person scale.
- **Mandatory positive evidence:** (1) retained approved target references/paintovers or equivalent owner-approved visual baseline; (2) third-person traversal captures of the representative exterior, threshold and public interior under representative occupancy; (3) direct owner visual-approval record for the final candidate; (4) evidence covering both quiet and busier occupancy/readability conditions; (5) a visual defect/residual ledger that distinguishes blocking target failures from accepted breadth/polish residuals; (6) confirmation that required benchmark roles remain `KEEPER_READY`, not proxy/blocked states disguised by dressing.
- **Negative gates:** FAIL if approval relies only on a hero screenshot/top-down/editor view; if `PROXY_VISUAL` or `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` is presented as closure; if props/vegetation/material treatment hide unresolved primitive host geometry; if raw/generic asset-pack identity materially breaks the approved target; or if deterministic structural tests are substituted for required human visual judgement.
- **Non-claims:** no H2 world-authoring capability claim, Living World behaviour, final town-wide asset breadth or shipping-performance claim.
- **Allowed residuals:** breadth outside the representative chain, minor polish, additional asset variety, final lighting/material refinement and non-blocking animation/detail improvements. Residuals may not leave the fundamental visual language undecided, a required connection visually broken, or a required benchmark role in proxy/blocked state.
- **Evaluation method:** owner human visual approval plus third-person/play inspection is mandatory; deterministic/structured checks are used only for affected structural, provenance and readiness facts that are machine-verifiable. Neither evidence class substitutes for the other.
- **Consumed predecessors:** `WP-ART-01` supplies the keeper-ready assembly vocabulary/readiness truth and approved target direction; `WP-CITY-07` supplies retained keeper spatial structure; `WP-H2-02` supplies the third-person playable shell; `WP-H2-03` supplies representative character occupancy/presentation.
