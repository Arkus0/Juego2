## Claim and work

Extend the accepted Quaternius/Juego2 assembly grammar and the now-proved scenario-production factory to the **actual first keeper block** of the fictional large port town / villa portuaria. Resolve demand-led waterfront/street facades, commercial threshold, ground/material treatment, signage, props, wardrobe-context needs and lighting so the first keeper neighbourhood can be assembled without hiding primitive boxes.

The final town proper name is unresolved. `Villa Bruma` is non-canonical going forward, so permanent signage, UI-facing environmental text and reusable named assets must remain placeholder-safe unless a later reviewed naming decision exists.

The visual read must support a substantial northern-Spain port town and comarca hub rather than an anonymous major city. Keep exact asset provenance and ART-01 `KEEPER_READY` vs `PROXY_VISUAL` distinction. Do not finish every neighbourhood's art before this WP can pass. The accepted nightlife distribution—Casco primary, Talleres secondary/alternative, Mercado ordinary evening life, Muelle night work, Viviendas low intensity—is a later-town visual constraint, not a requirement to expand the first Mercado–Muelle block into those future districts here.

The port-town identity is judged on the **composed third-person result**, not on literal regional authenticity of every upstream mesh. `DIRECT`, `ADAPTABLE`, `DONOR`/component reuse and reviewed Juego2 derivatives are all valid. Timber-heavy and medieval/fantasy-pack source may be reused when bounded adaptation and context remove an incompatible genre read; wood or pack origin alone is not a rejection reason.

## Industrialization requirement

This WP is no longer only a one-scene port adaptation. It must consume `ART-ENV-02` production profiles/rules rather than introducing a second port-only assembly pipeline. New waterfront/commercial pieces that are genuinely required become reviewed extensions of the same factory, with dimensions, connection rules, provenance and reuse disposition.

At least one port-specific addition must demonstrate that it can be reused in a second bounded composition/variant without new scene-specific framework code.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

**Mandatory evidence:**

- first-block demand/coverage matrix;
- source/derived asset identities and reuse dispositions;
- dimension/connection and scenario-factory integration rules;
- representative third-person exterior-to-interior composition under admitted URP;
- owner visual verdict on the port-town read at scene level;
- one bounded reuse/variant of a port-specific family;
- confirmation that final-town proper-name presentation remains placeholder-safe;
- explicit gaps and lawful acquisition/production plan, with external gaps justified only after reuse/adapt/donor paths are considered.

**FAIL if:** port-town identity is asserted from the inland pilot alone; required first-block roles remain proxy yet marked keeper; assets are used without provenance; scenery hides structural gaps; the art drifts toward a metropolitan scale inconsistent with the product amendment; permanent art/signage hard-codes `Villa Bruma` or another unreviewed final town name; the first-block scope is inflated merely to implement future Casco/Talleres nightlife; port work bypasses the accepted scenario factory and becomes a bespoke second pipeline; or useful source breadth is discarded merely because of visible wood or medieval/fantasy pack origin without the accepted adaptation/donor analysis.

**Allowed residuals:** additional neighbourhoods, distant background variants, rare waterfront/industrial hero assets, future Casco/Talleres nightlife realization and later polish outside the first block. First-block required kit gaps are blockers.
