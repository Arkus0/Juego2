# WP-ART-CHAR-02 — Repeatable character factory + population batch

Status: **PROPOSED / NOT_STARTED**
Class: PRODUCT ART / PRODUCTION INDUSTRIALIZATION
Depends on: `WP-ART-CHAR-01` PASS + accepted H2F foundation
Blocks: `WP-H2-03`, `WP-ART-ANIM-02`, `WP-ART-03`
Binding decision: `Docs/product/VISUAL_PRODUCTION_FACTORY_AMENDMENT.md`

## Claim

A fresh author can create ordinary Juego2 population characters from the accepted character/wardrobe grammar quickly and consistently, producing visibly varied, correctly rigged presentations without bespoke per-character pipeline work.

## Required work

Build the smallest reusable production surface that makes the `ART-CHAR-01` recipe operational. It may be editor tooling, deterministic scripts, prefab recipes, DCC helpers or a deliberately simple combination, but it must not require a new custom tool for each NPC.

Produce a representative **factory batch** large enough to expose clone, clipping, palette and silhouette problems. Planning target: roughly 12–20 ordinary population presentations, but PASS is based on useful variation and repeatability rather than hitting a number while producing near-identical clones.

The batch should exercise multiple admitted wardrobe/role families and palette combinations and should contain enough characters to judge a busy street rather than only isolated hero shots.

## Required factory properties

- stable source/derived provenance;
- deterministic or documented variant selection where automated;
- Humanoid/scale validation;
- reusable material/palette handling;
- clipping/intersection checks or explicit visual inspection gates;
- output prefabs/variants consumable by H2/GC2 presentation;
- no GC2 runtime GUID, prefab GUID or wardrobe index becomes Arkus canonical person identity;
- regeneration/update does not silently destroy hand-authored hero overrides.

## Fresh-author proof

A fresh capable author/agent receives a short population brief and must create a small group of new presentable characters using the published factory. The proof must distinguish:

- initial factory setup cost;
- repeated character-creation operations;
- any manual visual fixes;
- gaps that genuinely require new art.

The repeated batch must show an observable reduction in setup/assembly work versus the first legal character assembly under stated counting assumptions. No universal seconds-per-character threshold is imposed.

## PASS-before-work acceptance contract

**Mandatory evidence:**

1. representative batch captures at third-person distance and closer inspection;
2. variation ledger covering wardrobe family, silhouette/read, palette and accessories actually exercised;
3. factory recipe/tool transcript for at least one first assembly and repeated variants;
4. fresh-author group-production proof;
5. invalid/blocked combination handling;
6. a residual ledger for hero-specific work and missing future wardrobe breadth.

**FAIL if:** variation is mostly recolours of one outfit; ordinary characters still require bespoke Blender/Unity hand repair as the normal path; raw/unclothed characters remain an acceptable population output; factory regeneration loses provenance or destroys reviewed outputs; or the batch looks like an asset-pack clone army despite meeting a numerical count.

## Non-claims

No 80–120-person population completion, narrative importance, schedules, memory, facial-performance system or final hero-character quality claim.
